# Deekshant Sharma

## Relationship and shared work

Somil’s Beyond Pixels materials address Deekshant as a sales partner for the Gym OS offering and provide him with a self-onboarding package for prospective partners. The package describes Deekshant’s role as introducing partners to the gym-management product while Somil supplies gym leads or data and approves portal access after an agreement is signed.

The partner materials specify a portal for registering leads, tracking a pipeline, and submitting daily reports. They propose a minimum of 10 calls per day and a commission of 10% on a partner’s first two deals, then 15% from the third deal onward; these are written program terms, not confirmation of his current participation or completed deals.

Gym-focused websites, dashboards, and member-portal concepts recur in the same business record. For the broader business context, see [Beyond Pixels](../../beyond-pixels/main.md) and the [Gym OS project](../../gym-os/main.md).
