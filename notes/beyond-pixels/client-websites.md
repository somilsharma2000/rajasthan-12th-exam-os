# Client Website Previews

[← Beyond Pixels](main.md)

## Approach

Somil repeatedly prepared tailored website concepts as a way to demonstrate a concrete direction to prospective local-business clients rather than sending only a generic pitch. The concepts were based on information described as publicly available or researched for the business, and outreach commonly offered a no-obligation preview followed by an invitation to discuss adapting or launching it. The source material shows proposals and preview builds, not confirmed acceptance by the named businesses; keep that distinction clear in any portfolio or sales claims.

Common elements included mobile-responsive layouts, service and pricing sections, clear contact or booking calls to action, social proof, FAQs, maps or business-hours details, and search metadata. Gym concepts often extended beyond a public website into an owner dashboard and member portal, aligning with the broader [Gym OS](../gym-os/main.md) product direction.

## Selected examples in the work record

- **Manchester gyms:** Concepts and systems were prepared for Fight Factory, Flex N Tone, REDS Gym, Betta Bodies, and Delaneys Gym. The materials describe different visual identities and gym-specific information, including boxing, bodybuilding, schedules, coaches, membership options, reviews, equipment, and gym-management features. Some packages were built as interactive HTML previews; the evidence does not establish that those gyms commissioned or deployed them.
- **Dental practices:** Outreach and preview concepts covered practices in the US and UK, including responsive layouts, service presentation, appointment calls to action, and patient-facing features. These were prospecting concepts, not confirmed clinic implementations.
- **Other local businesses:** A preview was prepared for The Bella Of Massage Beauty Spa. The broader pattern was to tailor the concept to a business’s existing public identity and invite feedback.

## Reusable delivery lessons

The strongest examples emphasize using business-specific details and a distinctive visual direction, while keeping conversion paths easy to find on mobile. Interactive features should be described as demonstrations unless connected to verified production systems; sample dashboard figures, simulated bookings, or proposed automation are not real client results. For deployed gym-platform features and reported deployment history, see [Gym OS](../gym-os/main.md).
