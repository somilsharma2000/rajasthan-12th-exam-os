# Beyond Pixels

Beyond Pixels is the business context behind Somil’s gym-management product work and bespoke website concepts for prospective clients. Its recurring offer is more than a brochure site: it combines a public-facing website with operational tools such as member or customer management, lead tracking, analytics, and automation. The related [Gym OS project](../gym-os/main.md) is the clearest product example; [client website work](client-websites.md) records the separate preview-and-outreach workstream.

## Business and delivery model

The materials describe a small, hands-on digital-services operation that builds tailored websites and software previews for gyms and other local businesses. Examples span gym websites and management dashboards, dental-practice website concepts, and a beauty-spa redesign. Many examples were previews or sales materials, so they should not be treated as proof that a prospect became a client or that a preview was deployed for that business.

A partner package prepared for [Deekshant Sharma](../people/deekshant-sharma/main.md) set out a self-onboarding sales-partner model for Gym OS. Somil’s stated role was to provide gym leads or data and approve portal access after the partner agreement; partners would register leads, manage their pipeline, and submit reports through a portal. The package proposed a minimum of 10 calls a day, daily reporting by 10 PM, and a commission of 10% on a partner’s first two deals, then 15% from the third deal onward. Those are package terms in authored material, not confirmation of current enrollment or completed sales.
