# Bloomwire

Bloomwire is Somil’s Jaipur-made handcrafted pipe-cleaner flower brand and e-commerce project. Its public-facing copy describes long-lasting, hand-twisted flowers and gift products; the project’s website and commerce features are distinct from the client-services work at [Beyond Pixels](../beyond-pixels/main.md).

## Site and product positioning

A 2026 launch-readiness email reported the site live at [somilsharma2000.github.io/bloomwire](https://somilsharma2000.github.io/bloomwire/). The project materials describe product browsing, bundles, cart and checkout flows, delivery estimates, newsletter capture, customer rewards, referrals, and promotional offers. They also refer to the brand site `bloomwire.in`; the supplied evidence does not establish its present availability or relationship to the reported GitHub Pages deployment.

The emails describe flowers as handcrafted in Jaipur and designed to last, with free shipping above ₹499 in promotional copy. An August 7, 2026 launch report listed a tiered cart-reward concept, a mobile purchase bar, an estimated four-day delivery display, and a newsletter discount offer. Promotional values and shipping promises should be checked against current store configuration before reuse.

## Reported implementation and fixes

A launch-readiness report dated August 7 described 17 bug fixes and six launch features. The fixes included bundle-price calculations, direct checkout behavior for “Buy Now,” cart quantity handling, dynamic category counts, mobile layout corrections, and consistent delivery-tier keys. The feature list included product-card add-to-cart actions, order-threshold rewards, a trust section, and email capture.

An August 8 update reported a CORS preflight fix, purchase-verification gating for refer-a-friend features, removal of demo toggles from the Creators page, mobile floating-button adjustments, and a rewritten Google Sheets sync handled by a deployed backend function. It reported a successful build and GitHub Pages deployment at that time.

## Measurement snapshot

A Google Analytics email covering August 22–September 18, 2026, reported 2 active users, 1 new user, 4 seconds average engagement time, and 67 events, with large declines versus the prior period. It also showed 24 page views for the home page and a 93.33% bounce rate for that page. This is a single short reporting window, not a complete assessment of current performance, but it does indicate that acquisition and engagement merit investigation before treating the launch as validated.
