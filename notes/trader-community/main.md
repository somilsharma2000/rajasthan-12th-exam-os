# Trader Community Partnership Concept

Somil drafted a concept for a trader community intended to retain members through daily activities, education, competitions, leaderboards, and a community reward currency. The source material repeatedly characterizes the platform as **pre-launch** and contains partnership pitches, forecasts, and proposed commercial terms—not evidence of a live community, realized trading volume, signed broker partnership, or achieved member targets.

## Proposed model

The concept combines trader education and community activity with brokerage or prop-firm partnerships. Draft proposals describe revenue from introducing traders or referring evaluation purchases, with a portion of commission returned to participants as cashback or rewards. Other proposed partner benefits include community discount codes, sponsored competitions, a partner manager, shared tracking, and co-branded onboarding.

The proposal’s target of 10,000 active traders within 12 months and volume or conversion estimates are planning assumptions only. Different outreach drafts gave different acquisition estimates and mechanisms, so none should be treated as a settled forecast. The materials also describe multiple potential counterparties—among them Fusion Markets, Pepperstone, FTMO, FundedNext, FundingPips, and others—but do not establish that any partnership was finalized.

## Commercial discussions recorded

A Somil-authored August 2026 response to Pepperstone said that the quoted L1 rate of $1 per lot and L3 rate of $3 per lot would not support the proposed reward economics; it requested custom terms in the $5–7-per-lot range. A separate Fusion Markets email described rates of $0.68 per lot on a Zero account and $4 per lot on a Classic account as a starting point, and proposed negotiating higher tiered rebates and revenue share. These are historical statements in correspondence, not verified current offers or accepted agreements.

An Exness correspondence thread contains an earlier statement that Somil did not have an active account, followed by a later correction saying that he did. The later statement should take precedence, but the supplied material does not establish current affiliate approval, active referral status, or the terms available now.

## Risks and due diligence

This concept involves financial promotions, trading incentives, cross-border referrals, rewards, and in some drafts participation based on deposits and trading activity. Before any launch, verify each provider’s current eligibility rules and written terms, and obtain appropriate legal and compliance review for target jurisdictions, financial-promotion restrictions, contest rules, tax treatment, and reward mechanics. Do not present trading volume, returns, deposits, funded-account numbers, member counts, or partnership terms as guaranteed or achieved. Incentives should not encourage participants to trade more than they can afford to lose.

## Open questions

- Has a community product or public presence launched since these pre-launch pitches?
- Did any broker or prop-firm partner accept a proposal or issue current written terms?
- What lawful, sustainable reward design would avoid incentivizing excessive trading?
- Which jurisdictions and products can be served, and what disclosures or approvals are required?
