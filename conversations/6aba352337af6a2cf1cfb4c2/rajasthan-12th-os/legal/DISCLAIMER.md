## 2. SOURCES OF PAST YEAR QUESTIONS (PYQs) & CONTENT PROVENANCE

### 2.1 Origin of Exam Questions
Questions included in our practice tests and Past Year Question (PYQ) archives originate from:
* **Memory-Based Candidate Transcriptions:** Questions recalled and transcribed by candidates immediately following public examination sessions.
* **Public Domain Archives & Educational Portals:** Publicly circulated question collections available on educational archives (such as `sscportal.in`, public university libraries, and community test repositories).
* **Expert-Authored / Derived Questions:** Original practice questions authored by subject matter experts to align with official syllabi (e.g., NCERT/RBSE 10th and 12th level textbooks).

### 2.2 Question Provenance Transparency
To ensure total transparency regarding question origin, accuracy, and drafting methodology, every question in our database displays an explicit **Provenance Tag** within the application interface:

| Provenance Marker | Meaning and Source Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `official-paper` | Directly transcribed from publicly published government master question papers and final answer keys released by RSSB/RSMSSB. |
| `memory-transcription` | Reconstructed from memory-based candidate responses and educational archive transcriptions (e.g., public archives like sscportal.in). Minor language variations may exist from original paper slips. |
| `agent-authored` | Originally written by expert subject educators and verified by automated/peer review systems to test specific syllabus topics. |
| `verified-derived` | Derived from standard textbook facts (RBSE / NCERT) or legal/statutory references (e.g., Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act) and verified across multiple standard reference guides. |

## 3. ACCURACY POLICY AND ERRORS REDRESSAL

### 3.1 Disclaimer of Guaranteed Accuracy
While we employ rigorous editorial review, multi-stage automated validation, and expert verification pipelines:
* Questions, option choices, and explanations are provided for educational study purposes only.
* Official RSSB answer keys may occasionally differ due to board revisions, question deletions, or disputed representations.
* The Company makes no warranty that every question or explanation is 100% error-free or that answer keys reflect official board final master keys in every instance.

### 3.2 Error Reporting & Correction Mechanism
We welcome candidate feedback and maintain a dedicated error redressal process:
1. **Reporting Errors:** If you spot a factual error, typo, or disputed answer key in any question, you can report it directly via the in-app feedback link or by emailing `content@rajasthan12th-os.in`.
2. **Review Process:** Reported questions are immediately quarantined and re-evaluated against standard reference texts (e.g., Hindi Granth Academy textbooks, official RSSB final answer keys).
3. **Correction & Update:** Valid corrections are updated across the live application within 48 hours.
