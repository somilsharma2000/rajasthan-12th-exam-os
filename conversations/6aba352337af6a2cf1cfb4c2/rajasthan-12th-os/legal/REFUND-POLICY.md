## 1. OVERVIEW AND FOUNDER RECOMMENDATION

This Refund Policy governs subscriptions purchased for **Rajasthan 12th OS** (priced at ₹99 or other promotional/tier prices). 

**Founder Recommendation Notice:**  
Unlike physical goods, digital exam-prep subscriptions offer immediate access to complete question banks, mock tests, and explanations upon payment confirmation. Indian consumer protection framework allows digital service providers to define fair refund conditions. 

To foster consumer trust, prevent payment gateway disputes/chargebacks, and align with best practices under Indian e-commerce guidelines, we recommend adopting a **7-Day No-Questions-Asked Guarantee Model** with fair-use safeguards.

---

## 2. RECOMMENDED 7-DAY NO-QUESTIONS-ASKED REFUND MODEL

### 2.1 Refund Eligibility Window
If you are dissatisfied with your subscription purchase for any reason, you may request a **100% full refund** within **seven (7) calendar days** from the date and time of subscription purchase.

### 2.2 Conditions for 7-Day Refund
To qualify for a full refund under the 7-day policy:
1. **Timeframe:** The refund request must be received by our support team at `support@rajasthan12th-os.in` within 168 hours (7 days) of transaction confirmation.
2. **Fair-Use Usage Limit:** The account must not exhibit automated scraping, bulk downloading, or systematic extraction of question banks prior to the refund request (e.g., attempting more than 50% of total bank questions within 24 hours of purchase to harvest content).
3. **First-Time Purchase:** The refund policy applies to a user's initial subscription purchase. Repeated purchase-and-refund attempts by the same user or payment instrument are not eligible.

## 3. REFUND PROCEDURE AND TIMELINES

### 3.1 How to Submit a Refund Request
To initiate a refund, please send an email to `support@rajasthan12th-os.in` with the subject line: **"Refund Request - [Order / Transaction ID]"**.

Please include the following details in your email:
* Account email address / mobile number used during registration.
* Payment Order ID / Payment Gateway Reference Number (from Razorpay/Stripe confirmation receipt).
* Date of transaction.
* Optional feedback (helps us improve our product, though not mandatory for approval).

### 3.2 Processing and Payout Timeline
* **Acknowledgement:** Our team will acknowledge receipt of your request within 24 hours.
* **Approval & Verification:** Eligible requests will be verified and approved within 2 business days.
* **Payout Method:** Approved refunds will be credited directly back to the **original payment method** used during purchase (e.g., original UPI ID, credit card, debit card, or net banking account).
* **Credit Settlement:** Depending on your bank or card issuer, the refunded amount typically reflects in your statement within **5 to 7 business days** following processing.

## 4. NON-REFUNDABLE SCENARIOS (AFTER 7 DAYS)

Refund requests will be denied under the following circumstances:
1. **Requests After 7 Days:** Any refund request submitted after the expiration of the 7-day guarantee window.
2. **Account Suspension for Violation:** If your account was suspended or terminated due to a violation of our [Terms of Service](./TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md) (such as web scraping, automated extraction, or unauthorized account sharing).
3. **Third-Party Charges:** Any third-party transaction fees, currency conversion surcharges, or internet banking charges levied independently by your financial institution are non-refundable.

## 5. INDIAN CONSUMER LAW CONTEXT & LEGAL NUANCE

### 5.1 Consumer Protection Act, 2019 & E-Commerce Rules, 2020
Under the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** framed under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019:
* Digital platform operators are required to explicitly disclose refund, return, and exchange terms prior to transaction completion.
* E-commerce entities must provide a clear mechanism for handling consumer grievances and process legitimate refunds within reasonable statutory periods.

### 5.2 Digital Goods Exception
Under Indian law, digital content that is immediately delivered, rendered, and consumable upon payment is generally exempt from mandatory statutory return rights once access is fully granted, provided the consumer was informed prior to purchase. However, maintaining a transparent 7-day refund guarantee mitigates potential complaints before the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (Consumer Forum) and satisfies payment gateway merchant underwriting criteria.
