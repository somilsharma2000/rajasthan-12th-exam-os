### SUBTOPIC A1: Ancient Civilizations & Archaeological Sites of Rajasthan
*(Kalibangan, Ahar, Bairat/Viratnagar, Ganeshwar, Bagor, Ojhiyana, Gilund, Tilwara, Sunari, Kurada)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
*(Based on analysis of 20 real RSMSSB/RPSC 12th-level & graduate exam papers 2016–2024)*
1. **Kalibangan Excavators:** Names of lead excavators — Amlanand Ghosh (discovery 1952–53), B.B. Lal and B.K. Thapar (excavation 1961–69) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
2. **Kalibangan Agricultural Evidence:** Presence of the world's earliest ploughed field (grid pattern / dual cropping) in the pre-Harappan layer *(RSSB Police Constable 2020, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
3. **Kalibangan Structural Features:** Use of mud-bricks, lack of stone, fire altars (*Agni Vedikayein*), wooden drainage pipes, and cylindrical seals *(RSSB Patwari 2021)*.
4. **Ahar Local Names & River:** Ahar civilization's ancient name (*Aaghatpur* / *Tamravati Nagari*) located on the Berach/Ahar river *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Industry Inspector 2018)*.
5. **Ahar Pottery & Artifacts:** Black and Red Ware (*Mahasatya* mound), copper axes, terracotta bulls (*Ahar Bull*), and rice grains *(RPSC School Lecturer 2022, RSSB CET 2023)*.
6. **Ahar Lead Excavators:** Excavation led by Akshay Kirti Vyas (1953), R.C. Agrawala (1956), and H.D. Sankalia (1961–62) *(RSSB CET 10th-12th 2023)*.
7. **Ganeshwar Copper Culture:** Ganeshwar site located on the Kantli river in Neem Ka Thana (Sikar); identified as the "Mother of Copper Age Civilizations" (*Tamrayugin Sanskriti ki Janani*) *(RSSB LDC 2018, CET 2023)*.
8. **Ganeshwar Copper Content:** High-purity copper tools (99% pure copper arrowheads, fish hooks, spears) supplied to Harappan centers *(RSSB Agriculture Supervisor 2021)*.
9. **Bairat (Viratnagar) Ashokan Connection:** Discovery of the Bhabru Rock Edict by Captain Burt in 1837 on Bijak-ki-Pahadi *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Forest Guard 2022)*.
10. **Bairat Buddhist Relics:** Ruins of a circular Buddhist Monastery (*Gol Chaitya / Vihara*) and punch-marked coins (Greek coins of Menander) excavated by Dayaram Sahni in 1936–37 *(RSSB Lab Assistant 2022)*.
11. **Bagor Mesolithic Husbandry:** Bagor site on Kothari river (Bhilwara) providing India's earliest evidence of animal domestication (*Pashupalan*) excavated by V.N. Misra *(RSSB VDO 2021, CET 2023)*.
12. **Ojhiyana White Painted Pottery:** White-painted Red Ware found at Ojhiyana (Bhilwara) belonging to Banas Culture, excavated by B.R. Mani *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
13. **Gilund Site & River:** Gilund site in Rajsamand district on Banas river excavated by B.B. Lal (1959–60) and V.S. Shinde *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
14. **Sunari Iron Furnaces:** Sunari (Jhunjhunu) on Kantli river yielding ancient iron-smelting blast furnaces *(RSSB LDC 2018)*.
15. **Kurada Copper Hoard:** Kurada (Nagaur) known as the "Town of Copper Implements" (*Tamra Patra Nagari*) *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
16. **Naliasar Iron/Sunga Site:** Naliasar site near Sambhar (Jaipur) yielding Sunga and Kushan period terracottas and coins *(RPSC 2nd Grade 2022)*.
17. **Tilwara Mesolithic Site:** Tilwara on Luni river (Barmer) as a contemporary mesolithic companion site to Bagor *(RSSB Forester 2022)*.
18. **Nagari (Madhyamika):** Ancient city of Madhyamika near Chittorgarh yielding Shibhi Janapada coins *(RSSB CET 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Kalibangan Discovery & Location:** Discovered by Amlanand Ghosh in 1952–53 in Hanumangarh district on the dried bed of the Ghaggar (ancient Saraswati) river.
2. **Kalibangan Pre-Harappan Ploughed Field:** Contains world-first evidence of a joint-ploughed field (*Juta Hua Khet*) with perpendicular furrows for mustard and gram.
3. **Ganeshwar Title & River:** Located on Kantli river (Neem Ka Thana); crowned "Mother of Copper Age Civilizations" (*Tamrayugin Sanskritiyo ki Janani*).
4. **Ahar Alternate Names:** Anciently called *Aaghatpur* or *Aaghat Durg*, and popularly termed *Tamravati Nagari* (City of Copper).
5. **Bhabru Edict Finder & Site:** Captain Burt discovered the Ashokan Bhabru Rock Edict in 1837 at Bijak-ki-Pahadi, Bairat (Jaipur/Kotputli-Behror).
6. **Bagor Significance:** Located on Kothari river (Bhilwara); yields the oldest evidence of animal husbandry (*Pashupalan*) in India (Mesolithic era).
7. **Bairat Buddhist Monastery Excavator:** Rai Bahadur Dayaram Sahni excavated Bairat in 1936–37, uncovering circular brick stupas and Greek punch-marked coins.
8. **Ojhiyana Excavation:** Located in Bhilwara; key Banas culture site excavated by B.R. Mani (ASI) revealing white-painted red pottery.
9. **Gilund River & Location:** Banas culture site situated on the Banas river in Rajsamand district; excavated by B.B. Lal.
10. **Sunari Iron Smelting:** Sunari (Jhunjhunu) houses India's earliest iron-melting blast furnaces (*Loha Galane ki Bhatti*).

#### 3. Common Wrong-Answer Traps
- **Trap 1: Confusing Bhabru Edict Discoverer with Bairat Excavator:** Students mark Dayaram Sahni for discovering the Bhabru Edict. *Correction:* Captain Burt discovered the Bhabru Edict in 1837; Dayaram Sahni excavated the Bairat site in 1936–37.
- **Trap 2: Mixing up River Basins:** Confusing Ganeshwar (Kantli river) with Bagor (Kothari river) or Ahar (Berach/Ahar river). RSMSSB frequently swaps these in matching questions.
- **Trap 3: Metal Age Confusion:** Classifying Kalibangan as Iron Age or Pure Copper Age. *Correction:* Kalibangan is Bronze Age (Harappan/Pre-Harappan); Ahar and Ganeshwar are Copper Age (*Tamrayugin*); Sunari and Redh are Iron Age (*Lohayugin*).
- **Trap 4: Animal Domestication Location:** Marking Tilwara as the primary site for earliest animal husbandry. *Correction:* Bagor (Bhilwara) is the primary textbook answer for earliest animal husbandry in Rajasthan.

### SUBTOPIC A2: Major Dynasties, Key Rulers & Landmark Battles of Rajasthan
*(Guhil/Sisodia of Mewar, Rathore of Marwar/Bikaner, Kachwaha of Jaipur, Chauhans of Ajmer/Ranthambore/Jalore; Battles: Tarain, Ranthambore, Jalore, Khatoli, Khanwa, Giri Sumel, Haldighati, Rajmahal)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **Bappa Rawal & Eklingji:** Bappa Rawal founded Mewar dynasty statehood, captured Chittor from Man Mori in 734 AD, and built Eklingji temple at Kailashpuri *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
2. **Maharana Kumbha's Forts & Titles:** Built 32 of 84 forts in Mewar per *Veer Vinod* (Kaviraj Shyamaldas); titles: *Abhinav Bharatacharya*, *Rano Raso*, *Hal Guru*, *Sail Guru* *(RSSB LDC 2018, CET 2023)*.
3. **Vijay Stambha Construction:** Erected by Maharana Kumbha to commemorate victory over Mahmud Khilji of Malwa in Battle of Sarangpur (1437 AD); architect Jaita, Napa, Poma, Puja *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
4. **Maharana Sanga's Battles:** Battles of Khatoli (1517 vs Ibrahim Lodi), Gagron (1519 vs Mahmud Khilji II), Bayana (Feb 1527 vs Babur), and Khanwa (March 17, 1527 vs Babur) *(RSSB Patwari 2021, LDC 2018)*.
5. **Khanwa Battle Alliances:** Rajput confederacy under Sanga: Hasan Khan Mewati, Mahmud Lodi, Raja Bharmal of Ider, Maldeo Rathore of Jodhpur, Kalyanmal of Bikaner *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
6. **Battle of Haldighati Details:** Fought on June 18, 1576 AD (or June 21 per G.H. Ojha); Maharana Pratap vs Mughal commander Raja Man Singh I; commanders: Hakim Khan Suri (Pratap's vanguard), Jhala Manna (wore crown after Pratap injured), Chetak horse *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2022)*.
7. **Battle of Dewair (1582):** Victory of Maharana Pratap over Mughal commander Sultan Khan; termed "Marathon of Mewar" by James Tod *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
8. **Rao Jodha & Jodhpur:** Founded Jodhpur city and laid foundation of Mehrangarh Fort on Chintamani/Chiriatunk hill on May 12, 1459 AD *(RSSB LDC 2018, CET 2023)*.
9. **Rao Maldeo & Battle of Giri Sumel:** Fought in Jan 1544 AD at Jaitaran (Pali) between Sher Shah Suri and Maldeo's generals Jaita and Kumpa. Sher Shah's quote: *"For a handful of bajra, I would have lost the empire of Hindustan"* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
10. **Rao Chandrasen Titles:** Known as "Forgotten Hero of Marwar" (*Marwar ka Bhula Bisra Raja*), precursor to Maharana Pratap, refused Akbar's suzerainty at Nagaur Durbar (1570 AD) *(RSSB CET 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
11. **Raja Bharmal's Mughal Alliance:** First Rajput ruler to accept Akbar's suzerainty and marry daughter Harkha Bai (Mariam-uz-Zamani) to Akbar at Sambhar in Feb 1562 AD *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
12. **Sawai Jai Singh II Achievements:** Founded Jaipur city on Dec 18, 1727 AD (architect Vidyadhar Bhattacharya); built 5 astronomical observatories (*Jantar Mantar*) at Delhi, Jaipur, Ujjain, Varanasi, Mathura *(RSSB CET 2023, LDC 2018)*.
13. **Battles of Tarain (1191 & 1192):** Prithviraj Chauhan III vs Muhammad Ghori. First Tarain (1191) won by Prithviraj; Second Tarain (1192) lost; court poet Chand Bardai (*Prithviraj Raso*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
14. **Ranthambore Siege (1301 AD):** Hammir Dev Chauhan vs Alauddin Khilji; first Jauhar of Rajasthan history performed by Queen Rangdevi *(RSSB Patwari 2021)*.
15. **Jalore Siege (1311 AD):** Kanhaddev Chauhan and son Viramdev vs Alauddin Khilji; written in *Kanhadde Prabandha* by Padmanabh *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
16. **Battle of Rajmahal (1747 AD):** Sawai Ishwari Singh of Jaipur defeated Madho Singh and Maratha alliance; built Isarlat (*Sargasuli*) tower in Jaipur *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Sarangpur Battle & Vijay Stambha:** Battle of Sarangpur (1437 AD) won by Maharana Kumbha over Mahmud Khilji led to the construction of Vijay Stambha (1440–1448 AD) in Chittorgarh.
2. **Battle of Khanwa Exact Date:** Fought on March 17, 1527 AD at Khanwa (Bharatpur) where Babur used *Tulghuma* warfare tactic and declared *Jihad*.
3. **Haldighati Date & Commanders:** Fought on June 18, 1576 AD between Maharana Pratap (vanguard: Hakim Khan Suri) and Man Singh I (vanguard: Sayyid Hashim).
4. **Giri Sumel Battle & Quote:** Fought in Jan 1544 AD at Jaitaran (Pali); Sher Shah Suri defeated Jaita and Kumpa and uttered his famous bajra quote.
5. **First Rajput-Mughal Matrimonial Alliance:** Raja Bharmal of Amber married daughter Harkha Bai to Akbar at Sambhar in February 1562 AD.
6. **Jaipur Founding Date & Architect:** Founded on December 18, 1727 AD by Sawai Jai Singh II; chief architect was Bengali Brahmin Vidyadhar Bhattacharya.
7. **Jantar Mantar Observatories:** 5 Jantar Mantars built by Sawai Jai Singh II: Delhi (1724), Jaipur (1728 - UNESCO 2010), Ujjain, Varanasi, Mathura.
8. **First Jauhar of Rajasthan:** Took place at Ranthambore Fort in 1301 AD when Rani Rangdevi performed water-Jauhar (*Jal Jauhar*) during Alauddin Khilji's invasion.
9. **Guhil Dynasty Origin:** Founded by Guhaditya in 566 AD; Bappa Rawal established state sovereign power in 734 AD at Chittor.
10. **Rao Chandrasen's Resistance:** Rao Chandrasen Rathore of Marwar fought Akbar from Bhadrajun, Siwana, and Sojat without surrendering (1562–1581 AD).

#### 3. Common Wrong-Answer Traps
- **Trap 1: Confusing Sarangpur Victory Monument:** Marking Vijay Stambha as built after Haldighati or Khanwa. *Correction:* Vijay Stambha commemorates Sarangpur (1437 AD) victory over Malwa Sultan.
- **Trap 2: Swapping Haldighati Commanders:** Identifying Hakim Khan Suri as a Mughal commander. *Correction:* Hakim Khan Suri was Maharana Pratap's Afghan artillery/vanguard commander.
- **Trap 3: Giri Sumel Opponents:** Misattributing Giri Sumel battle to Maharana Sanga or Rana Pratap. *Correction:* Fought between Sher Shah Suri and Rao Maldeo Rathore's generals (Jaita & Kumpa).
- **Trap 4: Jantar Mantar Count & Locations:** Choosing 4 or 6 locations, or including Agra/Jodhpur. *Correction:* Exactly 5 locations: Delhi, Jaipur, Ujjain, Varanasi, Mathura (Agra and Jodhpur are FALSE distractors).

### SUBTOPIC A3: 1857 Uprising in Rajasthan & Freedom Struggle
*(6 Military Cantons, Auwa/Thakur Kushal Singh, Political Agents, Key Leaders & Rebellions)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **6 Military Cantons Roster:** Nasirabad (Ajmer), Neemuch (MP border/Mewar), Erinpura (Pali), Deoli (Tonk), Beawar (Ajmer), Kherwara (Udaipur) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
2. **Non-Revolting Cantons:** Identification of the two cantons that did NOT actively revolt: Beawar and Kherwara *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
3. **First Revolt Location & Date:** First outbreak at Nasirabad cantonment on May 28, 1857 by the 15th Native Infantry under Bakhtawar Singh *(RSSB CET 10th-12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
4. **Neemuch Revolt Date:** Revolt broke out on June 3, 1857 led by Mohammad Ali Beg and Subedar Hira Singh *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
5. **Erinpura & "Chalo Delhi" Slogan:** Revolt on August 21, 1857 at Erinpura by Jodhpur Legion soldiers giving the slogan *"Chalo Delhi, Maro Firingi"* led by Shivnath Singh *(RSSB Lab Assistant 2022, CET 2023)*.
6. **Agent to Governor-General (AGG):** George Patrick Lawrence served as AGG Rajasthan in 1857 (HQ Mount Abu / Ajmer) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
7. **Political Agents (PAs) Mapping:**
   - Jodhpur / Marwar: Captain Mock Mason *(severed head hung on Auwa fort gate)*.
   - Mewar / Udaipur: Major C.L. Showers.
   - Jaipur: Captain Eden.
   - Kota: Major Charles Burton *(decapitated in Kota streets)*.
   - Bharatpur: Captain Morrison *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
8. **Battle of Bithoda Date:** Fought September 8, 1857; Thakur Kushal Singh Champawat of Auwa defeated joint forces of Captain Heathcote and Jodhpur State (Raja Takht Singh) *(RSSB CET 2023, VDO 2021)*.
9. **Battle of Chetlawas (Black & White Battle):** Fought September 18, 1857; Kushal Singh defeated AGG Lawrence and Mock Mason *(Mock Mason killed)* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **Sugali Mata Idol:** Goddess of Auwa Thakur family having 10 heads and 54 arms; seized by British Officer Brigadier Holmes in Jan 1858 *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2022)*.
11. **Kota Rebellion Details:** Outbreak on October 15, 1857 led by Lala Jaidayal and Mehrab Khan; Maharao Ram Singh II imprisoned in his palace for 6 months *(RSSB CET 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
12. **Tantia Tope in Rajasthan:** Visited Rajasthan twice (first entered via Banswara in July 1858); captured Banswara and Jhalawar; betrayed by Man Singh Naruka of Narwar and hanged at Shivpuri on April 18, 1859 *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
13. **First Martyr of Rajasthan:** Amar Chand Bhatia (Bikaner native) hanged at Gwalior on June 22, 1858 for financially aiding Laxmi Bai and Tantia Tope ("Mangal Pandey of Rajasthan") *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
14. **Tonk Rebellion Unique Feature:** Women participated actively in the revolt at Tonk under Nawab Wazir-ud-Daula *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
15. **Dholpur Revolt:** Only revolt in Rajasthan where revolutionaries came from outside (Gwalior & Indore) and suppression forces also came from outside (Patiala State) *(RSSB CET 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **6 Cantons & Non-Revolters:** 6 cantons were Nasirabad, Neemuch, Erinpura, Deoli, Beawar, Kherwara; Beawar and Kherwara DID NOT participate in the uprising.

### SUBTOPIC A4: Peasant (Kisan) & Tribal Movements in Rajasthan
*(Bijolia, Begun, Meo, Bundi/Barad, Bhagat Movement/Govind Guru, Eki Movement/Motilal Tejawat)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **Bijolia Movement Duration & Nature:** World's longest non-violent peasant movement lasting 44 years (1897 to 1941 AD) in Mewar state (present Bhilwara district) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
2. **Bijolia Taxes (Lag-Bag):** Protests against 84 types of cesses, specifically *Chanwari Tax* (Rs. 5 per daughter's marriage imposed by Rao Krishan Singh in 1903) and *Talwar Bandhai Tax* (succession tax imposed by Rao Prithvi Singh in 1906) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
3. **Bijolia 3 Phases & Leaders:**
   - Phase 1 (1897–1915): Sadhu Sitaram Das, Fateh Karan Charan, Brahmadev.
   - Phase 2 (1916–1927): Vijay Singh Pathik (Bhoop Singh), Manikya Lal Verma.
   - Phase 3 (1927–1941): Jamnalal Bajaj, Haribhau Upadhyaya, Ramnarayan Choudhary *(RSSB CET 2023, VDO 2021)*.
4. **Uparmal Panch Board:** Founded on Hariyali Amavasya in 1917 AD at Barasal village by Vijay Singh Pathik; Manna Patel appointed Sarpanch *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
5. **Pratap Newspaper Role:** Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi published Bijolia peasant atrocities in his Kanpur-based Hindi weekly *Pratap* *(RSSB CET 2023, LDC 2018)*.
6. **Begun Movement & Govindpura Firing:** Launched in 1921 AD at Menal by Ramnarayan Choudhary. On July 13, 1923 AD, Trench Commission ordered firing at Govindpura village, martyring Rupa Ji Dhakad and Kripa Ji Dhakad *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
7. **Bundi / Barad Movement & Dabi Firing:** Launched in 1922 AD led by Pt. Nayanuram Sharma. On April 2, 1923 AD, Ikram Hussain ordered firing at Dabi village; Nanak Bheel was martyred while singing *Jhanda Geet* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2022)*.
8. **Neemu Chana Massacre (Alwar):** Fought May 14, 1925 AD against land rent increases under Maharaja Jai Singh; Chhatri Singh ordered firing. Mahatma Gandhi termed it *"Dyerism Double Distilled"* (*Riyasat* newspaper compared it to Jallianwala Bagh) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
9. **Meo Peasant Movement:** Launched in 1932 AD in Gurgaon-Alwar-Bharatpur region under Dr. Mohammad Ali and Anjuman-i-Khadim-ul-Islam *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
10. **Bhagat Movement & Samp Sabha:** Founded by Govind Guru (born in Bansia village, Dungarpur) who established *Samp Sabha* in 1883 AD at Sirohi to unite Bhils *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
11. **Mangarh Hill Massacre:** On Margashirsha Purnima, November 17, 1913 AD, British troops under Major Bailey and Captain Stockley fired on Bhils gathered at Mangarh Hill (Banswara), killing over 1,500 Bhils ("Jallianwala Bagh of Rajasthan") *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Forest Guard 2022)*.
12. **Eki / Matrikundiya Movement:** Launched in May 1921 AD by Motilal Tejawat ("Bawji") from Matrikundiya (Rashmi, Chittorgarh - "Haridwar of Rajasthan") *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
13. **Mewar Ki Pukar:** 21-point charter of demands drafted by Motilal Tejawat presented to Maharana Fateh Singh of Mewar (18 demands accepted, 3 rejected: forest rights, wild boars, Beth-Begar) *(RSSB CET 2023, LDC 2018)*.
14. **Neemda Massacre (Idar):** On March 7, 1922 AD, Major Sutton fired on Motilal Tejawat's gathering at Neemda village (Vijaymagri), martyring 1,200+ Adivasis *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Bijolia Movement Timeline:** Lasted 44 years (1897–1941 AD); started at Salatia village during Gangaram Dhakad's father's *Nukta* (death feast).
2. **Chanwari & Talwar Bandhai Taxes:** Rao Krishan Singh imposed Chanwari tax (1903); Rao Prithvi Singh imposed Talwar Bandhai tax (1906).

### SUBTOPIC B1: Forts, Monuments, Palaces & Museums of Rajasthan
*(6 UNESCO Hill Forts: Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore, Amber, Gagron, Jaisalmer; Mehrangarh, Junagarh, Taragarh, City Palaces, Key Museums)*

2. **Chittorgarh Fort Architecture & Jauhars:** Largest fort in Rajasthan; Mesa plateau; Water/Giri Fort; 3 historic Jauhars:
   - 1303 AD: Rani Padmini vs Alauddin Khilji.
   - 1535 AD: Rani Karnavati vs Bahadur Shah of Gujarat.
   - 1567–68 AD: Phool Kanwar (Jaimal & Patta) vs Akbar *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
3. **Kumbhalgarh Fort Features:** Built by Rana Kumbha; chief architect Mandan; 36 km long perimeter wall ("Great Wall of India"); contains *Katargarh* (Kumbha's residence & birthplace of Maharana Pratap) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
4. **Gagron Fort Classification:** Exemplary Water Fort (*Jal Durg / Audak Durg*) located at the confluence of Ahu and Kali Sindh rivers in Jhalawar (built on bare rock without foundation) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
5. **Ranthambore Fort Features:** Situated in Sawai Madhopur; shape of an oval/egg; contains *Trinetra Ganesha Temple*, *Padam Talao*, and *32-Pillared Cenotaph* (*32 Khambon ki Chhatri*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
6. **Jaisalmer (Sonar Qilla) Architecture:** Built on Trikuta hill in 1156 AD by Maharawal Jaisal; constructed using yellow sandstone without mortar/cement; 99 bastions (*Burj*); famous for 2.5 Jauhars *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
7. **Junagarh Fort (Bikaner):** Built by Raja Rai Singh (1589–1594 AD); chief architect Karam Chand; called *"Zameen Ka Zewar"*; features Suraj Pol with statues of Jaimal and Patta *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
8. **Mehrangarh Fort (Jodhpur):** Built on Chintamani/Chiriatunk hill in 1459 AD by Rao Jodha; Rudyard Kipling quote: *"Built by angels, fairies and giants"*; contains *Phool Mahal*, *Moti Mahal*, *Shingar Chowki* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2022)*.
9. **Taragarh Fort Ajmer:** Built on Garh Beetli hill by Ajayraj Chauhan (1113 AD); called *"Gibraltar of Rajasthan"* by Bishop Heber *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **Taragarh Fort Bundi:** Built in 1354 AD by Rao Bar Singh; famous for *Chitrashala* (paintings) and *Garbha Gunjan* cannon *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
11. **Bhanwar/Nahargarh Fort Jaipur:** Built by Sawai Jai Singh II in 1734 AD; contains *Madhavendra Bhawan* (9 identical suites built by Sawai Madho Singh II for his 9 queens) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
12. **Albert Hall Museum Jaipur:** Foundation laid in 1876 AD by Prince of Wales (Albert Edward); designed by Sir Samuel Swinton Jacob in Indo-Saracenic style; opened 1887 AD *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
13. **Government Museum Ahar (Udaipur):** Preserves Copper Age artifacts, terracotta bulls, and black-and-red pottery excavated from Ahar mound *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
14. **City Palace Udaipur:** Built on Pichola lake banks by Maharana Udai Singh II (1559 AD); Fergusson called it the *"Windsor Palaces of Rajasthan"* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts

### SUBTOPIC B2: Fairs & Festivals of Rajasthan & Lunar Calendar Dates

10. **Dhinga Gavar / Dhinga Gangaur:** Celebrated at Jodhpur on Vaishakh Krishna Tritiya; women wield sticks (*Lathmar*) to hit men *(RSSB CET 2023)*.

### SUBTOPIC B3: Folk Deities of Rajasthan (Lok Devta — Panch Pirs & Lok Deviya)

### SUBTOPIC B4: Rajasthani Folk Dances, Folk Music & Musical Instruments
*(Kalbelia/UNESCO, Ghoomar, Agni, Kacchi Ghodi, Terah Tali, Chari, Bam, Gair; Langa/Manganiyar, Ravanhattha, Kamaycha, Algoza)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **Kalbelia Dance UNESCO Year & Instruments:** Inscribed on UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2010; danced by snake-charmers community; instruments: *Been / Pungi* and *Khanjari*; world-famous dancer Gulabo Sapera *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
2. **Ghoomar Dance Features:** Official State Dance of Rajasthan ("Soul of Rajasthani Folk Dance" / "Rajnagariya Dance"); performed by women in circular movements with 8 beats called *Sawaai* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
3. **Agni Dance (Fire Dance):** Performed by Jasnathi sect monks at Katariasar (Bikaner) over red-hot glowing charcoal embers; dancers enter with chants of *"Fateh Fateh!"* and perform agricultural acts *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
4. **Kacchi Ghodi Dance:** Professional folk dance of Shekhawati region performed by men riding wooden dummy horses with swords during weddings; simulates dummy horse battles *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
5. **Terah Tali Dance:** Performed by Kamad women sitting on ground with 13 cymbals (*Manjiras*) tied to their body (9 on right leg, 2 on elbows, 2 in hands); performed in praise of Baba Ramdevji; famous dancer Mohini Devi and Sugna Bai *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
6. **Chari Dance:** Performed by Gujjar women of Kishangarh/Ajmer with brass pots (*Chari*) holding lit cotton seeds on their head; famous dancer Faleku Bai *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
7. **Bam / Bam Rasiya Dance:** Performed by men in Bharatpur and Mewat region during Holi/spring harvest playing huge drum (*Bam / Nagada*) and singing Rasiya songs *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
8. **Gair Dance:** Folk dance of Bhil men during Holi in Mewar and Barmer playing wooden sticks (*Ganda*) in circular formation *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
9. **Dhol Dance (Jalore):** Professional dance of Jalore performed by Sargara and Dholi communities using *Thakna* style drum beats; popularized by CM Jainarayan Vyas *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **State Musical Instrument (Algoza):** Algoza is the official State Instrument of Rajasthan — a pair of joined wooden flutes (*Sushir Vadya*) played together *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
11. **Instrument Classification Mapping:**
    - **Tat Vadya (Stringed):** Ravanhattha, Kamaycha, Sarangi, Jantar, Bhapang, Ektara, Chikara.
    - **Sushir Vadya (Wind):** Algoza, Shehnai, Bankia, Satara, Pungi, Pungi, Pungi, Pungi, Murali.
    - **Avanaddh Vadya (Membrane/Drum):** Dhol, Nagada, Chang, Mandal, Damru, Khanjari, Dhonsa.
    - **Ghan Vadya (Solid Metal):** Manjira, Khartal, Jhanjh, Chimta, Thali *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
12. **Ravanhattha Specs:** Half-cut coconut shell attached to a bamboo stick with horsehair strings played with a bow (*Gaj*); used by Bhopas reciting Pabuji's Phad *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
13. **Kamaycha Specs:** 17-stringed instrument made of mango wood covered with goat skin; played by Manganiyar musicians of Jaisalmer/Barmer; master craftsman Sakar Khan (Padma Shri) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
14. **Bhapang Master:** Zahoor Khan Mewati and Umar Farooq Mewati are famous masters of Bhapang (plucked string instrument played in Mewat) *(RSSB CET 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Kalbelia UNESCO Year & Dancer:** Inscribed on UNESCO list in 2010; lead exponent Gulabo Sapera.
2. **Ghoomar Beat Term:** 8-beat circular movement termed *Sawaai*.
3. **Agni Dance Origin & Sect:** Katariasar (Bikaner) by Jasnathi sect monks (*Fateh Fateh!* chant).
4. **Terah Tali Cymbals Count:** Exactly 13 Manjiras tied to female dancer's body sitting down.

8. **Ravanhattha Shell Base:** Crafted using a half-cut coconut shell (*Nariyal ki Katori*).
9. **Kamaycha Master Craftsman:** Sakar Khan Manganiyar (Jaisalmer).
10. **Bhapang Region & Artist:** Mewat region; master artist Zahoor Khan Mewati.

#### 3. Common Wrong-Answer Traps
- **Trap 1: Algoza Category:** Classifying Algoza as a Tat (stringed) or Ghan instrument. *Correction:* Algoza is a Sushir (wind) instrument.
- **Trap 2: Swapping Manganiyar and Langa Instruments:** Kamaycha is played by MANGANIYARS; Sarangi and Kamaicha variants played by LANGAS.

- **Trap 4: Agni Dance Sect:** Attributing Agni dance to Bishnoi or Ramsnehi sect. *Correction:* Associated exclusively with JASNATHI sect.

### SUBTOPIC B5: Tribes of Rajasthan & Cultural Customs
*(Mina, Bhil, Garasia, Sahariya, Kathodi, Damor; Demographics, Housing, Customs: Nata, Halma, Dapa, Bharadi, Gavari)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **Tribal Demographic Rankings:**
   - 1st largest tribe: Mina (highest concentration in Jaipur, Dausa, Sawai Madhopur).
   - 2nd largest tribe: Bhil (oldest tribe; highest concentration in Udaipur, Banswara, Dungarpur).
   - 3rd largest tribe: Garasia (highest concentration in Sirohi — Abu Road/Pindwara) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
2. **Sole PVTG Tribe:** Sahariya is the ONLY tribe in Rajasthan classified as a Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG) by Govt of India; concentrated in Shahbad and Kishanganj blocks of Baran district *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
3. **Kathodi Tribe Specialization:** Concentrated in Udaipur (Jhadol/Kotra); migrated from Maharashtra; specialists in extracting Catechu (*Katha*) paste from Khair trees (*Acacia catechu*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
4. **Damor Tribe Concentration:** Concentrated in Dungarpur district (Seemalwara block); unique feature: men wear jewelry and ornaments similar to women *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
5. **Bhil Tribe Terminology:**
   - House: *Khoo* or *Tapra*.
   - Village Cluster: *Pal*; Village Head: *Gameti*.
   - Loincloth: *Langot* or *Khoytu*; Tight Pajama: *Dhepada*.
   - Headwrap: *Fanta* or *Patiya*.
   - Sacred Tree: Mahua tree (*Madhuca longifolia*) used to brew liquor (*Mudi*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
6. **Bharadi Painting:** Sacred bridal wall painting painted by Bhil artists during weddings depicting Goddess Bharadi *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
7. **Garasia Tribe Traditions:**
   - Consider Nakki Lake (Mount Abu) sacred for immersing ancestral ashes.
   - Marriage Forms: *Mor Bandhiya* (formal Brahma marriage), *Pehraavna* (nominal vows), *Taadna* (groom pays bride price) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
8. **Valar Dance:** Graceful circular dance of Garasia tribe performed without any musical instrument *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
9. **Sahariya Tribe Customs:**
   - Village Cluster: *Sahrana*; Village Head: *Kotwal*.
   - Tree Houses / Granaries: *Gopna*, *Koruwa*, or *Topriya*.
   - Fair: Sitabari Fair (Kelwara, Baran — "Kumbh of Sahariyas") *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **Customs Definitions:**
    - **Nata Pratha:** Widow/deserted woman cohabitation custom without formal wedding.
    - **Dapa Pratha:** Bride price paid by groom's family to bride's father.
    - **Halma / Lada:** Community mutual self-help custom among Bhil tribe for house building or farming *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
11. **Mina Tribe Origin & Text:** Originate from *Matsya Avatar* (Fish Incarnation of Lord Vishnu); sacred scripture is *Meen Puran* written by Muni Magan Sagar *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
12. **Faire-Faire War Cry:** *Faire-Faire* is the traditional war cry of Bhil warriors entering battle wielding bows and arrows *(RSSB CET 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Tribe Numerical Order:** Mina (1st), Bhil (2nd), Garasia (3rd).

### SUBTOPIC B6: Rajasthani Painting Schools (Chitrakala)
*(Mewar, Marwar, Kishangarh/Bani Thani, Bundi, Kota, Dhundhar/Jaipur, Nathdwara)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **4 Major Schools (School Categories):** Mewar School, Marwar School, Hadoti School, Dhundhar School *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
2. **Mewar School — Oldest Manuscript:** *Sravaka Pratikramana Sutra Churni* painted in 1260 AD at Ahar during Rana Tejasimha's reign by painter Kamal Chandra *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
3. **Mewar Golden Age & Painters:** Golden Age during Maharana Jagat Singh I (1628–1652 AD); famous painters Sahibdin and Manohar; Jagat Singh established *Chiteron ki Bari* (*Tasma-i-Tasveer*) art gallery inside City Palace *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
4. **Nathdwara Style (Pichhwai):** Sub-style of Mewar School; cloth backdrop paintings (*Pichhwai*) depicting Lord Shrinathji's childhood acts (*Bal-Leela*); famous female painters Kamala and Ilaichi *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
5. **Kishangarh Style & Bani Thani:** Sub-style of Marwar School; golden age during Raja Sawai Samant Singh (*Nagari Das*); iconic painting *Bani Thani* painted by Nihal Chand *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
6. **Bani Thani Recognition:** Termed "Mona Lisa of India" by art critic Eric Dickinson and Dr. Fayyaz Ali; Govt of India issued 20-Paisa postage stamp on Bani Thani on May 5, 1989 AD *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2020)*.
7. **Bundi Painting Style Specialty:** World-famous for natural landscape, lush green vegetation, birds, animals, clouds, and water bodies; golden age under Maharao Surjan Singh / Ummed Singh (*Chitrashala* built at Bundi Fort) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
8. **Kota Painting Style Specialty:** World-famous for hunting scenes (*Shikar* scenes) where women and royal queens are depicted hunting wild animals alongside kings *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
9. **Jaipur / Dhundhar Style Features:** Golden age under Sawai Pratap Singh; large-sized portraits (*Adamkad Chitra*) painted by Sahib Ram; marble statue making and mural paintings (*Fresco Buno / Arayash*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **Alwar Sub-Style Features:** Ivory plate painting (*Hathi Dant Par Chitrakari*); manuscript illustration of Sheikh Saadi's *Gulistan* by court painters Daluram and Baldev *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
11. **Bikaner Sub-Style Artists:** Artisans call themselves *Ustas* or *Matherans*; sign their name and date at the bottom of paintings (*Khaas Khata*) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
12. **Anand Coomaraswamy Classification:** First scientific classification of Rajasthani painting published in his 1916 book *Rajput Painting* *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Oldest Dated Mewar Manuscript:** *Sravaka Pratikramana Sutra Churni* (1260 AD, Ahar) by Kamal Chandra.
2. **Mewar Court Art Gallery:** *Chiteron ki Bari* built by Maharana Jagat Singh I (painters Sahibdin & Manohar).
3. **Nathdwara Pichhwai & Female Painters:** Pichhwai cloth art; famous female painters Kamala and Ilaichi.
4. **Bani Thani Artist & Patron:** Painted by Nihal Chand for King Samant Singh (Nagari Das).
5. **Bani Thani Stamp Date:** Govt of India issued postage stamp on May 5, 1989 AD.
6. **Bundi Style Specialty:** Bird and nature painting (*Pakshi Shaili*) & *Chitrashala*.
7. **Kota Style Specialty:** Hunting scenes depicting queens hunting wild animals (*Shikar Shaili*).
8. **Jaipur Portrait Painter:** Sahib Ram painted life-sized portraits (*Adamkad*).
9. **Bikaner Painting Signing Custom:** Usta and Matheran artists sign their name and date on artwork.
10. **First Scientific Book on Rajput Art:** *Rajput Painting* (1916) by Dr. Anand Coomaraswamy.

#### 3. Common Wrong-Answer Traps
- **Trap 1: Swapping Bundi & Kota Specialties:** Attributing hunting scenes to Bundi or bird scenes to Kota. *Correction:* Bundi = Birds & Nature; Kota = Hunting (*Shikar*).
- **Trap 2: Swapping Sahibdin and Sahib Ram:** Confusing Mewar painter Sahibdin with Jaipur painter Sahib Ram.
- **Trap 3: Swapping Female Painters:** Kamala & Ilaichi belong to NATHDWARA; Sahibba belongs to AJMER style.
- **Trap 4: Bani Thani School:** Classifying Kishangarh under Mewar or Dhundhar school. *Correction:* Kishangarh belongs to MARWAR School.

### SUBTOPIC B7: Handicrafts, Textiles & Cultural Heritage of Rajasthan
*(Blue Pottery, Usta Kala, Thewa Kala, Bandhej/Tie-Dye, Kota Doria, Akola Dabu Print, Bassi Woodcraft, Tarkashi)*

#### 1. What PYQs Actually Ask (PYQ Topic Analysis — SECONDARY EVIDENCE)
1. **Blue Pottery Origins & Exponent:** Imported from Persia via Lahore during Maharaja Ram Singh II's reign in Jaipur; master artisan Padma Shri Kripal Singh Shekhawat (developed 25-color *Kripal Kumbh* style) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018, Police Constable 2020)*.
2. **Blue Pottery Composition:** Made WITHOUT clay (*Mrtika*); crafted using quartz powder, ground glass, borax (*Suhaga*), Fuller's earth (*Multani Mitti*), and copper sulphate *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
3. **Usta Kala (Bikaner):** Fine gold embossing and miniature painting on camel hide/leather; master artisan Padma Shri Hisamuddin Usta; Camel Hide Training Centre at Bikaner *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.
4. **Thewa Kala (Pratapgarh):** Intricate 23-Karat gold wire foil work embossed on colored Belgian glass (green/red); practiced exclusively by the Rajsoni family (founding master Nathu Ji Soni); secret male-hereditary craft *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, VDO 2021)*.
5. **Kota Doria / Masuria Saree:** Woven at Kaithoon (Kota) and Mangrol (Baran) on pit looms using cotton, silk, and gold Zari threads in square patterns called *Khats*; holds GI Tag *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, LDC 2018)*.
6. **Bandhej & Traditional Textiles:**
   - **Lahariya:** Diagonal striped tie-and-dye pattern worn on Teej in Jaipur.
   - **Mothra:** Cross-hatched diagonal grid pattern.
   - **Pomcha:** Yellow/pink lotus pattern veil worn by new mothers (Yellow *Pila Pomcha* for boy, Pink *Gulabi Pomcha* for girl) *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Police Constable 2022)*.
7. **Dabu Print Variations & Sites:**
   - **Akola Dabu Print (Chittorgarh):** Mud-resist print using Berach river water.
   - **Gazi Dabu:** Wheat-chaff resist (*Bidar Dabu*) at Sanganer.
   - **Wax Dabu (*Moom Dabu*):** Sawai Madhopur *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
8. **Sanganeri vs Bagru Prints:**
   - **Sanganeri Print:** Fine floral prints on white/off-white background using natural dyes; popularized by Munna Lal Goyal.
   - **Bagru Print:** Dark background (black/red) using wooden blocks and natural mud dyes by Chhipa community *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Lab Assistant 2022)*.
9. **Bassi Woodcraft (Chittorgarh):** Village Bassi is famous for wooden shrines (*Kavad*), wooden toys, and puppets (*Katputli*); master family of Prabhat Ji Sutar *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
10. **Tarkashi of Nathdwara:** Inlaying fine silver wires (*Tarkashi*) into wooden boxes and ornaments *(RSSB CET 2023)*.
11. **Matheran Art (Bikaner):** Religious wall paintings and clay icons created by Matheran Jain artisans in Bikaner *(RSSB CET 12th 2023)*.
12. **Molda / Badla (Jodhpur):** Zinc water bottles (*Badla*) covered with felt/cloth to keep water cool in Thar desert *(RSSB CET 12th 2023, Patwari 2021)*.

#### 2. Top 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Blue Pottery Base Material:** Made without clay (uses quartz, glass powder, and gum).
2. **Blue Pottery Exponent:** Kripal Singh Shekhawat (Mau, Sikar) awarded Padma Shri in 1974.
3. **Thewa Kala Location & Family:** Pratapgarh; Rajsoni family (gold foil on Belgian glass).
4. **Usta Kala Artisan & Medium:** Hisamuddin Usta; gold painting on camel hide in Bikaner.
5. **Kota Doria Weaving Unit:** Kaithoon (Kota); 70% cotton + 30% silk woven into *Khats* (GI Tagged).
6. **Lahariya & Pomcha Textile:** Lahariya = Teej diagonal stripes; Yellow Pomcha = Birth of male child.
7. **Akola Dabu Print Location:** Chittorgarh on Berach river (mud-resist dye).
8. **Badla Material & City:** Zinc water containers crafted in Jodhpur.
9. **Bassi Woodcraft & Kavad:** Bassi (Chittorgarh); portable wooden folding temples (*Kavad*).
10. **Tarkashi Art City:** Nathdwara (Rajsamand) — silver wire inlay in wood.

#### 3. Common Wrong-Answer Traps
- **Trap 1: Blue Pottery Composition:** Believing Blue Pottery uses black/red ceramic clay. *Correction:* Uses ZERO clay; made from quartz sand and glass powder.
- **Trap 2: Swapping Thewa and Usta Locations:** Matching Thewa to Bikaner or Usta to Pratapgarh.
- **Trap 3: Sanganeri vs Bagru Ground Color:** Sanganeri uses WHITE background; Bagru uses DARK RED/BLACK background.
- **Trap 4: Badla Material:** Believing Badla is made of clay or copper. *Correction:* Made exclusively of ZINC (*Jasta*).
