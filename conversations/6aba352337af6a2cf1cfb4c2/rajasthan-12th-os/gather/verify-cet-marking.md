## Fact 1: CET 12th Level Written Exam Marking Rules & 5th Option Penalty

### 1. Negative Marking for Wrong Answers
* **Verdict:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Official Rules Evolution:**
  * **2022 Notification (Advt. No. 10/2022 dated 10.10.2022):** Under Section 14 (*Scheme of Examination*), the initial rule explicitly stated: *"इस परीक्षा में नकारात्मक अंकन (Negative Marking) नहीं किया जायेगा।"* (No negative marking for wrong answers).
  * **2024 Notification & Updated Pattern (Advt. No. 08/2024 dated 14.03.2024 onwards):** RSSB introduced **1/3 negative marking** (deduction of 1/3rd mark for each wrong answer) in the written examination scheme for CET Senior Secondary level.

### 2. Penalty Rule for Unattempted Questions (5th Option / Option 'E' Rule)
* **Verdict:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Official Rule Details:**
  * **5-Option OMR System:** Every question on the OMR answer sheet has 5 options: **A, B, C, D, and E**.
  * **Unattempted Questions ("अनुत्तरित प्रश्न"):** If a candidate chooses not to attempt a question, they MUST darken/fill **Option 'E'**.
  * **Deduction Penalty:** If a question is left completely unattempted without darkening Option 'E' (i.e. none of the 5 options A-E are filled), **1/3 (one-third) of the marks allocated to that question** will be deducted as a penalty for EACH such question.
  * **Disqualification Threshold:** If a candidate leaves more than **10% of the total questions** un-darkened (failing to mark any option A through E for >15 questions out of 150), the candidate is **DISQUALIFIED (`अयोग्य घोषित`)** from the exam.

### Verification of Candidate Posts (6 or 7 Count Resolution)

* **CONFIRMED INCLUDED (7 Services / 5 Job Categories):**
  1. **LDC / Junior Assistant:** **INCLUDED** (Spans 3 Services: Secretariat Clerk Gr-II, RPSC Clerk Gr-II, Subordinate Offices Junior Assistant).
  2. **Police Constable (कांस्टेबल):** **INCLUDED** (Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service).
  3. **Forester (वनपाल):** **INCLUDED** (Rajasthan Forest Subordinate Service).
  4. **Hostel Superintendent (छात्रावास अधीक्षक):** **INCLUDED** (Rajasthan Minority Affairs Subordinate Service).
  5. **Jamadar Grade-II (जमादार ग्रेड-II):** **INCLUDED** (Rajasthan Excise Subordinate Service).

* **CONFIRMED EXCLUDED (Not in CET 12th Level Merit List):**
  * **Forest Guard (वनरक्षक):** **EXCLUDED.** Only **Forester (वनपाल)** is in the 12th-level CET merit list. Forest Guard is recruited separately / 10th-level non-CET rules.
  * **Jail Prahari (जेल प्रहरी):** **EXCLUDED.** Jail Prahari is conducted directly by the Jail Department outside CET 12th level. (Note: *Assistant Jailor / Deputy Jailor / उप-जेलर* is recruited under CET Graduation level, not 12th level).
