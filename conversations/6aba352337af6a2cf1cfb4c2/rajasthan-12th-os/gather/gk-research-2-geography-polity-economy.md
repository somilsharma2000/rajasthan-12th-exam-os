#### B. The 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Chambal Perennial System:** Chambal is Rajasthan's only perennial river, originating at Janapav Hills (Vindhyan Range, MP), entering Rajasthan at Chaurasigarh (Chittorgarh), and forming a 241 km interstate boundary with Madhya Pradesh.
2. **Banas Inland Flow:** Banas river originates at Khamnor Hills (Rajsamand) and is the longest river flowing entirely within Rajasthan (512 km); known as 'Varna-Asha' (Hope of the Forest).
3. **Luni Water Salinity:** Luni river originates at Nag Pahar (Ajmer); its water remains fresh for 224 km up to Balotra (Balotra district) and turns saline downstream due to high salt content in the desert soil.
4. **Jojari Anomaly:** Jojari is the ONLY right-bank tributary of Luni and the ONLY tributary that does not originate in the Aravalli hills (originates at Padlu village, Nagaur).
5. **Mahi & Tropic of Cancer:** Mahi river originates at Mehad Lake (Amjhera, Dhar, MP), enters Rajasthan at Khandu village (Banswara), cuts the Tropic of Cancer twice, and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat.
6. **Beneshwar Triveni Sangam:** Beneshwar Dham (Sabla, Dungarpur) is the confluence of Mahi, Som, and Jakham rivers, famous as the 'Kumbh of Tribals' on Magh Purnima.
7. **Ghaggar Inland Channel:** Ghaggar river originates at Kalka (Shivalik Hills, HP), enters Rajasthan at Talwara (Hanumangarh), and is known locally as 'Nali' or 'Soti' and as 'Hakra' in Pakistan during floods.
8. **Kakni River Record:** Kakni (Masurdi) river in Jaisalmer is the shortest inland river in Rajasthan (~17 km) and terminates in Bhuj Lake.
9. **Bisalpur Drinking Water Project:** Bisalpur Dam across Banas River in Toda Rai Singh (Tonk) is Rajasthan's largest drinking water project, supplying Jaipur, Ajmer, and Tonk.
10. **Jakham Dam Height Record:** Jakham Dam built across Jakham River in Pratapgarh district is the tallest dam in Rajasthan at a height of 81 meters.

#### C. Common Traps & Exam Confusions
- **Trap 1 (Bedach vs Ayar Name Change):** Candidates confuse Bedach with Banas. Bedach originates as 'Ayar' at Gogunda Hills (Udaipur) and takes the name 'Bedach' only AFTER exiting Udai Sagar Lake.
- **Trap 2 (Luni Drainage Destination):** Option traps claim Luni flows into the Bay of Bengal. Luni belongs strictly to the Arabian Sea drainage system and terminates in the Rann of Kutch (Gujarat).
- **Trap 3 (Chambal Dams Sequence):** Misordering Chambal dams from South to North. The correct sequence from upstream (MP) to downstream (Rajasthan) is: 1. Gandhi Sagar (MP), 2. Rana Pratap Sagar (Rawatbhata, Chittorgarh), 3. Jawahar Sagar (Kota), 4. Kota Barrage (Kota). Gandhi Sagar is the ONLY dam situated outside Rajasthan.
- **Trap 4 (Banas vs West Banas):** Banas originates in Khamnor Hills (Rajsamand) and flows into Chambal (Bay of Bengal); West Banas originates in Naya Sanwara (Sirohi) and flows into Little Rann of Kutch (Arabian Sea).
- **Trap 5 (Luni Tributary Bank Identification):** Bandi, Sukri, Jawai, Guhiya, Lilri, and Sagi are LEFT-bank tributaries; Jojari is the SINGLE RIGHT-bank tributary.

#### B. The 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Sambhar National Salt Contribution:** Sambhar Lake accounts for ~8.7% of India's total salt production and is Rajasthan's largest inland saltwater lake.
2. **Pachpadra High-Purity Salt:** Pachpadra Lake (Balotra district) yields 98% sodium chloride salt, harvested traditionally by the Kharwal caste using Morli (Leptadenia pyrotechnica) twigs.
3. **Jaisamand Dhebar Record:** Jaisamand Lake was built by Maharana Jai Singh (1685-1691 AD) damming the Gomti river; it is Rajasthan's largest artificial freshwater lake (area ~87 sq km).
4. **Raj Prasasti Stone Monument:** Rajsamand Lake features 'Raj Prasasti' on Nau Chowki Pal—25 black marble slabs containing 1,106 Sanskrit verses written by Ranchhod Bhatt Telang.
5. **Ana Sagar Architecture:** Ana Sagar Lake (built by Arnoraja Chauhan ~1135-1150 AD) features Daulat Bagh (Subhash Udyan) added by Jahangir and 5 white marble Baradaris built by Shah Jahan in 1637 AD.
6. **Nakki Lake Elevation:** Nakki Lake (Mount Abu) situated at 1,200 meters elevation is Rajasthan's highest altitude lake; features unique rock formations Toad Rock, Nun Rock, and Nandi Rock.
7. **Pichola Lake Origin:** Pichola Lake was built in the late 14th century during Maharana Lakha's reign by a Banjara grain merchant (Pichhu Chhita); contains Jag Mandir, Jag Niwas (Lake Palace), and Natini Ka Chabutara.
8. **Didwana Industrial Salt:** Didwana Lake produces salt with high sodium sulphate content, making it unfit for direct human consumption and supplying chemical factories (Rajasthan State Chemical Works).
9. **Pushkar Sacred Lake:** Pushkar Lake features 52 bathing ghats (including Gau Ghat and Brahma Ghat) and is the venue for the world-famous Pushkar Camel Fair on Kartik Purnima.
10. **Siliserh Palace Lake:** Siliserh Lake (Alwar) was constructed in 1845 by Maharaja Vinay Singh for Queen Shila; called the 'Lake Palace / Venice of Rajasthan'.

#### C. Common Traps & Exam Confusions
- **Trap 1 (Sambhar Production Figure):** Question choices list 87% instead of 8.7%. Sambhar produces 8.7% of INDIA'S salt, NOT 87%.
- **Trap 2 (Didwana vs Pachpadra Salt Quality):** Didwana salt is NON-EDIBLE (high sodium sulphate); Pachpadra salt is the MOST EDIBLE and highest purity (98% NaCl).
- **Trap 3 (Jaisamand Island Names):** Misidentifying largest vs smallest island. Largest is Baba Ka Bhakra (or Baba Ka Magra); SMALLEST is Pyari.
- **Trap 4 (Fateh Sagar Builder History):** Originally constructed by Maharana Jai Singh in 1687 (as Diwali Talab), but reconstructed and renamed after Maharana Fateh Singh in 1888.
- **Trap 5 (Jag Mandir vs Jag Niwas Location):** Both Jag Mandir and Jag Niwas (Lake Palace) are located in Lake Pichola (Udaipur), NOT in Lake Rajsamand or Lake Jaisamand.

#### B. The 10 Highest-Yield Facts
1. **Guru Shikhar Record:** Guru Shikhar (1,722 m, Mount Abu, Sirohi) is the highest peak in Rajasthan and Central India; dubbed "Santonn Ka Shikhar" (Peak of Saints) by Col. James Tod.
2. **Aravalli Antiquity:** Aravalli range is the world's oldest relict fold mountain system (Pre-Cambrian era, ~692 km total length from Khedbrahma to Delhi; 550 km or ~80% in Rajasthan).
3. **Oriya Plateau Elevation:** Oriya Plateau (1,360 m, Sirohi) is the highest plateau in Rajasthan, situated directly at the base of Guru Shikhar.
4. **Bhorat Water Divide:** Bhorat Plateau lies between Kumbhalgarh (Rajsamand) and Gogunda (Udaipur), forming the major watershed divide between Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal drainage.
5. **Thar Desert Demographics:** Thar Desert occupies 61.11% of Rajasthan's total land area and contains 40% of its population, making it the most densely populated desert in the world.
6. **Barchan Mobility:** Barchans are crescent-shaped, highly mobile sand dunes prevalent in Churu, Suratgarh, Bhaleri, and Jaisalmer, representing the chief driver of desertification.
7. **Chhappan Ka Maidan Basin:** Chhappan Ka Maidan is the fertile alluvial plain of the Mahi river between Banswara and Pratapgarh, named after 56 village clusters / river streams.
8. **Great Boundary Fault (GBF):** GBF is a prominent tectonic fault line separating the Aravalli range from the Vindhyan plateau, running through Chittorgarh, Bhilwara, Bundi, and Sawai Madhopur.
9. **Northern & Central Aravalli Peaks:** Raghunathgarh (1,055 m, Sikar) is the highest peak of Northern Aravalli; Taragarh (870 m, Ajmer) and Nag Pahar (795 m) dominate Central Aravalli.

#### C. Common Traps & Exam Confusions
- **Trap 1 (Jan Aadhaar Digit Count):** Family ID is TEN (10) digits; Individual Member ID is ELEVEN (11) digits.
- **Trap 2 (Sujash vs Economic Review Publisher):** Sujash is published by DIPR (`dipr.rajasthan.gov.in`); Economic Review is published by DES (`des.rajasthan.gov.in`).
- **Trap 3 (Jan Soochna Portal RTI Section):** Launched under Section 4(2) of RTI Act 2005 (proactive public disclosure), NOT Section 6 or Section 19.
- **Trap 4 (Helpline Number Confusion):** CM Helpline / Sampark is 181; Women Helpline is 1090; Child Helpline is 1098; Cyber Crime Helpline is 1930.
- **Trap 5 (Exam Portal URL):** Exam notifications, master answer keys, and candidate portal are hosted on `rssb.rajasthan.gov.in` (Selection Board) and `rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in` (Public Service Commission).
