   - *Report Citation:* RSSB Advt 06/2024 Scheme Clause 11.
   - *Independent Source:* Rajasthan Staff Selection Board Standing OMR Marking Orders & Public Service Examination Norms 2023-2024.
   - *Pass 3 Verdict:* **CONFIRMED**
3. **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass) + Computer Qualification (RSCIT / DOEACC O-Level / COPA / Diploma or Degree in CS/IT) + Qualified CET 12th Level. Mandatory Rule: Candidate MUST score a minimum of 9 marks (36%) out of 25 in EACH of the 4 typing test components (Hindi Speed, Hindi Efficiency, English Speed, English Efficiency).
   - *Report Citation:* RSSB Advt 06/2024 Clause 6.
   - *Independent Source:* Department of Administrative Reforms (Group-3) Rajasthan Notification on Ministerial Staff Qualifications & Typing Standards.
   - *Pass 3 Verdict:* **CONFIRMED**
4. **Stages:** Stage 1: CET 12th Shortlisting (15x ratio) -> Stage 2: Phase-I Written Exam (2 Papers, 200 Marks) -> Stage 3: Phase-II Computer Typing & Efficiency Test (3x ratio shortlist, 100 Marks) -> Stage 4: Document Verification & Final Merit (out of 300 Marks).
   - *Report Citation:* RSSB LDC Exam Rules & Syllabus PDF.
   - *Independent Source:* RSSB Official LDC Selection Scheme Bulletin & Rajasthan Subordinate Staff Rules.
   - *Pass 3 Verdict:* **CONFIRMED**
5. **Pay Matrix Level:** Pay Level L-5 (Pre-revised Grade Pay ₹2,400 under 7th CPC). Fixed probation stipend: ₹14,600/month for 2 years. Post-probation starting Basic Pay: ₹20,800/month.
