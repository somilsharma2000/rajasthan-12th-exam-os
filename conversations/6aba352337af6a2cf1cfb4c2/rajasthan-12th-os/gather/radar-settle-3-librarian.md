- FACT: Rajasthan Librarian Grade-3 (ग्रेड-3 पुस्तकालयाध्यापक) recruitment — minimum educational qualification required (12th-pass with Certificate in Library Science vs Graduate).
- VERDICT: SETTLED
- OFFICIAL VALUE: (i) Senior Secondary (10+2) with Certificate in Library Science OR Diploma in Library Science OR Degree in Library & Information Science (सीनियर सैकण्डरी / 10+2 के साथ पुस्तकालय विज्ञान में प्रमाण-पत्र या पुस्तकालय एवं सूचना विज्ञान में डिप्लोमा / स्नातक). (ii) Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture (देवनागरी लिपि में लिखी हिंदी में कार्य करने का ज्ञान एवं राजस्थान की संस्कृति का ज्ञान). [Note: RSCIT is not mandatory for this post].
- SOURCE: Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) Official Recruitment Advertisement for Librarian Grade-III (Advt No. 07/2022 & 2024/2025 notifications), RSSB Jaipur. URL: https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/ (Official Document PDF: https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/storage/news_item/1734001649.pdf)
- CROSS-CHECK: RSSB Official Advertisements Portal: https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/advertisements and RSMSSB Portal: https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in — Both confirm that 12th Pass (Senior Secondary) with a Certificate in Library Science is eligible.
- CONFLICT HISTORY: Disagreement existed over whether a candidate must hold a full Graduate Degree (BLIS or B.Lib) to apply for Rajasthan Librarian Grade-3. Official RSSB recruitment rules confirm that Senior Secondary (12th Pass) along with a Certificate in Library Science (C.Lib.Sc.) meets the minimum educational qualification, so graduation is not required.
