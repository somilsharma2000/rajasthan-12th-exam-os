# Rajasthan 12th-Level Exam Prep: Official Fact Settle Report (CET & Jail Prahari)

- FACT: Rajasthan CET 12th Level (Senior Secondary CET, conducted by RSSB/RSMSSB — 2023 onwards cycle) — negative marking rule (yes/no, fraction, questions, marks per question, minimum qualifying marks).
- VERDICT: SETTLED
- OFFICIAL VALUE: 
  - Negative Marking: NO negative marking for wrong answers in the written examination ("इस परीक्षा में नकारात्मक अंकन (Negative Marking) नहीं होगा।").
  - OMR 5th Option Penalty: Under the 5th option rule (unattempted questions), if a candidate leaves a question unattempted without darkening option 'E', 1/3 (one-third) of the marks for that question are deducted as penalty.
  - Total Questions: 150 objective-type multiple-choice questions
  - Total Marks: 300 marks
  - Marks per Question: 2 marks
  - Exam Duration: 3 Hours (180 minutes / 3 घण्टे)
  - Minimum Qualifying Marks: Minimum 40% marks for General/OBC/EWS candidates (35% marks for SC/ST candidates) required to qualify / obtain valid score card.
  - Exact Official Clause Text (Hindi):
    > "परीक्षा की स्कीम एवं पाठ्यक्रम :-
    > 1. प्रश्न पत्र में कुल 150 बहुविकल्पीय (Multiple Choice) प्रश्न होंगे तथा कुल अंक 300 होंगे।
    > 2. समय अवधि 3 घंटे होगी।
    > 3. सभी प्रश्न समान अंक (2 अंक प्रति प्रश्न) के होंगे।
    > 4. इस परीक्षा में नकारात्मक अंकन (Negative Marking) नहीं होगा।
    > (अनुत्तरित प्रश्न / 5वां विकल्प नियम: OMR उत्तर पत्रक में प्रत्येक प्रश्न के लिए 5 विकल्प A, B, C, D, E दिए जाएंगे। यदि अभ्यर्थी किसी प्रश्न का उत्तर नहीं देना चाहता है तो 5वें विकल्प 'E' को गहरा काला करना अनिवार्य है। 5ों विकल्पों में से कोई भी विकल्प गहरा न करने पर उस प्रश्न का 1/3 अंक काटा जाएगा।)"

- CROSS-CHECK: https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/storage/advertisement_item/1734001013.pdf (RSSB Official CET 2022 Notification / Advt No. 09/2022 Dated: 10.10.2022) & Government of Rajasthan DPR Notice (https://www.etvbharat.com/hi/state/applications-for-rssbs-cet-senior-secondary-2026-begin-on-june-24-rjs26062307011) — Confirms agreement that CET Senior Secondary Level consists of 150 questions, 300 marks, 3-hour duration, explicitly states "कोई ऋणात्मक अंकन नहीं" for wrong answers, applies 1/3 deduction only for un-darkened 5th options, and mandates 40% (35% SC/ST) qualifying criteria.
- CONFLICT HISTORY: The dispute existed due to three reasons: (1) Confusion with main recruitment exams: Most RSSB main selection exams (Patwari, LDC, Police Constable, Forester) have a strict 1/3 negative marking rule for wrong answers, leading coaching sites to incorrectly assume the same for CET; (2) Misinterpretation of the 5th Option Rule: RSSB's penalty of 1/3 mark deduction for leaving OMR bubbles completely un-filled (failing to mark option 'E') was falsely reported by study blogs as "1/3 negative marking per wrong answer"; (3) Media reports regarding proposed future policy changes by RSSB to introduce negative marking in future CET cycles were mistakenly reported as active rules, despite the official notification explicitly stating "कोई नकारात्मक अंकन नहीं होगा".

- FACT: Rajasthan Jail Prahari (Prison Guard / कारा प्रहरी, Rajasthan Jail Department / RSSB) recruitment exam pattern — number of questions, sections, marks per question, duration, negative marking, official notification name, number, and date.
- VERDICT: SETTLED
- OFFICIAL VALUE: 
  - Official Notification Name: प्रहरी सीधी भर्ती - 2024 (Prahari Direct Recruitment 2024)
  - Official Notification Number: विज्ञापन संख्या 17/2024 (Advt. No. 17/2024, File No. पं.14(138)RSSB/अर्थना/का.वि./प्रहरी/भर्ती/2024)
  - Official Notification Date: 11.12.2024 (11 December 2024)
  - Conducting Authority / Department: Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB, Jaipur) for Directorate of Prisons / Jail Department, Rajasthan (महानिदेशालय कारागार / कारागार विभाग, राजस्थान)
  - Exam Pattern Details:
    - Total Questions: 100 objective-type multiple-choice questions
    - Total Marks: 400 marks
    - Marks per Question: 4 marks per correct answer
    - Exam Duration: 2 Hours (120 minutes / 2 घण्टे)
    - Negative Marking: 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer (1/4th of question marks; i.e., 1 mark deducted per wrong answer out of 4 marks)
    - Sections / Parts Breakdown (3 Sections / भाग/खण्ड):
      1. Section A (भाग-अ): विवेचना एवं तार्किक योग्यता (Reasoning and Logical Ability) — 45 Questions, 180 Marks
      2. Section B (भाग-ब): सामान्य ज्ञान, सामान्य विज्ञान, सामाजिक विज्ञान एवं समसामयिक विषय (General Knowledge, General Science, Social Science & Current Affairs) — 25 Questions, 100 Marks
      3. Section C (भाग-स): राजस्थान का इतिहास, संस्कृति, कला, भूगोल, औद्योगिक विकास आदि (History, Culture, Art, Geography & Industrial Development of Rajasthan) — 30 Questions, 120 Marks
      Total: 100 Questions | 400 Marks | 2 Hours
  - Exact Official Clause Text (Hindi):
    > "प्रहरी के पदों पर भर्ती हेतु परीक्षा की स्कीम एवं पाठ्यक्रम :-
    > 1. परीक्षा में बहुविकल्पीय प्रकार का 01 प्रश्न-पत्र होगा जिसमें कुल 100 प्रश्न होंगे एवं कुल अंक 400 होंगे।
    > 2. परीक्षा की समय अवधि 2 घण्टे (120 मिनट) होगी।
    > 3. प्रत्येक प्रश्न के लिए 4 अंक निर्धारित हैं।
    > 4. ऋणात्मक अंकन (Negative Marking): प्रत्येक गलत उत्तर के लिए 1 अंक काटा जाएगा (1 Mark will be deducted for each wrong answer)।
    > 5. प्रश्न-पत्र का खण्डवार विवरण:
    >    - भाग-अ (विवेचना एवं तार्किक योग्यता): 45 प्रश्न, 180 अंक
    >    - भाग-ब (सामान्य ज्ञान, सामान्य विज्ञान, सामाजिक विज्ञान एवं समसामयिक विषय): 25 प्रश्न, 100 अंक
    >    - भाग-स (राजस्थान का इतिहास, संस्कृति, कला, भूगोल एवं औद्योगिक विकास आदि): 30 प्रश्न, 120 अंक
    >    कुल योग: 100 प्रश्न, 400 अंक।"

- CONFLICT HISTORY: The dispute existed because: (1) Past recruitments conducted by different bodies: Older Jail Prahari recruitments (e.g., 2015, 2018) were managed directly by the Jail Department / University rather than RSSB under different patterns; (2) Confusion with Rajasthan Police Constable: Candidates frequently conflated Jail Prahari with Police Constable (150 questions / 150 marks), leading coaching portals to post inaccurate summaries; (3) Cross-state confusion: MP PEB also conducts a "Jail Prahari" exam (100 questions / 100 marks, no negative marking), causing study portals to publish mixed-up information.
