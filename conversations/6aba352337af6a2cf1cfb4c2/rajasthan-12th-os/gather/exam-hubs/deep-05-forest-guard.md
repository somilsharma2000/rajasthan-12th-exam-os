### 2. Pay Level & Remuneration
- **Pay Level:** Pay Matrix Level **L-4** under 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) Pay Structure.
- **Pay Scale / Band:** ₹5,200 – ₹20,200 with Grade Pay **₹1,900**.
- **Probation Period:** 2 Years.
- **Probationary Remuneration:** Fixed monthly stipend/remuneration as prescribed by the Government of Rajasthan for Level-4 posts (approx. ₹13,500/month basic fixed stipend during 2-year probation). Full Pay Scale + Allowances (DA, HRA, Hardship/Forest Allowance) applicable upon successful completion of probation.
- **Comparison Note:** Forester (Vanpal) is in Pay Level **L-8** (GP ₹2,800), whereas Forest Guard (Van Rakshak) is in Pay Level **L-4** (GP ₹1,900).

### 5. Selection Process & Stages
The selection process for Rajasthan Forest Guard (Van Rakshak) comprises four sequential stages:

1. **Stage 1: Written Examination (लिखित परीक्षा):**
   - Objective MCQ-based test of 100 marks to screen candidates.
   - Merit list created to shortlist candidates for Stage 2 in a ratio of **5 times (5x)** the number of advertised vacancies.
2. **Stage 2: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET / Walking Test) (शारीरिक मापदंड एवं दक्षता परीक्षण):**
   - Physical measurement (Height & Chest).
   - Walking endurance test (25 km for males / 16 km for females in 4 hours).
   - Event tests (Sit-ups, Cricket ball throw for males; Standing long jump, Shot put for females).
   - *Nature:* Qualifying only (No marks added to final merit).
3. **Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) (दस्तावेज़ सत्यापन):**
   - Document verification for candidates qualifying the PET/PST in a ratio of **1.5x to 2x** of final vacancies.
4. **Stage 4: Medical Examination (चिकित्सा परीक्षण):**
   - Conducted by a State Government Medical Board / Chief Medical & Health Officer (CMHO) to certify physical fitness for field forestry duty.

- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

| **Chest Normal (सीना सामान्य)** | **84 cm** | N/A | **84 cm** | N/A |
| **Chest Expansion (सीना फुलाव)** | **Minimum 5 cm expansion** (Min 89 cm expanded) | N/A | **Minimum 5 cm expansion** | N/A |

*Note on ST / Tribal Relaxations:* For candidates belonging to Scheduled Tribes (ST) and listed Hill Areas (Gorkhas, Garhwalis, Kumaonis), minimum height relaxed to 152 cm (Male) and 145 cm (Female) as per Forest Rules.

#### B. Physical Efficiency Test (PET - शारीरिक दक्षता परीक्षा)

The PET is **mandatory and qualifying in nature** (no marks are added to the written examination score, but failing any PET event leads to disqualification).

##### 1. Walking Test (पैदल चाल परीक्षण / Walk Test) — Mandatory for All
- **Male Candidates:** Must walk/cover a distance of **25 km within 4 Hours (240 Minutes)**.
- **Female Candidates:** Must walk/cover a distance of **16 km within 4 Hours (240 Minutes)**.
- *Testing Environment:* Usually conducted on designated road circuits or stadium tracks monitored via RFID chips/manual checkpoints by the Forest Department circle officers.

##### 2. Gender-Specific Physical Events (Qualifying)

**For Male Candidates:**
1. **Sit-ups (सीट-अप्स):** **25 repetitions within 1 Minute** (1 मिनट में 25 दोहराव). Legs bent at knees, hands behind head, complete repetition required.
2. **Cricket Ball Throw (क्रिकेट बॉल फेंक):** **55 Meters** distance throw (Standard Leather Cricket Ball).

**For Female Candidates:**
1. **Standing Long Jump (खड़ी लंबी कूद):** **1.35 Meters** minimum distance jump without run-up.
2. **Shot Put (गोला फेंक):** **4.5 Meters** distance throw with a **4 kg Shot Put**.

### 8. Detailed Subject-wise Syllabus

The written examination tests candidates across General Science, Mathematics, Daily Current Affairs, and Rajasthan-focused General Knowledge (Social Studies, Geography, History, Art & Culture, Forestry & Environment).

```
================================================================================
                    RSMSSB FOREST GUARD WRITTEN SYLLABUS SCHEME
================================================================================
  [100 Questions | 100 Marks | 2 Hours | 1/3 Negative Marking | 10th Standard]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Section A: Daily Current Affairs (राष्ट्रीय एवं राज्य समसामयिक घटनाएं)
   Section B: General Science (सामान्य विज्ञान - 10th Standard Everyday Science)
   Section C: Elementary Mathematics (गणित - 10th Standard Arithmetic & Geometry)
   Section D: Rajasthan Geography, Economy & Environment (भूगोल एवं पर्यावरण)
   Section E: Rajasthan History, Art, Culture & Heritage (इतिहास, कला एवं संस्कृति)
================================================================================
```

#### A. Daily Current Affairs (समसामयिक विषय)
- Major current events at State (Rajasthan), National, and International levels.
- Government schemes, public initiatives, state developmental programs.
- Key personalities, awards, honors, sports events, and appointments in Rajasthan.

#### B. General Science (सामान्य विज्ञान — Secondary 10th Level)
- **Physics:** Basic principles of Motion, Work, Energy, Power, Gravitation, Light (Reflection & Refraction), Sound, Electricity, Magnetism, Simple Machines.
- **Chemistry:** Matter, Elements, Compounds, Mixtures, Chemical Reactions, Acids, Bases & Salts, Metals & Non-Metals, Carbon & its Compounds, Daily life chemistry.
- **Biology & Environment:** Human Physiology, Digestive/Respiratory/Circulatory Systems, Human Diseases & Prevention, Vitamins & Nutrition, Plant Structure & Photosynthesis, Ecosystems, Biodiversity, Pollution & Environmental Conservation.

#### C. Elementary Mathematics (गणित — Secondary 10th Level)
- **Number System:** Natural numbers, Integers, LCM & HCF, Decimals, Fractions, Simplification.
- **Arithmetic:** Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Profit & Loss, Simple Interest & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Speed, Distance & Time, Mixtures & Alligations.
- **Mensuration & Geometry:** Perimeter, Area of 2D shapes (Triangles, Rectangles, Circles), Surface Area & Volume of 3D solids (Cubes, Cuboids, Cylinders, Spheres), Basic lines, angles, and triangles.
- **Statistics & Algebra:** Elementary algebra, linear equations in one/two variables, basic data representation (Mean, Median, Mode, Bar charts).

#### D. Rajasthan Geography, Economy, Forest & Environment (राजस्थान का भूगोल, अर्थव्यवस्था एवं वन)
- **Physical Divisions:** Arid Plain (Thar Desert), Aravalli Range, Eastern Plains, Hadoti Plateau.
- **Climate & Drainage:** Monsoon, Rainfall patterns, Perennial & Seasonal Rivers (Chambal, Banas, Luni, Mahi, Sabarmati), Lakes (Sambhar, Rajsamand, Jaisamand, etc.), Dams & Canal Irrigation (Indira Gandhi Canal Project / IGNPP).
- **Soils & Vegetation:** Soil types, Forest types in Rajasthan (Tropical Dry Deciduous, Thorn Forests, Sub-tropical Evergreen).
- **Forestry, Wildlife & Environment (Special Focus for Forest Guard):**
  - Forest Cover in Rajasthan (State Forest Report facts, forest density).
  - Protected Areas: National Parks (Ranthambore, Keoladeo Ghana, Mukundra Hills), Tiger Reserves (Ranthambore, Sariska, Mukundra, Ramgarh Vishdhari, Dholpur-Karauli), Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves.
  - State Symbols: State Tree (*Khejri*), State Flower (*Rohida*), State Animal (*Chinkara* & *Camel*), State Bird (*Godawan* / Great Indian Bustard).
  - Wildlife Conservation Laws, Forest Acts, Amrita Devi Bishnoi Movement & Bishnoi community conservation heritage.
- **Minerals & Agriculture:** Major metallic & non-metallic minerals, Kharif & Rabi crops, agro-climatic zones.

#### E. Rajasthan History, Art, Culture & Heritage (इतिहास, कला, संस्कृति एवं साहित्य)
- **Ancient Civilizations:** Kalibangan, Ahar, Ganeshwar, Bairath, Bagor.
- **Major Dynasties:** Guhilas/Sisodias of Mewar (Kumbha, Sanga, Pratap), Rathores of Marwar & Bikaner (Jodha, Maldeo, Rai Singh), Kachhwahas of Amber/Jaipur (Man Singh, Jai Singh I & II), Chauhans of Shakambhari/Ajmer/Ranthambore (Prithviraj III, Hammir).
- **Freedom Struggle & Integration:** 1857 Revolt in Rajasthan, Peasant (Kisan) Movements (Bijolia, Begun), Tribal Movements (Bhagat Movement, Govind Guru, Motilal Tejawat), Prajamandal Movements, Integration of Rajasthan (7 stages from 17 March 1948 to 1 November 1956).
- **Art, Culture & Architecture:**
  - Forts & Palaces (Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Mehrangarh, Amber, Junagarh, Ranthambore).
  - Temples, Stepwells (*Baoris*), Havelis, Cenotaphs (*Chhatris*).
  - Fairs & Festivals (Pushkar, Gangaur, Teej, Karni Mata Fair, Ramdevra).
  - Folk Deities & Saints (*Lok Devtas/Devis:* Ramdevji, Tejaji, Pabuji, Gogaji, Harbhuji, Devnarayanji, Karni Mata, Saint Pipaji, Jambhoji, Jasnathji).
  - Folk Dances & Music (Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Agni, Chari, Kacchi Ghodi, Khyal, Puppetry, Folk Instruments).
  - Paintings & Handicrafts (Mewar, Marwar, Kishangarh Bani Thani, Bundi schools; Blue Pottery, Phad painting, Usta art, Bandhej).
