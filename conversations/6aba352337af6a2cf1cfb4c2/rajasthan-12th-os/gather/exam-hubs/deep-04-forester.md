## 1. Recruiting Body & Statutory Framework

* **Primary Authority:** Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB), Jaipur (राजस्थान कर्मचारी चयन बोर्ड, जयपुर).
* **User Department:** Rajasthan Forest Department (वन विभाग, राजस्थान), Government of Rajasthan.
* **Governing Statutory Rules:**
  1. *Rajasthan Forest Subordinate Service Rules, 2015* (राजस्थान वन अधीनस्थ सेवा नियम, 2015 - as amended).
  2. *Rajasthan Scheduled Area Subordinate, Ministerial and Class IV Service (Recruitment and Other Conditions of Service) Rules, 2014* (for TSP / Scheduled Areas).
  3. *Rajasthan Common Eligibility Test Board Regulations, 2022 / 2024*.
* **Cadre Category:** Subordinate Forest Executive Cadre (Non-Gazetted).
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

## 2. Pay Matrix, Grade Pay & Financial Emoluments

* **Pay Matrix Level:** **Pay Level L-8** (पे मैट्रिक्स लेवल-8) under the 7th Rajasthan Revised Pay Rules, 2017.
* **Pre-Revised Pay Scale & Grade Pay:** Pay Band PB-1 (₹5,200 – ₹20,200) + Grade Pay **₹2,800**.
* **Probation Period:** 2 Years compulsory probation.
* **Probation Pay (Fixed Remuneration):** Fixed stipend of **₹18,500 per month** during the 2-year probation period. (No DA, HRA, or other state allowances payable during probation).
* **Post-Probation Salary:**
  * **Basic Pay (Level L-8 Cell 1):** Starts at **₹26,300 per month** (Scale Range: ₹26,300 – ₹85,500).
  * **Allowances:** Dearness Allowance (DA as applicable to Raj Govt employees, e.g., 50%+), House Rent Allowance (HRA: 9% / 18% / 27% based on posting city Y/Z class), Special Forest Duty Allowance / Uniform Allowance, Travelling Allowance (TA), and State Insurance / NPS / RGHS Medical Cover.
* **Note on Commercial Coaching Errors:** Note that central job sites (such as Testbook tables) sometimes erroneously list Level 8 as Grade Pay ₹4,800 (which is the Central Govt 7th CPC definition). In Rajasthan State 7th CPC rules, Level L-8 is strictly **Grade Pay ₹2,800** (Basic ₹26,300).
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`  
  * *Primary Source:* RSSB Advt 01/2026 Page 1 & Page 9; Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017 Schedule.
  * *Secondary Source:* YojnaPortals Job & Salary Analysis, Testbook Salary Guide.

## 3. Educational Qualification & Mandatory Prerequisites

* **Essential Educational Qualification:**
  * **12th Pass (Senior Secondary / 10+2)** from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), Ajmer, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), or any other recognized Board.
  * **Stream Requirement:** **ANY STREAM** (Arts, Science, Commerce). *(Note: Unlike Forest Telecommunication, Science is NOT mandatory for Forester/Vanpal).*
* **Language & Cultural Proficiency:**
  * Knowledge of written Hindi in Devanagari script (देवनागरी लिपि में लिखित हिन्दी में कार्य करने का ज्ञान).
  * Knowledge of Rajasthan Culture & Heritage (राजस्थान की संस्कृति का ज्ञान).
* **Mandatory Gateway Screening Prerequisite:**
  * Must have appeared in and qualified the **RSMSSB Common Eligibility Test (CET) Senior Secondary Level (12th Level) 2024**.
  * Only candidates possessing a valid CET 12th Level score card and falling within the **15-times merit cutoff** of total advertised vacancies per category are eligible to submit the Forester Direct Recruitment application.
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`  
  * *Primary Source:* RSSB Advt 01/2026 Page 9 Section 6(i) & Page 1 Special Note.
  * *Secondary Source:* Adda247 Forester Qualification Guide, Jagranjosh Recruitment Alert, SarkariResultBihar.

## 4. Age Limits & Relaxation Norms

* **Age Calculation Anchor Date:** 1st January of the year following the notification (e.g., 01.01.2026 for 2026 recruitment).
* **Standard Age Range:**
  * **Minimum Age:** **18 Years**
  * **Maximum Age:** **40 Years** (Revised upper limit extended by Rajasthan Government from 28/35 to 40 years).
* **Upper Age Relaxations (Category-wise for Rajasthan Domicile):**
  * **Male Candidates (SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS):** **+5 Years** relaxation (Upper limit: 45 years).
  * **Female Candidates (General / Unreserved):** **+5 Years** relaxation (Upper limit: 45 years).
  * **Female Candidates (SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS):** **+10 Years** relaxation (Upper limit: 50 years).
  * **Ex-Servicemen (ESM):** Up to **15 Years** relaxation (Maximum permissible age: 50 years; 55 years if lower post experience required).
  * **NCC Cadets / Instructors:** Period of NCC service deducted from age (up to 3 years relaxation).
  * **Widows & Divorced Women (विवाह-विछिन्न महिला):** **No Upper Age Limit** (eligible up to statutory superannuation age, e.g., 60 years).

## 5. Selection Process & Detailed Physical Standards

### 5.1 Selection Stages Overview
1. **Stage 1: CET Screening Shortlisting:** CET Senior Secondary 12th Level Score (15x candidates shortlisted per category).
2. **Stage 2: Written Examination:** 100 Marks objective MCQ test (OMR-based).
3. **Stage 3: Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET / Walking Test):** Qualifying stage conducted by Rajasthan Forest Department.
4. **Stage 4: Document Verification (DV):** Original credential checking & character verification.
5. **Stage 5: Medical Examination & Final Merit List:** Final selection based on Written Exam score subject to qualifying PST/PET and Medical fitness.

### 5.3 Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Walking Test (Foot March)

Physical tests are strictly **qualifying in nature** (no marks are added to written examination score).

1. **Walking Test / Foot March (पैदल चाल / वॉकिंग टेस्ट):**
   * **Male Candidates:** **25 Kilometres** to be completed within **4 Hours** (240 minutes).
   * **Female Candidates:** **16 Kilometres** to be completed within **4 Hours** (240 minutes).
   * *Venue & Conduct:* Organised on a measured track or road circuit under the supervision of the Forest Department Board.

2. **Physical Efficiency Events (विशेष शारीरिक दक्षता परीक्षण):**
   * **For Male Candidates:**
     * **Sit-ups (सिट-अप्स):** **25 Repetitions** in **1 Minute**.
     * **Cricket Ball Throw (क्रिकेट बॉल थ्रो):** **55 Metres** distance throw.
   * **For Female Candidates:**
     * **Standing Long Jump (स्टैंडिंग लॉन्ग जंप):** **1.35 Metres**.
     * **Shot Put / Spherical Shot Throw (शॉट पुट - 4 kg):** **4.5 Metres** distance throw.

## 6. Written Examination Pattern & Scheme

* **Exam Mode:** Offline OMR-Based Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
* **Question Paper Standard:** Senior Secondary Level (10+2 Standard).
* **Total Questions:** **100 Questions**
* **Total Marks:** **100 Marks** (1 mark per correct answer).
* **Duration:** **2 Hours (120 Minutes)** + 10 Minutes extra time allocated for verifying the 5th option bubble filling.
* **Negative Marking Scheme:** **1/3rd Mark (0.33 Marks)** deducted for each wrong answer.

### Mandatory 5-Option OMR Rule (5th Option 'E' Implementation)
* Each MCQ in the question paper features **5 options (A, B, C, D, E)**.
* If a candidate decides **not to attempt** a question, they MUST darken option **'E'** on the OMR sheet.
* If a candidate leaves all 5 options blank for **more than 10% of total questions** (>10 questions), the candidate is **disqualified** from the examination.
* If a candidate leaves options blank for **less than 10% of questions**, **1/3rd mark (0.33)** is deducted for every unattempted blank question.

## 7. Comprehensive Topic-Wise Syllabus Outline

### Module A: Rajasthan History, Art, Culture & Heritage (राजस्थान का इतिहास, कला एवं संस्कृति)
* **Ancient Civilizations:** Kalibangan, Ahar, Ganeshwar, Bairath, Nagari, and archaeological sites.
* **Dynasties & Rulers:** Mewar (Kumbha, Sanga, Pratap), Marwar (Maldeo, Chandrasen), Amber/Jaipur (Man Singh, Jai Singh), Rathores of Bikaner, Parmars, Chauhans, Hadas.
* **Modern History & Integration:** Revolution of 1857 in Rajasthan, Peasant & Tribal Movements (Bijolia, Begu, Bhil/Eki Movement), Praja Mandal Movements, and 7-stage Integration of Rajasthan.
* **Art & Architecture:** Forts, Palaces, Temples, Stepwells (Baoris), Haveli architecture; Miniature Painting schools (Mewar, Marwar, Kishangarh, Bundi, Jaipur).
* **Folk Culture:** Folk Music & Instruments (Tatt, Sushir, Avanaddh, Ghan), Folk Dances (Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Chari, Agni, Gair, Kachhi Ghodi), Folk Dramas ( ख्याल, स्वांग, तमाशा, रम्मत).
* **Folk Deities & Saints:** Panch Pirs (Pabu, Ramdev, Goga, Mehaj, Harbhu), Tejaji, Devnarayan, Jambhoji, Jasnathji, Dadu Dayal, Meera Bai, Laldassji.
* **Social Traditions:** Fairs & Festivals, Dresses, Ornaments (for men & women), Customs, Traditions, Dialects & Literature (Mwari, Marwari, Dhundhari, Mewati, Wagdi).

---

### Module B: Indian & Rajasthan Political System (भारतीय एवं राजस्थान की प्रशासनिक व्यवस्था)
* **Indian Constitution:** Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP), Fundamental Duties.
* **State Executive & Legislature:** Governor, Chief Minister, Council of Ministers, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly.
* **Judiciary & Commissions:** High Court of Rajasthan, RPSC, Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), State Election Commission, State Information Commission, State Human Rights Commission.
* **District Administration & Local Governance:** District Collector, Sub-Divisional Structure, Tehsildar, Panchayati Raj Institutions (Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad) & 73rd/74th Constitutional Amendment Acts.

---

### Module C: Geography of Rajasthan & Wildlife Resources (राजस्थान का भूगोल, वन एवं वन्यजीव)
* **Physical Profile:** Location, Extent, Boundaries, Administrative Setup, Physical Divisions (Aravalli Range, Western Desert, Eastern Plains, Hadoti Plateau).
* **Climate & Soils:** Climatic zones, Monsoons, Rainfall distribution, Drought & Desertification, Soil types and soil erosion.
* **Drainage & Water Resources:** Rivers (Chambal, Banas, Luni, Mahi, Sabarmati), Inland Lakes (Sambar, Rajsamand, Jaisamand), Major Canal & Irrigation Projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project - IGNP, Bisalpur, Jakham).
* **Forest & Wildlife (Core Domain):** Forest Classification (Reserved, Protected, Unclassed), Forest Density, Flora & Fauna, Wildlife Sanctuaries, National Parks (Ranthambore, Keoladeo Ghana, Sariska, Mukundra Hills), Desert National Park, Eco-Tourism, Tiger Reserve Projects, Conservation Reserves, Climate Change & Biodiversity.
* **Minerals & Energy:** Metallic & Non-Metallic Minerals, Energy Resources (Thermal, Solar, Wind, Biomass, Hydroelectric).

---

### Module D: Economy of Rajasthan (राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था)
* **Economic Overview:** Features of State Economy, State Income (GSDP), Budget concepts, Inflation, Unemployment.
* **Agriculture & Animal Husbandry:** Major Crops (Kharif/Rabi), Livestock Breeds (Cattle, Sheep, Goats, Camels), Dairy Development, Agro-industries.
* **Industries & Handicrafts:** Mineral-based industries, Textile, Cottage & Small Enterprises, Handicraft Products (Blue Pottery, Bandhej, Block Printing, Kota Doria).
* **Welfare Schemes & Institutions:** State Social Security Schemes, Free Medicine/Diagnostic Schemes, RIICO, RFC, RAJSICO, Rural Development Programs.

---

### Module E: Everyday Science & Information Technology (दैनिक विज्ञान एवं आईटी)
* **Physical & Chemical Concepts:** Physical and Chemical Changes, Metals, Non-Metals, Metalloids, Important Alloys and Compounds.
* **Optics & Energy:** Laws of Light, Reflection, Refraction, Lenses, Mirrors, Solar and Thermal Energy.
* **Genetics & Biology:** Basic Terminology of Genetics, Mendel's Laws, Human Body Systems (Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Excretory, Nervous Systems).
* **Health, Diseases & Environment:** Nutrition, Balanced Diet, Vitamins & Deficiency Diseases, Infectious & Non-Infectious Human Diseases, Waste Management, Ecosystem Dynamics.
* **Computers & Information Technology:** Fundamentals of Computers, Hardware & Software, Internet, Email, Cyber Safety Basics.

---

### Module F: Mathematics (प्रारंभिक गणित - 10th Standard)
* **Number Systems & Arithmetic:** LCM & HCF, Average, Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple Interest, Compound Interest.
* **Measurement & Geometry:** Time & Work, Work & Wages, Time, Speed & Distance, Area & Perimeter of Plane Figures (Triangles, Rectangles, Circles), Volume & Surface Area of Solids (Cylinders, Spheres, Cones, Cubes).
* **Data Interpretation:** Tables, Bar Graphs, Histograms, Pie Charts, Line Charts.

---

### Module G: Current Affairs (समसामयिकी)
* **Rajasthan State Current Events:** Major Government Policies, Flagship Schemes, Sports Personalities, Awards, Appointments, Cultural Festivals, Infrastructure Projects.
* **National & International Current Events:** Politics, Economy, Science & Technology, Environmental Summits, National Sports Events, National Personalities.
