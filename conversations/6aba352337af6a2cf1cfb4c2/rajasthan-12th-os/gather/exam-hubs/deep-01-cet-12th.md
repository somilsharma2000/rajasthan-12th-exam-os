### 1.2 Statutory Mandate & Purpose
The Rajasthan CET (Senior Secondary Level) was established under the *Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services (Common Eligibility Test) Rules, 2022*. It functions as a single, standardized screening test to filter candidates for direct recruitment into Level-5 to Level-8 subordinate and ministerial posts across state departments, replacing multiple preliminary examinations.

### 2.1 Educational Qualification
* **Essential Academic Criteria:** Must have passed **Senior Secondary (10+2 / 12th Class)** or equivalent examination from the Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), Ajmer, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), or any recognized State/Central Board.
* **Appearing Candidates Rule:** Candidates appearing in the final year of Senior Secondary (10+2) are eligible to apply for CET, provided they possess the required passing marksheets/certificates on or before the date of the written examination / document verification of the specific main recruitment.
* **Special Post Qualifications (Post-CET Stage):**
  * *For LDC / Junior Assistant:* Must possess RS-CIT (VMOU Kota), NIELIT O-Level, COPA, CS Diploma/Degree, or equivalent computer qualification prior to Mains exam / typing test.
  * *For Police Constable (Telecom):* Senior Secondary with Physics & Mathematics / Science stream.
  * *For Police Constable (Driver):* Valid LMV/HMV Driving License issued at least 1 year prior to the main recruitment notification.

### 2.2 Age Limits & Calculation Base Date
* **Age Calculation Anchor Date:** Age is calculated as of **January 1 of the year following the notification** (e.g., for 2024 notification, anchor date is 01-01-2025; for 2026 notification, anchor date is 01-01-2027).
* **Minimum Age:** **18 Years** (as of anchor date).
* **Maximum Age:** **40 Years** (as of anchor date).
* **3-Year Non-Recruitment Relaxation Provision:** Per DoP Notification dated 23-09-2008, if direct recruitment for a specific cadre was not conducted in previous years, candidates who were age-eligible during those missed years receive an age relaxation equal to the missed period, up to a maximum of **3 Years**.

## Field 3: Selection Stages, Shortlisting Rules & Score Validity

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Primary Source (Official):** DoP Notification No. F.14(215)RSSB/CET/2022 dated 23-05-2022 & DoP Gazette Notification dated 16-01-2025; RSSB CET Senior Secondary Examination Scheme.  
  *Official Portal:* [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** JagranJosh Exam Analysis & Prepp Rajasthan CET Shortlisting Rules Guide.  
  *Secondary Portals:* [https://www.jagranjosh.com](https://www.jagranjosh.com) | [https://prepp.in](https://prepp.in)

### 3.1 Two-Tier Selection Framework
1. **Stage 1 (Common Screening Gateway):** Common Eligibility Test (CET) Senior Secondary Level written examination conducted by RSSB.
2. **Stage 2 (Mains / PET / PST / Skill Tests):** Individual departmental main recruitment competitive exams (e.g., LDC Mains, Police Constable CBT/PET/PST, Forester Mains) conducted for shortlisted candidates.

### 3.2 The 15-Times (15x) Shortlisting Rule
* **Rule Definition:** Whenever a participating department releases advertised vacancies for any of the 7 subordinate posts, RSSB or the respective department calls candidates equal to **15 times (15x) the number of total advertised vacancies** per category for the Stage 2 examination/test.
* **Ranking Criterion:** Candidates who applied for the post are arranged in descending order of their normalized CET score. The score of the candidate at the 15x cutoff position becomes the category cutoff score. All candidates securing marks equal to or higher than this cutoff score are declared eligible for Stage 2.
* **Equal Marks Tie-Breaker:** All candidates who score marks equal to the 15x cutoff mark in their respective category are admitted to Stage 2, even if the total count exceeds 15 times.

### 3.3 CET Scorecard Validity Period
* **3-Year Scorecard Validity Policy:** Per DoP Notification dated **16-01-2025** (amending the original 1-year rule), the CET score declared by RSSB is **valid for 3 Years** from the official date of scorecard release on the SSO portal.
* **Score Improvement Feature:** Candidates are permitted to appear in subsequent CET Senior Secondary examinations to improve their performance. If a candidate holds multiple valid CET scorecards, their **highest valid score** will automatically be considered for shortlisting in upcoming main recruitment drives.
* **Attempt Cap:** There is **no limit** on the number of attempts for appearing in the Rajasthan CET.

### 4.1 Historical Evolution of Written Exam Pattern

| Parameter | CET 2022 & CET 2024 Scheme (Advt 10/2022 & 09/2024) | Revised CET 2026 Scheme (Advt 08/2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Questions** | **150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** | **120 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** |
| **Total Marks** | **300 Marks** (2 Marks per Question) | **240 Marks** (2 Marks per Question) |
| **Exam Duration** | **3 Hours (180 Minutes)** | **2 Hours 30 Minutes (150 Minutes)** |
| **Negative Marking** | **No Negative Marking** (0 Marks deducted) | **1/3rd (0.33) Deduction** per wrong answer |
| **OMR Options** | 5 Options (A, B, C, D, E) | 5 Options (A, B, C, D, E) |
| **Medium of Paper** | Bilingual (Hindi & English) | Bilingual (Hindi & English) |
| **Question Standard** | Senior Secondary Level (10+2 Standard) | Senior Secondary Level (10+2 Standard) |

### 4.2 OMR Sheet 5th Option 'E' Rule & Disqualification Clause
To ensure complete transparency and prevent post-exam OMR tampering, RSSB enforces the **Mandatory 5-Option OMR Rule**:
1. **5-Bubble Requirement:** Every question on the OMR sheet contains 5 options: `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, and `E`.
2. **Attempted Questions:** Candidates must darken one circle among `A`, `B`, `C`, or `D` using a **blue ballpoint pen** to select their answer.
3. **Unattempted Questions:** If a candidate chooses NOT to attempt a question, they MUST darken option **`E`** (indicating "Question Not Attempted").
4. **Penalty for Blank OMR Bubbles:** If a candidate leaves all 5 bubbles (`A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`) blank for any question, **1/3rd (0.33) of the total marks of that question will be deducted** as negative penalty.
5. **The >10% Disqualification Rule:** If a candidate leaves all 5 bubbles un-darkened for **more than 10% of total questions** (i.e., more than 15 questions in 150-Q paper or more than 12 questions in 120-Q paper), the candidate is **permanently DISQUALIFIED** from the exam, and their OMR sheet will not be evaluated.
6. **Extra 10 Minutes Allocation:** Candidates are given an **additional 10 minutes** over and above the scheduled exam time (e.g., 3 Hours + 10 Minutes) specifically to verify that every question has at least one circle darkened.

### Domain 1: History of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का इतिहास)
* **Ancient Civilizations & Archaeological Sites:** Kalibangan (कालीबंगा), Ahar (आहड़), Ganeshwar (गणेश्वर), Bairath (बैराठ), and Bagor (बागौर).
* **Prominent Dynasties, Rulers & Achievements:** Major achievements of prominent rulers of Mewar, Marwar, Amber/Jaipur, Bikaner, Bundi, and Chauhan dynasties of Ajmer & Ranthambore.
* **Freedom Movement & Political Awakening:**
  * The Revolt of 1857 in Rajasthan (6 military cantonments: Naseerabad, Neemuch, Erinpura, Deoli, Beawar, Kherwara; role of Thakur Kushal Singh of Auwa).
  * Peasant Movements (Bijolia, Begun, Bundi, Alwar, Neemuchana farmer revolts).
  * Tribal Movements (Bhagat Movement under Govind Guru, Ekki Movement under Motilal Tejawat).
  * Praja Mandal Movements (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Mewar, Bikaner, Kota, Marwar Praja Mandals).
* **Integration of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का एकीकरण):** 7 stages of political integration from Matsya Sangh (27 March 1948) to United Rajasthan (1 November 1956).
* **Prominent Historical Personalities:** Maharana Pratap, Sawai Jai Singh, Rao Jodha, Vijay Singh Pathik, Manikya Lal Verma, Arjun Lal Sethi, Kesari Singh Barhat, Gokulbhai Bhatt, Hiralal Shastri.

### Domain 2: Art, Culture, Literature, Traditions & Heritage of Rajasthan (राजस्थान की कला, संस्कृति, साहित्य एवं विरासत)
* **Architecture & Painting:**
  * *Forts & Palaces:* Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Mehrangarh, Amber, Junagarh, Ranthambore, Jaisalmer (Sonar Fort), Taragarh, Gagron.
  * *Temples & Havelis:* Dilwara Jain temples, Abhaneri Stepwell, Patwon ki Haveli, Nathmal ki Haveli.
  * *Schools of Painting (चित्रकला शैली):* Mewar, Marwar, Kishangarh (Bani Thani), Nathdwara, Dhundhar, Kota-Bundi styles.
* **Folk Music, Musical Instruments, Dance & Theatre:**
  * *Folk Performing Arts:* Lok Geet, Lok Natya (Khyal, Tamasha, Rammat, Raasleela, Nautanki).
  * *Musical Instruments:* Stringed (Tatt - Ravanhattha, Kamaycha), Wind (Sushir - Algoza, Shehnai), Percussion (Avanaddh - Chang, Dhak), Solid (Ghan - Manjira).
  * *Folk Dances:* Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Chari, Agni dance, Terah Taali, Kachhi Ghodi, Gair dance.
* **Religious Sects, Saints & Folk Deities (धार्मिक संप्रदाय, संत एवं लोक देवता/देवियां):**
  * *Folk Deities:* Ramdevji, Pabuji, Tejaji, Gogaji, Mehaji, Harbhuic, Mallinathji, Devnarayanji.
  * *Folk Goddesses:* Karni Mata, Sakambhari Mata, Sheetla Mata, Jean Mata, Tanot Mata.
  * *Saints & Sects:* Jambhoji (Bishnoi sect), Jasnathji, Dadu Dayal (Dadu Panth), Meerabai, Laldassji, Charan Dassji.
* **Social Life & Heritage:**
  * *Dresses & Ornaments:* Traditional male/female attire (Angrakhi, Pagri/Safa, Odhani, Ghaghra) & Ornaments (Borla, Rakhdi, Hansli, Timaniya, Kangan, Bichhiya).
  * *Fairs & Festivals:* Pushkar Fair, Desert Festival Jaisalmer, Gangaur, Teej, Karni Mata Fair, Baneshwar Dham Fair.
  * *Language, Dialects & Literature:* Rajasthani Language dialects (Marwari, Mewati, Jaipuri/Dhundhari, Malvi, Wagdi); Prominent literary works (Prithviraj Raso, Padmavat, Veli Krishan Rukmani Ri, Kanhadade Prabandh).

### Domain 3: Geography of India & Rajasthan (भारत एवं राजस्थान का भूगोल)
* **Geography of India:**
  * *Physical Features:* Major relief divisions — Himalayan Mountains, Peninsular Plateau, Thar Desert, Northern Plains, Coastal Plains & Islands.
  * *Drainage & Water Resources:* Major Himalayan & Peninsular river systems (Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri); Dams & major lakes.
  * *Wildlife & Forestry:* National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, Forest types and conservation.
  * *Disaster Management:* Floods, Droughts, Cyclones, Earthquakes, and Landslides.
* **Geography of Rajasthan:**
  * *Physical Divisions:* Western Sandy Plain (Thar), Aravalli Range & Hilly Region, Eastern Plains, South-Eastern Hadoti Plateau.
  * *Climate & Drainage:* Agro-climatic zones, Rainfall distribution, Monsoonal patterns; Rivers (Chambal, Banas, Luni, Mahi, Sabarmati, Banganga) & Inland lakes (Sambhar, Pachpadra, Rajsamand, Jaisamand, Fateh Sagar).
  * *Natural Vegetation & Soils:* Soil classification (Aridisols, Alfisols, Entisols, Inceptisols, Vertisols), Forest cover and wildlife reserves (Ranthambore, Sariska, Keoladeo Ghana, Mukundra Hills).
  * *Agriculture & Livestock:* Principal Kharif & Rabi crops; Animal husbandry, livestock breeds (Gir, Tharparkar, Rathi, Kankrej, Chokla sheep), dairy development.
  * *Minerals & Energy:* Metallic minerals (Zinc, Lead, Copper, Iron ore), Non-metallic minerals (Rock phosphate, Gypsum, Marble, Granite); Renewable energy (Solar, Wind, Biomass) & Non-renewable energy (Lignite coal, Petroleum, Hydroelectric).
  * *Demographics & Projects:* Population density, Growth rate, Sex ratio, Literacy (as per Census 2011); Major Irrigation Projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project - IGNP, Chambal Valley Project, Mahi Bajaj Sagar, Bisalpur Dam).

### Domain 4: Indian Political System with Special Reference to Rajasthan (भारतीय राजनीतिक व्यवस्था एवं राजस्थान का प्रशासनिक ढांचा)

### Domain 5: Economy of Rajasthan (राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था)
* **Economic Structure & Key Sectors:** Role of Agriculture, Industry, Livestock, and Mining sectors in State Gross Domestic Product (GSDP).

### 6.1 Multi-Shift Score Normalization Formula
Since the Rajasthan CET Senior Secondary exam is conducted across 6 distinct shifts over 3 days, varying difficulty levels across question paper sets are mathematically equalized using the **Standard Mean-Standard Deviation Normalization Formula**:

$$S_{ij} = ar{M}_{g}^t + rac{M_{ij} - M_{i}^{avg}}{\sigma_i} 	imes \sigma_g$$

**Where:**
* $S_{ij}$ = Normalized score assigned to candidate $j$ in shift $i$.
* $M_{ij}$ = Raw score obtained by candidate $j$ in shift $i$.
* $M_{i}^{avg}$ = Arithmetic mean (average) of raw marks scored by all candidates in shift $i$.
* $\sigma_i$ = Standard deviation of raw marks of candidates in shift $i$.
* $ar{M}_{g}^t$ = Target mean score computed from the top **0.1%** of candidates across all combined shifts.
* $\sigma_g$ = Standard deviation of raw marks of the top candidates across all combined shifts.

### 10.1 Scorecard vs. Cut-off Methodology Explanation
Because the Rajasthan CET is a qualifying screening examination, RSSB **does not publish a single fixed "CET passing cutoff"** for the exam itself. Instead:

2. Whenever a target recruitment drive is announced (e.g., LDC 4,197 posts), RSSB computes and releases the **Official 15x CET Cutoff Score** required for that specific post/category!

## Conclusion & Strategic Takeaways for Prep OS
1. **Product Centerpiece Position:** CET 12th Level dictates entry to 7 subordinate cadres. Preparation content must emphasize high-weightage sections: **Rajasthan History & Art/Culture**, **Rajasthan Geography**, **Everyday Science**, and **Quantitative Aptitude & Vedic Maths**.
2. **OMR 5th Option Mastery:** Mock test engines must incorporate the mandatory 5th Option 'E' rule with a 10-minute timer buffer and automated negative marking penalties for blank bubbles.
3. **Score Benchmark Target:** For maximum safety across low-vacancy cadres (Hostel Supt / Forester), mock target scores should be anchored at **180+ marks out of 300** (~60%+), while for high-vacancy cadres (LDC / Police Constable), a benchmark score of **155+ marks** (~52%+) guarantees Stage 2 15x selection.
