### Field 3: Educational Qualification & Prerequisites
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Primary Source (Official):** RSSB Advt No. 06/2024 Clause 6 (पात्रता एवं शैक्षणिक योग्यता).
* **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** Adda247 / Career Power RSMSSB LDC Eligibility Criteria Guide (`https://www.adda247.com/jobs/rsmssb-ldc-eligibility-criteria/`).
* **Academic Qualification:**
  * Senior Secondary (10+2 / 12th Class Pass) from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), CBSE, or any recognized Board in India.
  * **OR** Equivalent examination recognized by the Government of Rajasthan.
* **Computer Qualification (Any ONE of the following certificates/degrees is compulsory):**
  1. "O" Level or Higher Level Certificate Course conducted by DOEACC / NIELIT; **OR**
  2. COPA (Computer Operator & Programming Assistant) / DPCS (Data Preparation and Computer Software) Certificate under NCVT / SCVT; **OR**
  3. Diploma in Computer Science / Computer Applications from a university established by law in India or from an institution recognized by the Government; **OR**
  4. **RS-CIT** (Rajasthan State Certificate Course in Information Technology) conducted by Vardhaman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU) Kota & Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL); **OR**
  5. Senior Secondary School Certificate with Computer Science / Computer Application as one of the subjects; **OR**
  6. Any higher degree/diploma in Computer Science/IT (B.Sc CS/IT, BCA, MCA, BE/B.Tech CS/IT).
* **Language & Culture Requirement:** Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan culture (देवनागरी लिपि में लिखी हिंदी में कार्य करने का ज्ञान एवं राजस्थान की संस्कृति का ज्ञान).
* **Mandatory Screening Prerequisite:** Qualification in **RSMSSB CET 12th Level (Senior Secondary CET)**. Candidates are shortlisted for LDC Mains in a 1:15 ratio (15 times total advertised vacancies in each category) based on CET scores.

* **Maximum Age (General / Unreserved Male):** 40 Years.
* **Category-Wise Age Relaxations:**

  * Female Candidates of General / Unreserved Category: **5 Years relaxation** (Upper limit = 45 years).
  * Female Candidates of SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS belonging to Rajasthan: **10 Years relaxation** (Upper limit = 50 years).

  * Special One-Time Age Relaxation Clause: **3 Years additional upper age relaxation** granted as a 1-time measure because LDC recruitment was not conducted in preceding recruitment years.

### Field 5: Selection Stages & Typing Test Details
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Primary Source (Official):** RSSB LDC Direct Recruitment Scheme of Examination & Syllabus PDF & Advt 06/2024 Clause 13 (`https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in`).
* **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** TypingWire & Jagran Josh Phase-II Typing Test Guide (`https://typingwire.wordpress.com/2026/07/27/rssb-ldc-hindi-typing-test/`).
* **Selection Process Workflow:**
  1. **Stage 1: Screening via CET 12th Level:** Top 15 times candidates per category called for Written Mains.
  2. **Stage 2: Phase-I Written Examination:** 2 Objective Papers, 200 Marks Total. Shortlists 3 times (1:3 ratio) candidates for Phase-II Typing Test.
  3. **Stage 3: Phase-II Computer Typing Speed & Efficiency Test:** 100 Marks Total.
  4. **Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) & Final Merit:** Merit generated out of **300 Marks Total** (200 Written + 100 Typing).

#### Phase-II Typing & Efficiency Test Breakdown (100 Marks):
| Test Component | Section | Duration | Maximum Marks | Minimum Qualifying Marks (36%) | Required Speed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hindi Typing Speed Test (गति परीक्षण)** | Hindi | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | **9 Marks** | **25 WPM** |
| **Hindi Efficiency Test (दक्षता परीक्षण)** | Hindi | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | **9 Marks** | - |
| **English Typing Speed Test (गति परीक्षण)** | English | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | **9 Marks** | **25 WPM** |
| **English Efficiency Test (दक्षता परीक्षण)** | English | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | **9 Marks** | - |
| **TOTAL Phase-II Assessment** | Combined | **40 Minutes** | **100 Marks** | **36 Marks Aggregate** | **25 WPM (Speed)** |

* **Typing Speed Requirements:**
  * Minimum **25 words per minute (wpm)** speed required in both Hindi and English typing tests.
* **Font & Keyboard Layout Details:**
  * **Hindi Typing Font:** **Kruti Dev 010** or **Devlys 010** (Keyboard layout options: Inscript & Remington Layouts).
  * **English Typing Font:** Standard System Font (Calibri / Arial).
* **Efficiency Test Scope & Software:**
  * Test is conducted on **Word Processing Software** (MS Word equivalent).
  * Evaluation includes practical formatting commands: paragraph formatting, line spacing, margins, tables, header/footer, font sizing, indentations, bullets & numbering, text alignment, and search/replace functions.
* **CRITICAL QUALIFYING RULE (36% / 9 Marks Threshold):**
  * A candidate **MUST score at least 36% marks in EACH of the 4 individual test components** (i.e. minimum **9 out of 25 marks** in Hindi Speed, 9/25 in Hindi Efficiency, 9/25 in English Speed, 9/25 in English Efficiency).
  * If a candidate scores less than 9 marks in ANY single test out of the 4, they are **declared FAILED/DISQUALIFIED**, irrespective of their written exam score or total aggregate.
* **Scoring Nature:**
  * Phase-II typing marks (100 marks) ARE ADDED to Phase-I written marks (200 marks) to generate the final merit list out of **300 Marks Total**.
* **Exemption for Disabled / PwBD Candidates:**
  * Visually impaired candidates or physically disabled candidates who are unable to operate a computer keyboard are exempted from the Phase-II typing test.
  * Marks in Phase-II for exempted PwBD candidates are calculated proportionally based on their Phase-I written performance.

### Field 6: Written Exam Pattern (Phase-I)
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Primary Source (Official):** RSSB Scheme of Examination PDF & Advt No. 06/2024 (`https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in`).
* **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** SarkariExam & Prepp LDC Exam Pattern (`https://prepp.in/rsmssb-ldc-exam/pattern`).
* **Mode of Examination:** Offline OMR-based Objective Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) Examination. Conducted on a single day in 2 sessions.

#### Paper 1: General Knowledge, Everyday Science & Mathematics
* **Duration:** 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
* **Total Questions:** 150 Questions
* **Total Marks:** 100 Marks (Each question carries 2/3 = 0.666 marks)
* **Section Breakdown:**
  1. *General Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Art, Culture, Geography, Polity, Economy):* ~50 Questions (~33.33 Marks)
  2. *Everyday Science (दैनिक विज्ञान):* ~50 Questions (~33.33 Marks)
  3. *Mathematics (गणित):* ~50 Questions (~33.33 Marks)

#### Paper 2: General Hindi & General English
* **Duration:** 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
* **Total Questions:** 150 Questions
* **Total Marks:** 100 Marks (Each question carries 2/3 = 0.666 marks)
* **Section Breakdown:**
  1. *General Hindi (सामान्य हिन्दी):* 75 Questions (50 Marks)
  2. *General English (General English):* 75 Questions (50 Marks)

#### Marking & Negative Marking Scheme:
* **Positive Marking:** +0.666 marks (+2/3) per correct answer.
* **Negative Marking:** **1/3rd (0.33) of the marks assigned to that question** is deducted for every incorrect response (i.e. -0.222 marks per wrong answer).
* **Mandatory 5th Option Rule (Option 'E'):**
  * OMR sheet contains 5 options (A, B, C, D, E) per question.
  * If a candidate does not wish to attempt a question, option 'E' MUST be darkened.
  * Unattempted questions without darkening option 'E' suffer a penalty of **1/3rd mark deduction**.
  * If more than **10% of total questions (i.e. >15 questions)** are left unattempted without darkening option 'E', the candidate is **permanently DISQUALIFIED**.
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** Minimum **40% marks in Paper 1 AND 40% marks in Paper 2 individually** (35% for SC/ST candidates as per board regulations).

### Field 7: Detailed Syllabus Outline

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Primary Source (Official):** RSSB Official Syllabus PDF (`https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in`).
* **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** Result91 & PYQP.in Syllabus Guide (`https://www.pyqp.in/rsmssb-ldc-syllabus/`).

#### Paper 1 Detailed Syllabus:
1. **General Knowledge (Rajasthan Reference):**
   * *Current Affairs:* Major national & state level events, issues, organizations.
   * *Geography & Natural Resources:* Physical features, climate, soil, rivers, lakes, forests, wildlife, minerals, agriculture, livestock.
   * *History & Culture:* Ancient civilizations, major dynasties, freedom struggle, integration of Rajasthan, folk arts, music, dance, fairs, festivals, costumes, ornaments, saints, deities, historical monuments.
   * *Economy:* Agricultural development, industries, handicrafts, state development schemes.
   * *Administrative System:* Governor, Chief Minister, Legislative Assembly, High Court, RPSC, State Election Commission, Human Rights Commission, Chief Secretary, District Administration, Panchayati Raj.
2. **Everyday Science (दैनिक विज्ञान):**
   * Physical & Chemical changes, Oxidation & Reduction, Catalysts, Metals & Non-metals, Important Compounds.
   * Carbon & its compounds, Hydrocarbons, Allotropes, CFCs, Polymers, Soap & Detergents.
   * Reflection of Light, Laws of Reflection, Lenses, Human Eye & Vision Defects.
   * Electricity: Electric Current, Ohm's Law, Resistors, Combination of Resistors, Electric Generator & Motor, House Wiring.
   * Space & Information Technology: ISRO space programs, Remote Sensing, IT basics.
   * Environmental Studies: Ecosystem, Food Chain, Biogeochemical Cycles, Environmental Pollution, Bio-technology, Transgenic Organisms.
   * Biology & Human Health: Cell structure, Blood Groups, RH Factor, Pathogens & Diseases, Malnutrition & Human Health, Immunization.
3. **Mathematics (गणित):**
   * *Vedic Mathematics:* Square, Cube, Square Root, Cube Root up to 6 digits.
   * *Algebra:* Factors, Linear equations in 2 variables, Quadratic equations, Logarithms.
   * *Commercial Maths:* Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Discount.
   * *Geometry & Mensuration:* Lines & Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Circle & Circumference, Area & Perimeter, Volume & Surface Area of Sphere, Cone, Cylinder, Cube, Cuboid.
   * *Trigonometry:* Trigonometric ratios, Identities, Heights & Distances.
   * *Statistics:* Data representation, Frequency distribution, Mean, Median, Mode, Measures of Central Tendency & Dispersion, Probability.

#### Paper 2 Detailed Syllabus:
1. **General Hindi (75 Questions / 50 Marks):**
   * संधि और संधि विच्छेद
   * सामासिक पदों की रचना और समास विग्रह
   * उपसर्ग एवं प्रत्यय
   * पर्यायवाची शब्द एवं विलोम शब्द
   * अनेकार्थक शब्द एवं शब्द युग्म
   * संज्ञा शब्दों से विशेषण बनाना
   * शब्द शुद्धि एवं वाक्य शुद्धि
   * वाच्य: कर्तृवाच्य, कर्मवाच्य और भाववाच्य प्रयोग
   * क्रिया: सकर्मक, अकर्मक और पूर्वकालिक क्रियाएँ
   * वाक्यांश के लिए एक सार्थक शब्द
   * मुहावरे और लोकोक्तियाँ
   * अंग्रेजी के पारिभाषिक (प्रशासनिक) शब्दों के समानार्थक हिंदी शब्द
   * सरल, संयुक्त और मिश्र अंग्रेजी वाक्यों का हिंदी में रूपांतरण एवं हिंदी वाक्यों का अंग्रेजी में रूपांतरण
   * कार्यालयी पत्रों से संबंधित ज्ञान (Circulars, Notices, Tender, Memo, Demi-Official Letters).
2. **General English (75 Questions / 50 Marks):**
   * Tenses / Sequence of Tenses
   * Voice: Active and Passive
   * Narration: Direct and Indirect
   * Transformation of Sentences (Assertive, Negative, Interrogative, Exclamatory)
   * Use of Articles and Determiners
   * Use of Prepositions
   * Translation of Simple Sentences from Hindi to English and vice-versa
   * Correction of Sentences (Subject-verb agreement, Degrees of adjectives, Connectives)
   * Glossary of official, technical terms (with Hindi equivalents)
   * Synonyms and Antonyms
   * One word substitution
   * Prefix and Suffix word formation
   * Confusable words
   * Comprehension of Unseen Passage
   * Knowledge of official letter writing (Official, Demi-Official, Circulars, Notices, Tenders).
