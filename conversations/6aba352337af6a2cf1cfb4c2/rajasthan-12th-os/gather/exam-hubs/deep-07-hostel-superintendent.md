## 1. Executive Summary & Framework Architecture

In Rajasthan state government recruitments, the role of **Hostel Superintendent (छात्रावास अधीक्षक - Chhatravas Adhyaksh)** has evolved significantly over the past decade. Candidates and prep platforms frequently confuse the two active streams and historical recruitments. This deep survey provides absolute clarity:

1. **Hostel Superintendent (Minority Affairs Department) - 12th-Pass Cadre (Advt. No. 04/2024):**  
   * **Target Qualification:** 12th Pass (Senior Secondary) + Computer Certificate (RSCIT / O-Level / COPA / CS Diploma).  
   * **Gateway Screening:** **RSMSSB CET 12th Level (Senior Secondary CET)**.  
   * **Pay Matrix:** **Level L-5** (Grade Pay ₹2400).  
   * **App Scope:** **IN SCOPE** for the Rajasthan 12th-Pass Exam OS Project.

2. **Hostel Superintendent Grade-II (Social Justice & Empowerment Department - SJED) - Graduate Cadre (Advt. No. 02/2024):**  
   * **Target Qualification:** Bachelor's Degree (Graduation in any discipline).  
   * **Gateway Screening:** **RSMSSB CET Graduate Level**.  
   * **Pay Matrix:** **Level L-8** (Grade Pay ₹2800).  
   * **App Scope:** Secondary / Comparative reference (Graduate-level entry).

3. **Historical Panchayati Raj / Housing Board Status Verification:**  
   * Historically (e.g., Joint Exam 2016, Advt. No. 08/2016), Hostel Superintendent Grade-II was recruited under the **Panchayati Raj Department** alongside Gram Sevak (VDO) by RSMSSB.  
   * **Current Portal & Authority:** Under the **Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services Common Eligibility Test Rules, 2022**, all direct recruitments for non-gazetted hostel superintendents in both Minority Affairs and Social Justice Departments are **100% centralized under RSMSSB (RSSB)** via the CET framework. Neither the Panchayati Raj Department nor the Rajasthan Housing Board conducts independent recruitment exams for this post anymore.

## 3. Recruiting Body & Cadre Verification

### Current Recruiting Authority
* **Board:** Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB), State Institute of Agriculture Management premises, Durgapura, Jaipur.
* **Official Website:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Cadre Department 1 (12th Pass):** Rajasthan Minority Affairs Subordinate Service (Alpashankhyak Mamle Vibhag - अल्पसंख्यक मामलात विभाग).

### Historical Context & Departmental Transition Verification
* **2016 Recruitment:** Conducted by RSMSSB under Advt. No. 08/2016 for 300 posts of Hostel Superintendent Grade-II alongside Gram Sevak under the *Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Subordinate Service Rules, 1998*.
* **Current Status:** Following the notification of the *Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services Common Eligibility Test Rules, 2022*, all direct recruitment powers for Hostel Superintendent posts across all state departments were transferred to RSSB under the CET framework. Neither the **Panchayati Raj Department** nor the **Rajasthan Housing Board** maintains independent exam powers for Hostel Superintendent posts. All fresh non-gazetted vacancies are routed through RSMSSB CET screening.

## 5. Educational Qualification & Mandatory Certificates

### 12th-Pass Post: Hostel Superintendent (Minority Affairs Dept) - Advt. No. 04/2024
To be eligible for this 12th-level post, candidates must meet **ALL THREE** eligibility criteria:

1. **Academic Qualification:**  
   * Senior Secondary (10+2 / 12th Pass) from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), CBSE, or any other recognized Board of Secondary Education in India.

2. **Mandatory Computer Qualification (Must possess AT LEAST ONE of the following):**  
   * **Higher Level Certificate Course ('O' Level or higher)** conducted by DOEACC / NIELIT under the Department of Electronics, Govt. of India.  
   * **COPA (Computer Operator & Programming Assistant) / DPCS (Data Preparation & Computer Software)** certificate under National/State Council for Vocational Training (NCVT/SCVT).  
   * **Degree / Diploma in Computer Science / Computer Applications / IT** from any recognized University established by law in India.  
   * **Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering** from a Polytechnic institution recognized by the Government.  
   * **RSCIT (Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology)** conducted by Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU Kota) and Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL).

3. **Mandatory Prerequisite Gateway Test:**  
   * Candidate **MUST HAVE QUALIFIED CET 12th Level (Senior Secondary CET)** conducted by RSMSSB and hold a valid CET score card.

## 7. Selection Process & CET Shortlisting Rules

The selection process consists of three distinct stages:

```
[Stage 1: CET 12th Level Screening]
       │
       ▼ (Shortlisting top 15x vacancies per category)
[Stage 2: Mains Direct Recruitment Written Exam]
       │ (100 MCQs, 100 Marks, 2 Hours, 1/3rd Negative Marking)
       ▼ (1.5x candidates called for verification)
[Stage 3: Document Verification & Medical Examination]
       │
       ▼
[Final Appointment & Departmental Allotment]
```

### CET 15x Shortlisting Formula
* RSMSSB shortlists candidates for the Mains Examination based on their **CET 12th Level Percentile Score**.  
* The total number of shortlisted candidates equals **15 times the total advertised vacancies** category-wise.  
* All candidates obtaining the same cutoff marks as the last shortlisted candidate in 15x list are called for Mains.

## 8. Written Examination Scheme & Question Paper Pattern

### Scheme of Examination (Mains Written Test)
* **Exam Mode:** Offline Pen-Paper Mode (OMR Sheet) or CBT (Computer-Based Test).
* **Format:** Single Objective Paper (Multiple Choice Questions).
* **Number of Questions:** 100 MCQs
* **Total Marks:** 100 Marks (1 Mark per question)
* **Exam Duration:** 2 Hours (120 minutes)
* **Language of Paper:** Bilingual (Hindi & English)

### Evaluation & Marking Rules
* **Correct Answer:** +1 Mark
* **Negative Marking:** **1/3rd Mark (0.33)** deducted for every incorrect response.
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** 40% Marks in aggregate (35% for SC/ST candidates of Rajasthan). Candidates scoring below 40% are ineligible for selection regardless of vacancy status.

### RSMSSB Mandatory 5th Option Rule (Option 'E')
* Every question on the OMR sheet features **5 Options (A, B, C, D, E)**.  
* If a candidate chooses not to attempt a question, they **MUST darken Option 'E'** (Unattempted Question).  
* **Penalty for Leaving Blank:** If a question is left completely unbubbled (neither A/B/C/D nor E), **1/3rd mark (0.33)** is deducted for that question.  
* **Disqualification Threshold:** If a candidate leaves **more than 10% questions (10+ questions)** without bubbling any of the 5 options, the candidate is **PERMANENTLY DISQUALIFIED** and their OMR sheet is rejected.  
* **Extra Time:** 10 extra minutes are provided at the end of the exam specifically to verify that option 'E' is marked for all unattempted questions.

### Module Breakdown & Topics

#### Section 1: General Hindi (सामान्य हिंदी) — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **संधि एवं संधि विच्छेद:** स्वर, व्यंजन एवं विसर्ग संधि।
* **समास:** सामासिक पदों की रचना एवं समास-विग्रह।
* **उपसर्ग एवं प्रत्यय:** सामान्य प्रयोग एवं शब्द रचना।
* **पर्यायवाची एवं विलोम शब्द:** मुख्य पर्यायवाची एवं विपरीतार्थक शब्द।
* **अनेकार्थक एवं युग्म शब्द:** शब्द-युग्मों का अर्थ-भेद।
* **शब्द शुद्धि एवं वाक्य शुद्धि:** अशुद्ध शब्दों एवं अशुद्ध वाक्यों का शोधन।
* **वाक्यांश के लिए एक शब्द:** पारिभाषिक एवं एकल शब्द।
* **मुहावरे एवं लोकोक्तियां:** अर्थ एवं वाक्य प्रयोग।

#### Section 2: General English — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **Tenses / Sequence of Tenses**
* **Voice:** Active and Passive Voice
* **Narration:** Direct and Indirect Speech
* **Use of Articles and Prepositions**
* **Correction of Sentences:** Agreement, Subject-Verb, Adjectives, Connectives.
* **Glossary of Official & Technical Terms** (with Hindi equivalents)
* **Synonyms, Antonyms & One-word Substitutions**
* **Forming new words by using prefixes and suffixes**

#### Section 3: Mathematics / Quantitative Aptitude (10th Standard) — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **Vedic Mathematics:** Square, Square root, Cube, Cube root (up to 6 digits).
* **Factors & Equations:** Factorization, Polynomials, Linear equations in two variables.
* **Arithmetic Applications:** Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Discount, Average.
* **Geometry & Mensuration:** Angles & Triangles, Area of Plane Figures, Surface Area and Volume of Sphere, Cone, Cylinder, Cube, Cuboid.
* **Trigonometry & Statistics:** Basic Trigonometric Ratios, Heights & Distances, Mean, Mode, Median, Data Presentation (Bar & Pie Charts).

#### Section 4: Computer Knowledge — 10 Qs / 10 Marks
* **Characteristics & Architecture of Computers:** CPU, RAM, ROM, Input/Output Devices, Memory Storage.
* **Operating Systems:** Windows OS, File Management, Shortcuts, Settings.
* **MS Office Applications:** MS Word (Document creation, formatting), MS Excel (Spreadsheets, Formulas, Functions), MS PowerPoint (Presentations).
* **Internet & Web Services:** Web Browsers, E-mail, Search Engines, Online Portal usage.
* **Cyber Security Basics:** Passwords, Malware, Antivirus, Digital Signature, Rajasthan IT initiatives.

#### Section 5: Rajasthan History, Art, Culture & Heritage — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **Ancient Civilizations of Rajasthan:** Kalibangan, Ahar, Ganeshwar, Bairath.
* **Major Historical Dynasties:** Guhil, Rathore, Chauhan, Kachhwaha dynasties and their notable rulers.
* **Freedom Movement & Political Awakening:** Peasant and Tribal Movements (Bijolia, Eki), Prajamandal Movements, Integration of Rajasthan.
* **Art, Architecture & Forts:** Major Forts, Palaces, Havelis, Temples, Cenotaphs (Chhatris).
* **Fairs, Festivals, Fairs & Folk Culture:** Folk Deities (Lok Devta/Deviyan - Pabuji, Tejaji, Ramdevji), Folk Saints, Fairs & Festivals of Rajasthan.
* **Folk Arts, Music & Dance:** Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Chari, Agni dance; Musical instruments and Miniature painting styles (Marwar, Mewar, Kishangarh, Dhundhar).

#### Section 6: Rajasthan Geography & Natural Resources — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **Physical Features & Divisions:** Thar Desert, Aravalli Range, Eastern Plains, Hadoti Plateau.
* **Climate & Drainage System:** Rainfall distribution, Droughts, Rivers, Lakes, Major Canal Projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project - IGNP).
* **Soil & Vegetation:** Soil types, Soil erosion, Forest cover, Wildlife Sanctuaries & National Parks.
* **Mineral & Energy Resources:** Metallic and Non-metallic minerals; Conventional & Renewable energy (Solar, Wind, Biomass).
* **Agriculture & Livestock:** Major crops, Agro-climatic zones, Livestock census, Dairy development in Rajasthan.

#### Section 7: Rajasthan Administrative System & Major Welfare Schemes — 15 Qs / 15 Marks
* **Administrative Structure:** Governor, Chief Minister, Council of Ministers, Chief Secretary, State Secretariat.
* **Local Self-Government:** Panchayati Raj Institutions (73rd Amendment) and Urban Local Bodies (74th Amendment) in Rajasthan.
* **State Commissions:** Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC), State Human Rights Commission, State Election Commission, State Information Commission.
* **District Administration:** Role of Collector, SDM, Tehsildar, Block Development Officer (BDO).
* **State Social Welfare Schemes:** Flagship schemes of Rajasthan Govt, Welfare schemes for Minorities, SC/ST, Women, Elderly, Hostel management rules & student stipend policies.
