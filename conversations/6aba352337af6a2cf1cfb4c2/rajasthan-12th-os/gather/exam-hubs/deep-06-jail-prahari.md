## 2. Pay Matrix, Grade Pay & Financial Emoluments

* **Pay Matrix Level:** **Pay Level L-5** (पे मैट्रिक्स लेवल-5) under the 7th Rajasthan Revised Pay Rules, 2017.
* **Pre-Revised Pay Scale & Grade Pay:** Pay Band PB-1 (₹5,200 – ₹20,200) + Grade Pay **₹2,400**.
* **Probation Period:** 2 Years compulsory probation.
* **Probation Pay (Fixed Remuneration):** Fixed stipend of **₹14,600 per month** during the 2-year probation period. (No DA, HRA, or state allowances payable during probation except medical cover/RGHS).
* **Post-Probation Salary:**
  * **Basic Pay (Level L-5 Cell 1):** Starts at **₹20,800 per month** (Scale Range: ₹20,800 – ₹65,900).
  * **Allowances:** Dearness Allowance (DA as applicable to Raj Govt employees, e.g., 50%+), House Rent Allowance (HRA: 9% / 18% / 27% based on posting city Y/Z class), Uniform Allowance, Hardship/Prison Allowance, State Insurance (SI), General Provident Fund (GPF/NPS), and RGHS Medical Cover.
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

  * **Senior Secondary (10+2 / 12th Pass)** from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), Ajmer, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), or any other recognized Board.

## 5. Selection Process, PST & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

### 5.1 Selection Stages Overview
1. **Stage 1: CET Gateway Screening:** Common Eligibility Test (Senior Secondary Level) Score shortlisting (15x candidates shortlisted category-wise).
2. **Stage 2: Written Examination (Mains Objective MCQ):** 100 Questions, 400 Marks, 2 Hours duration.
3. **Stage 3: Physical Standards Test (PST / Measurement):** Height, Chest, and Weight verification.
4. **Stage 4: Physical Efficiency Test (PET - 100 Marks):** 5 KM Running test (Marks evaluated based on completion timing).
5. **Stage 5: Document Verification (DV) & Medical Board Examination:** Verification of original documents and final medical fitness check.

### 5.3 Physical Efficiency Test (PET - 100 Marks Allocation)

The Physical Efficiency Test carries **100 Marks** in total (or evaluated on a time-based merit score system). Candidates must qualify the PST before appearing in PET.

#### Male Candidates Event: 5 KM Running (5 किलोमीटर दौड़)
* **Maximum Permissible Time:** 25 Minutes.
* **Timing & Mark Breakdown:**
  * **Up to 20 Minutes (20 मिनट तक):** **100 Marks** (Maximum / Top Tier Marks)
  * **20 Min 1 Sec to 22 Minutes (20:01 से 22 मिनट तक):** Mid-tier marks (~80 Marks)
  * **22 Min 1 Sec to 25 Minutes (22:01 से 25 मिनट तक):** Base qualifying marks (~60 Marks)
  * **More than 25 Minutes (> 25 मिनट):** **Disqualified** (Fail)

## 6. Exact Written Exam Scheme & Pattern (Deep Analysis)

> **VERIFICATION CRITICALITY:** This field is identified as the weakest verified fact in secondary coaching materials (which frequently confuse Rajasthan Jail Prahari's 400-mark scheme with MP Jail Prahari's 100-mark scheme or Police Constable's 150-question scheme). The facts below are verified directly from RSSB's official scheme notice and Jail Department Standing Order No. 01.

### 6.1 Written Examination Overview
* **Mode of Examination:** Offline OMR-based Objective Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) Examination.
* **Total Number of Questions:** **100 MCQs**
* **Total Maximum Marks:** **400 Marks**
* **Marks Per Question:** **4 Marks** per correct answer.
* **Duration of Examination:** **2 Hours (120 Minutes)**.
* **Negative Marking Rule:** **1 Mark deducted for each wrong answer** (1/4th or 25% negative marking of question weightage).
* **Medium of Question Paper:** Bilingual (Hindi and English).
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks:**
  * **General / OBC / EWS / MBC Candidates:** **40% Marks** (160 Marks out of 400).
  * **SC / ST Candidates:** **36% Marks** (144 Marks out of 400).

### 6.3 Key Structural Rules & Mechanics
1. **Equal Question Value:** All 100 questions carry uniform weightage of 4 marks each.
2. **Fifth Option (5th Bubble Rule - OMR 5th Option):** As per RSSB's updated examination regulations (2023/2024), OMR sheets feature 5 options (A, B, C, D, E). Option 'E' must be darkened if a candidate chooses to leave a question unattempted. Leaving all 5 options blank incurs a deduction of **1 mark (1/4th penalty)** per unattempted blank question. Leaving more than 10% questions blank leads to disqualification.
3. **No Sectional Cut-off:** Marks are calculated in aggregate across Parts A, B, and C. Candidates must score at least 40% (36% for SC/ST) overall to qualify for the merit list.
4. **Written Merit Shortlisting:** Candidates qualifying the written exam (up to 3x to 5x of advertised vacancies per category) are called for PST/PET.

## 7. Subject-Wise Detailed Syllabus

### Part 'A': Reasoning, Logical Ability & Basic Computer Fundamentals (45 Qs / 180 Marks)

#### 1. Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability (तार्किक क्षमता एवं मानसिक योग्यता)
* **Analogy & Classification:** Verbal and non-verbal analogy, letter/number classification.
* **Coding-Decoding:** Letter coding, number coding, substitution, deciphering message codes.
* **Series:** Number series, letter series, mixed series, finding missing numbers/letters.
* **Alphabet & Word Test:** Dictionary order arrangement, word formation, jumbled letters.
* **Blood Relations:** Family tree problems, coded blood relations, statement-based relations.
* **Direction & Distance Test:** Cardinal directions, shortest distance calculation, shadow-based direction problems.
* **Venn Diagrams & Syllogism:** Logical Venn diagrams, statement and conclusions, categorical syllogisms.
* **Order & Ranking:** Seating arrangements (linear and circular), position ranking in a line.
* **Non-Verbal Reasoning:** Mirror images, water images, paper folding and cutting, embedded figures, figure completion.

#### 2. Basic Computer Fundamentals (कंप्यूटर का बुनियादी ज्ञान)
* **Introduction to Computers:** History, generations, hardware components, input devices (keyboard, scanner, barcode reader), output devices (monitors, printers).
* **Computer Memory & Storage:** Primary memory (RAM, ROM, Cache), Secondary storage (HDD, SSD, Pen Drive, Optical Disks).
* **Operating Systems:** Basics of Windows OS, Linux/Unix concepts, file management, directory structures.
* **MS Office Suite:**
  * *MS Word:* Document creation, formatting, shortcuts, tables, mail merge.
  * *MS Excel:* Spreadsheets, basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, IF), formatting, cell referencing.
  * *MS PowerPoint:* Slide creation, transitions, animations, presentation settings.
* **Internet & Networking:** Basics of WWW, web browsers, URLs, IP addresses, email protocols (SMTP, POP3, IMAP), search engines.
* **Cyber Security Basics:** Viruses, malware, firewalls, phishing, cyber safety measures.

### Part 'B': General Knowledge, General Science, Social Science & Current Affairs (25 Qs / 100 Marks)

#### 1. General Science (सामान्य विज्ञान)
* **Physics:** Units & Measurements, Motion, Force & Laws of Motion, Work, Energy & Power, Light (Reflection & Refraction), Human Eye, Sound, Electricity & Magnetism, Solar System.
* **Chemistry:** Physical & Chemical Changes, Acids, Bases & Salts, Metals & Non-metals, Carbon & its Compounds, Chemical Reactions, Everyday Chemistry (Soaps, Detergents, Polymers, Fertilizers).
* **Biology:** Cell structure & function, Human Body Systems (Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Excretory, Nervous, Reproductive), Nutrition & Vitamins, Communicable & Non-Communicable Diseases, Plant Physiology, Environmental Science & Biodiversity.

#### 2. Indian History & Freedom Struggle (भारत का इतिहास)
* **Ancient & Medieval India:** Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic Period, Buddhism & Jainism, Maurya & Gupta Empires, Major Medieval Dynasties, Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire, Bhakti & Sufi Movements.
* **Modern Indian History:** British Rule expansion, 1857 Revolt, Indian National Congress formation, Socio-Religious Reform Movements, Freedom Struggle (Gandhian Era, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India Movement), Independence & Partition.

#### 3. Indian Polity & Governance (भारतीय संविधान एवं राजव्यवस्था)
* **Constitution of India:** Making of the Constitution, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Fundamental Duties, Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP).
* **Executive & Judiciary:** President, Prime Minister & Council of Ministers, Parliament (Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha), Supreme Court & High Courts.
* **Local Self-Government:** Panchayati Raj Institutions (73rd Amendment) and Urban Local Bodies (74th Amendment).

#### 4. Current Affairs (समसामयिक विषय)
* **National & International Events:** Major news events, International organizations (UN, G20, BRICS, SCO), Summits & Conferences.
* **Sports & Awards:** Major sports tournaments (Olympics, Asian Games, IPL, World Cups), National Awards (Bharat Ratna, Padma Awards, Khel Ratna).
* **Government Schemes:** Key Central and State Government welfare schemes, social security programs.
* **Science & Technology Updates:** ISRO space missions, Defence developments, AI and IT innovations.

### Part 'C': History, Art, Culture, Geography & Economy of Rajasthan (30 Qs / 120 Marks)

#### 1. Geography & Natural Resources of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का भूगोल)
* **Physical Features:** Four physical divisions (Western Desert, Aravalli Range, Eastern Plains, Hadoti Plateau), Sand dunes, Mountain passes.
* **Climate & Drainage:** Climatic zones, Monsoons, Major river systems (Chambal, Banas, Luni, Mahi, Sabarmati), Inland drainage rivers, Major lakes (Sambhar, Pichola, Ana Sagar, Rajsamand).
* **Soils & Vegetation:** Soil types and distribution, Forest cover (Reserved, Protected, Unclassified forests), National Parks (Ranthambore, Keoladeo, Sariska), Wildlife Sanctuaries, Tiger Reserves, Conservation Reserves.
* **Minerals & Agriculture:** Metallic and non-metallic minerals, Major crops (Kharif and Rabi), Irrigation projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project, Bisalpur, Mahi Bajaj Sagar).

#### 2. History of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का इतिहास)
* **Ancient Civilizations:** Kalibangan, Ahar, Bairath, Bagor, Ganeshwar.
* **Major Dynasties:** Guhilas & Sisodias of Mewar (Kumbha, Sanga, Pratap), Rathores of Marwar & Bikaner (Jodha, Maldeo, Rai Singh), Kachhwahas of Amber/Jaipur (Man Singh, Sawai Jai Singh), Chauhans of Ajmer & Ranthambore (Prithviraj III), Bhatis of Jaisalmer.
* **Resistance & Modern Movement:** 1857 Revolution in Rajasthan (Auwa, Erinpura, Nasirabad, Neemuch), Peasant Movements (Bijolia, Begun), Tribal Movements (Bhagat Movement, Eki Movement under Motilal Tejawat), Prajamandal Movement, Integration & Unification of Rajasthan (7 stages from 1948 to 1956).

#### 3. Art, Culture & Heritage of Rajasthan (राजस्थान की कला एवं संस्कृति)
* **Forts, Palaces & Monuments:** Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Mehrangarh, Amber, Junagarh, Jaisalmer, Hawa Mahal, City Palaces, Stepwells (Bawris).
* **Fairs & Festivals:** Pushkar Fair, Desert Festival, Teej, Gangaur, Karni Mata Fair, Ramdevra Fair, Sheetla Mata Fair.
* **Folk Deities & Saints:** Panch Pirs (Ramdevji, Pabuji, Gogaji, Mehaji, Harbhuji), Tejaji, Devnarayanji, Saint Jambheshwar, Dadu Dayal, Meera Bai.
* **Folk Dances, Music & Instruments:** Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Kachhi Ghodi, Agni Dance, Chari Dance, Terah Taali; Folk musical instruments (Ravanhatha, Kamaicha, Algoza, Bankia, Chang, Dhol).
* **Handicrafts & Paintings:** Blue Pottery, Thewa Art, Bandhej, Block Printing, Usta Art; Painting Schools (Mewar, Kishangarh - Bani Thani, Marwar, Bundi, Jaipur).
* **Language & Literature:** Rajasthani dialects (Marwari, Jaipuri/Dhundhari, Mewati, Malvi, Bagri), Major literary works (Prithviraj Raso, Nainsi ri Khyat, Veli Krishan Rukmani Ri).

#### 4. Rajasthan Economy, Administration & Prison Rules Basics (राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था व प्रशासन)
* **State Economy:** Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP), Industrial areas (RIICO), Renewable Energy (Solar & Wind energy leadership).

## 10. Verification Checks & Common Coaching Misconceptions

| Parameter / Subject | Common Coaching Error / Misconception | Verified Actual Fact | Primary Official Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Written Marks** | Many job portals state the written exam is out of **100 Marks** (100 Qs x 1 Mark) | Written exam is **400 Marks** (100 Qs x **4 Marks** per question) | RSSB Scheme Notice (`1733996876.pdf`) & Standing Order 01 |
| **Negative Marking** | Commercial sites report negative marking as 0.33 or 1/3rd | Negative marking is strictly **1 Mark** per wrong answer (**1/4th / 25%** of question weightage) | RSSB Official Exam Scheme & FastJobSearchers Guide |
| **Question Breakdown** | Coaching sites misreport 30-30-40 question split | Exact split is **Part A: 45 Qs (180 M)**, **Part B: 25 Qs (100 M)**, **Part C: 30 Qs (120 M)** | RSSB Standing Order / Exam Scheme PDF & HelpStudentPoint |
| **Conducting Body** | Believed to be RPSC or Rajasthan Police Department | Recruiting agency is **RSMSSB / RSSB** (Historically: Sardar Patel University of Police Jodhpur) | RSSB Advt No. 10/2024 & Jail Dept Standing Order 01 |
| **Educational Qualification** | Listed as 10th pass in outdated guides | Minimum qualification is **12th Pass (10+2)** + **CET 12th Level score** | RSSB CET Sr Sec Advt No. 10/2024 Section 6 |
| **Physical Running Event** | Confused with 100m sprint / 1.6 km race | Physical Efficiency Test (PET) is a **5 KM Running Test** (Max 25 mins Male / 35 mins Female) carrying **100 Marks** | Jail Dept Standing Order No. 01 & RSSB PET Rules |
