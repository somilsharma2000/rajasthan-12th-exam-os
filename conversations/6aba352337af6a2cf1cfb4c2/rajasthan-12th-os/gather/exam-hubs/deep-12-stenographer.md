* **Language & Cultural Requirement:** Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan culture.

### Field 5: Full Selection Process & Shorthand/Typing Speed Specifications

#### 5.1 Selection Workflow
Selection is carried out through a 3-stage process:
1. **Stage 1: Phase-I Written Examination (Objective Multiple Choice - 200 Marks Total):**
   - Two written papers held on the same day (Paper 1: GK & Science; Paper 2: Hindi & English).
   - Shortlists candidates for Phase-II in approximately **1:12 to 1:15 ratio** based on Phase-I merit, provided candidates clear the 40% per-paper minimum threshold.
2. **Stage 2: Phase-II Stenography Dictation & Computer Transcription Skill Test (100 Marks Total):**
   - Evaluates real-time shorthand dictation speed, note-taking accuracy, and computer transcription speed.
3. **Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) & Final Merit List:**
   - Final merit list is compiled out of **300 Marks Total** (200 Marks Written + 100 Marks Stenography Skill Test).

---

#### 5.2 Phase-II Stenography Skill Test Specifications (100 Marks)

Candidates select EITHER **Option Group 1 (English Stenography)** OR **Option Group 2 (Hindi Stenography)** at the time of Phase-II registration:

| Parameter | Option Group 1: English Stenography | Option Group 2: Hindi Stenography |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dictation Speed** | **100 WPM (Words Per Minute)** | **80 WPM (Words Per Minute)** |
| **Dictation Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Total Words Dictated** | **1,000 Words** | **800 Words** |
| **Computer Transcription Time** | **60 Minutes** | **70 Minutes** |
| **Skill Test Marks** | **100 Marks** | **100 Marks** |
| **Minimum Qualifying Standard** | **36 Marks (36%)** | **36 Marks (36%)** |
| **Font & Keyboard Layout** | Standard English Font (Calibri / Arial) | **Kruti Dev 010** / **Devlys 010** (Inscript / Remington Layout) |

#### Shorthand & Computer Transcription Rules:
* **Dictation Passage:** A 10-minute speech or passage is dictated at a uniform rate (100 WPM for English, 80 WPM for Hindi). Candidates record shorthand outlines in their official note pad.
* **Computer Transcription:** Candidates transcribe their shorthand notes directly into the custom software provided at the examination center within the permitted duration (60 minutes for English, 70 minutes for Hindi).
* **Error Evaluation & Marking Scheme:**
  * **Full Mistakes / Major Errors:** Omission of words, substitution of words, addition of extra words not in dictation.
  * **Partial Mistakes / Minor Errors:** Spelling errors, punctuation errors, wrong capitalization, paragraph formatting errors.
* **Qualifying Threshold:** Candidates MUST obtain at least **36% marks (36 marks out of 100)** in Phase-II to be considered for final merit listing.

#### 6.2 Examination Rules & Marking Scheme
1. **Question Type:** Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
2. **Mark Weightage:** Each question carries **2/3 mark** (~0.667 marks per question), total 150 questions = 100 marks per paper.
3. **Negative Marking:** **1/3rd mark (0.33 of question weightage / ~0.222 marks per wrong answer)** is deducted for every incorrect response.
4. **Per-Paper Minimum Threshold:** Candidates must secure at least **40% marks in Paper 1 and 40% marks in Paper 2 individually** to be eligible for Phase-II skill test shortlisting.
5. **Mandatory 5th Option Rule (Option 'E' OMR Mandate):**
   - Every MCQ contains 5 options: A, B, C, D, and E.
   - If a candidate chooses not to attempt a question, they MUST darken Option **'E'** (अनुत्तरित प्रश्न).
   - An extra **10 minutes** of exam time is provided exclusively for completing OMR option filling.
   - **Penalty for Unfilled Question:** If a question is left unattempted AND Option 'E' is NOT darkened, **1/3rd of the question mark is deducted**.
   - **Disqualification Rule:** If a candidate leaves more than **10% of total questions (i.e., > 15 questions per paper)** blank without darkening any of the 5 options, the candidate is **disqualified** from the recruitment process.

### Field 7: Detailed Subject-Wise Syllabus

#### 7.1 Paper 1: General Knowledge, Everyday Science & Rajasthan GK (100 Marks)

##### Part A: General Knowledge of India & Everyday Science (50 Marks / 75 Questions)
1. **Current Affairs:** State, National, and International current affairs; major sports events, awards, and eminent personalities.
2. **Indian Geography & Natural Resources:** Physical divisions of India, climate, rivers, lakes, soils, forests, wildlife conservation, energy resources, and agricultural overview.
3. **Everyday Science (दैनिक विज्ञान):**
   - **Physics:** Reflection and refraction of light, lenses, human eye and defects of vision; Electric current, Ohm's law, electric circuits, magnetic and heating effects of electric current, electric motors and generators.
   - **Chemistry:** Physical and chemical changes; Oxidation and reduction; Metals, non-metals, and their important compounds; Polymers, soaps, detergents, bleaching powder, plaster of Paris, baking soda, washing soda; Acids, bases, and salts.
   - **Biology:** Human organ systems (digestive, circulatory, respiratory, excretory, nervous, reproductive); Human nutrition, vitamins, deficiency diseases; Infectious diseases, vaccination, immunity; Genetics, Mendel's laws, DNA and RNA; Biotechnology in agriculture and medicine, tissue culture, genetic engineering, ecosystems and environmental pollution.

##### Part B: General Knowledge of Rajasthan (50 Marks / 75 Questions)
1. **Geography & Natural Resources of Rajasthan:** Physical divisions, climate, drainage system (rivers & lakes), soils, natural vegetation, forests, wildlife, biodiversity & conservation. Agriculture, major crops, irrigation projects, livestock, dairy development, mineral resources (metallic & non-metallic), energy resources.
2. **History, Art, Culture & Heritage of Rajasthan:** Ancient civilizations (Kalibangan, Ahar, Bairath, Ganeshwar). Major dynasties (Guhils, Rathores, Kachhwahas, Chauhans). Freedom movement, Political Awakening, Peasant & Tribal Movements, Prajamandal Movement, Integration of Rajasthan. Architecture: Forts, Palaces, Temples, Stepwells, Havellis. Fairs, Festivals, Saints, Lok Devtas & Lok Devis. Folk Art, Paintings, Handicrafts, Folk Music, Instruments, Dances, Drama. Rajasthani literature and regional dialects.
3. **Economy & Welfare Schemes of Rajasthan:** Economic scenario, industrial development, major state development projects, state social welfare schemes, poverty and unemployment alleviation programs.

##### Part A: General Hindi / सामान्य हिंदी (50 Marks / 75 Questions)

8. **Vocabulary:** Synonyms, Antonyms, One-Word Substitutions, Prefixes/Suffixes, Confusable Words.
9. **Official / Technical Terms:** English terms used in official correspondence with Hindi equivalents.
10. **Translation:** Translation of simple sentences from English to Hindi and vice-versa.
11. **Comprehension:** Questions based on an unseen passage.
