## Field 6: Written Examination Pattern

* **Primary Source:** RSSB CET (12th Level) & Jamadar Grade-II Examination Scheme Release.
* **Secondary Source:** RSSB OMR Instruction Gazette Orders & *Govt Job Hub India*.
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

### Key Findings
1. **Exam Mode:** Offline Written Test (OMR Sheet based).
2. **Total Questions:** **150 Questions**.
3. **Total Marks:** **300 Marks** (2 Marks per question).
4. **Exam Duration:** **3 Hours (180 Minutes)**.
5. **Question Format:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with **5 Options (A, B, C, D, E)**.
6. **Negative Marking Rules:**
   * Deduction of **1/3rd mark (0.66 marks)** for every incorrect answer.
   * **Mandatory Option 'E' Rule:** If a candidate chooses not to attempt a question, they MUST darken Option 'E' on the OMR sheet.
   * If a question is left unattempted without darkening Option 'E', **1/3rd mark (0.66 marks)** will be deducted for that blank question.
   * If a candidate leaves more than **10% of total questions (i.e. >15 questions)** completely blank without filling any option (A, B, C, D, or E), they are **disqualified** from the examination.
   * Extra **10 minutes** duration is allotted at the end of 3 hours specifically to ensure all Option 'E' bubbles are darkened for unattempted questions.

## Strategic Preparation Recommendations for Platform Content

1. **High-Yield Subject Focus:**
   * **Rajasthan GK (History, Culture & Geography):** Accounts for 120+ marks (~40% of the paper). Content must feature deep coverage of Rajasthan's integration, forts, local deities, and physical geography.
   * **General Science & Math:** Together carry 90 marks (30% of the paper). Vedic math and everyday biology/physics concepts yield high accuracy.
2. **Negative Marking & Option 'E' Drills:**
   * Practice tests on the platform MUST implement the mandatory 5th option ('E') interface and enforce the 10% unattempted threshold rule to train candidates against accidental disqualification.
3. **CET Dual Preparation:**
   * Since CET (12th Level) is the mandatory gateway, study plans should integrate CET PYQs and Jamadar main exam practice tests under a unified Rajasthan 12th-level prep hub.
