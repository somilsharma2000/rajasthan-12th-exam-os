## Detailed Load-Bearing Survey Fields

### Field 1: Recruiting Body, User Department & Pay Level Structure

#### A. Recruiting Bodies & Roles
* **Stage 1 (Eligibility Screening Test):** **Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE / BSER, Ajmer)** — *माध्यमिक शिक्षा बोर्ड राजस्थान, अजमेर*.  
  * **Role:** Conducts the Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers (REET) Level 1 screening test to certify eligibility for primary school teaching. Issue of lifetime eligibility certificates.
* **Stage 2 (Mains Direct Recruitment Examination):** **Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB, Jaipur)** — *राजस्थान कर्मचारी चयन बोर्ड, जयपुर*.  
  * **Role:** Conducts the competitive 3rd Grade Teacher (Primary Level 1) Mains examination, calculates merit, and shortlists candidates for appointment.
* **User Department:** **Elementary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan** (*प्रारंभिक शिक्षा विभाग, राजस्थान सरकार*) / Directorate of Elementary Education, Bikaner (*प्रारंभिक शिक्षा निदेशालय, बीकानेर*).
* **Posts Covered:** Primary School Teacher Grade-III (Level 1, Classes I to V) / *तृतीय श्रेणी अध्यापक लेवल-1 (कक्षा 1 से 5)*.
* **Cadre:** Subordinate Teaching Service (Elementary Education Cadre).

#### B. Pay Level & Remuneration Structure
* **Pay Matrix Level:** **Pay Level L-10** under the Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017 (7th Pay Commission scale).
* **Pre-revised Scale:** Grade Pay ₹3600 (Pay Band PB-2 ₹9,300 – ₹34,800).
* **Probationary Period:** Mandatory **2 Years** probation period as Probationer Trainee.
* **Fixed Remuneration During Probation:** **₹23,700/- per month** (Fixed stipend without Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, or city allowances during 2-year probation).
* **Post-Probation Salary Structure:**
  * **Minimum Basic Pay:** Starting at Level L-10 minimum cell (**₹33,800/- per month**).
  * **Allowances:** Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA @ 9% or 18% depending on posting location), City Compensatory Allowance (where applicable), and Rural Teaching allowances.
  * **Gross Salary (Post-Probation):** Approx. ₹50,000 – ₹55,000 per month (depending on prevailing DA rates).

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
  * **Primary Source (Official):** RSMSSB 3rd Grade Teacher Direct Recruitment Advertisement No. 12/2022 dated 16/12/2022, Clause 3 (*वेतनमान*); Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules 2017 Schedule-IV.
  * **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** Jagran Josh & Adda247 Rajasthan 3rd Grade Teacher Salary & Pay Scale Structure Guide.

### Field 2: Educational Qualification & B.Ed Exclusion Status

#### A. Mandatory Educational Qualification
1. **Academic Base:** Senior Secondary (10+2 / Intermediate or equivalent) with minimum **50% Marks** from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), CBSE, or any recognized State/Central Board.
2. **Professional Teaching Qualification:** Passed or appearing in final year of 2-year **Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / BSTC / D.Ed)** from an institution recognized by NCTE (National Council for Teacher Education).  
   *OR*  
   Senior Secondary (10+2) with minimum 50% marks and 4-year **Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed)**.  
   *OR*  
   Senior Secondary (10+2) with minimum 50% marks and 2-year Diploma in Education (Special Education).

#### B. Judicial Mandate on B.Ed Exclusion (Supreme Court Ruling)
* **Status:** **B.Ed Degree Holders are Strictly EXCLUDED from REET Level 1 (Classes I–V).**
* **Judicial History & Binding Precedent:**
  * The Rajasthan High Court (Division Bench) in its landmark judgment dated November 25, 2021 quashed the NCTE notification dated June 28, 2018 that had allowed B.Ed holders to teach primary classes (I–V) subject to a 6-month bridge course.
  * The **Supreme Court of India** in *Devesh Sharma vs. Union of India & Ors.* (Civil Appeal No. 5068 of 2023, Judgment dated August 11, 2023) upheld the Rajasthan High Court decision in full.
  * The Apex Court ruled that B.Ed is not a suitable qualification for primary education as it lacks pedagogical training specific to foundational learning for children aged 6–11 years. Consequently, **D.El.Ed / BSTC is the sole mandatory professional qualification** for REET Level 1.
* **No Re-Litigation:** Eligibility is fully settled and binding across all government primary teacher recruitments in Rajasthan.

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
  * **Primary Source (Official):** Supreme Court of India Judgment in Civil Appeal No. 5068 of 2023 (*Devesh Sharma v. Union of India and Others*, Order dated 11/08/2023); RSMSSB Advt No. 12/2022 Clause 5.
  * **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** LiveLaw Legal Report (11.08.2023) & Times of India Education Analysis on SC Ruling on REET Level 1.

### Field 3: Age Limits & Relaxations

#### A. REET Screening Eligibility Examination (RBSE Ajmer)
* **Minimum Age:** 18 Years (or minimum age required to complete 10+2 and D.El.Ed course).
* **Upper Age Limit:** **NO UPPER AGE LIMIT** for appearing in the REET Screening Examination conducted by RBSE.

#### B. RSMSSB 3rd Grade Teacher Mains Recruitment Examination (RSMSSB Jaipur)
* **Age Calculation Cut-Off Date:** January 1 of the year following the notification (e.g., 01/01/2024 for 2023 recruitment).
* **Minimum Age:** **18 Years**.
* **Maximum Age (General / Unreserved Male):** **40 Years**.

#### C. Category-Wise Upper Age Relaxations (For Mains Recruitment)
1. **Male Candidates of SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS (Rajasthan Domicile):** **5 Years relaxation** (Upper age limit = 45 years).
2. **General / Unreserved Female Candidates:** **5 Years relaxation** (Upper age limit = 45 years).
3. **Female Candidates of SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS (Rajasthan Domicile):** **10 Years relaxation** (Upper age limit = 50 years).
4. **Ex-Servicemen:** Upper age limit up to **50 years** (or continuous defense service duration + 3 years as per Rajasthan Service Rules).
5. **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD / PH):** **10 Years relaxation** in upper age limit.
6. **Widows and Divorced Women:** **NO UPPER AGE LIMIT** (Eligible up to the age of superannuation, 60 years).

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
  * **Primary Source (Official):** RSMSSB Direct Recruitment Advertisement No. 12/2022, Clause 6 (*आयु सीमा*) & Rajasthan Elementary Education Subordinate Service Rules.
  * **Secondary Source (Corroborating):** Prepp & Rojgar With Ankit REET Level 1 Age Limit & Relaxation Eligibility Summary.

#### B. Stage 2: RSMSSB 3rd Grade Teacher Mains Level 1 Examination Pattern (RSMSSB Jaipur)
* **Total Questions:** 150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
* **Total Marks:** 300 Marks (2 Marks per Question)
* **Duration:** 2 Hours 30 Minutes (150 Minutes)
* **Question Paper Medium:** Bilingual (Hindi & English)
* **Negative Marking Scheme:** **1/3rd Mark Deducted per Wrong Answer** (i.e. **0.66 Marks** deducted for every incorrect response).
* **Mandatory 5th Option Rule (Option 'E'):** 
  * RSMSSB enforces a mandatory 5th option (Option 'E') on OMR sheets for unanswered questions.
  * If a candidate does not wish to attempt a question, they must darken the 5th circle ('E').
  * Leaving any question un-attempted without darkening Option 'E' attracts a deduction of **1/3rd mark** (0.66 marks).
  * If a candidate leaves more than **10% of total questions (15 questions)** un-attempted without darkening Option 'E', they are **disqualified** from the examination.

### Field 6: Detailed Syllabus Summary

#### A. Stage 1: REET Screening Level 1 Syllabus Highlights
1. **Child Development & Pedagogy:** Growth and development concepts, physical/motor/cognitive/social development, theories of learning (Thorndike, Piaget, Vygotsky, Pavlov, Skinner), individual differences, intelligence theories, inclusive education, special needs children, learning difficulties, RTE Act 2009, CCE (Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation), action research.
2. **Language I & Language II (Hindi / English / Sanskrit etc.):** Unseen prose and poetry passages, grammatical analysis (parts of speech, tenses, sentence structure, idioms, vocabulary), teaching-learning materials, principles of language teaching, evaluation of language proficiency, remedial teaching.
3. **Mathematics:** Numbers up to 1 crore, place value, fundamental arithmetical operations, fractions, prime/composite numbers, LCM & HCF, unitary method, percentage, profit and loss, simple interest, plane and solid geometric figures, perimeter and area, money, measurement of weight/time/volume, pedagogical methods of teaching mathematics.
4. **Environmental Studies (EVS):** Family and relationships, clothes and habitats, profession and public places, waste management, transport and communication, personal hygiene, plants and animals, solar system, air and water pollution, geography/culture of Rajasthan, pedagogical issues in EVS, practical experiments/field trips.

#### B. Stage 2: RSMSSB Level 1 Mains Detailed Syllabus Highlights
1. **Rajasthan Geography (राजस्थान का भूगोल):** Physical divisions, climate, drainage system (rivers & lakes), forest resources, wildlife & biodiversity, soils, agriculture & major crops, mineral resources, population distribution, density, literacy & sex ratio, major industries, transportation, water conservation techniques.
2. **Rajasthan History & Culture (राजस्थान का इतिहास एवं संस्कृति):** Ancient civilizations (Kalibangan, Ahar, Ganeshwar, Bairath), major royal dynasties (Guhil, Rathore, Kachwaha, Chauhan) and their administrative achievements, forts, palaces, temples, stepwells, fairs, festivals, folk dances, folk music, folk deities (*Lok Devta & Deviyan*), saints, traditional attire and jewelry, painting styles (*Chitrakala*), 1857 Revolution in Rajasthan, Prajamandal movements, peasant movements, integration of Rajasthan (*Rajasthan Ekikaran*).
3. **Rajasthani Language (राजस्थानी भाषा):** Regional dialects of Rajasthan (Dingal, Pingal, Marwari, Mewati, Dhundhari, Malvi), major Rajasthani literary figures, classical literature, folk idioms and proverbs.
4. **Rajasthan GK, Educational Scenario & RTE:** Symbol/emblems of Rajasthan, government welfare schemes, flagship projects, educational administration setup in Rajasthan, free textbook distribution, mid-day meal scheme, RTE Act 2009 detailed sections, Rajasthan Free and Compulsory Child Education Rules 2011, current affairs of Rajasthan.
5. **School Subjects (Classes I–V Standard):**
   * **Hindi:** Noun, pronoun, adjective, verb, संधि, समास, उपसर्ग, प्रत्यय, पर्यायवाची, विलोम शब्द, वाक्य शुद्धि.
   * **English:** Parts of speech, tenses, active/passive voice, direct/indirect speech, articles, prepositions, idioms & phrases, unseen comprehension.
   * **Mathematics:** Real numbers, basic algebra, ratio and proportion, percentage, profit/loss, simple interest, lines and angles, area and perimeter of 2D shapes.
   * **General Science:** States of matter, chemical changes, metals and non-metals, cell structure, human body systems, nutrition, plant structure, energy sources.
   * **Social Studies:** Ancient Indian history/civilizations, Solar system, Earth's movements, Continents and Oceans, Constitution of India, fundamental rights, local self-government (Panchayati Raj).
6. **Teaching Methodology / Pedagogy (*रीति विज्ञान*):** Subject-wise teaching principles, instructional techniques, lesson planning, teaching aids, diagnostic and remedial teaching methods.
7. **Educational Psychology (*शैक्षणिक मनोविज्ञान*):** Educational psychology scope, learner characteristics, learning process, motivation, personality assessment, adjustment mechanisms.
8. **Information Technology (*सूचना प्रौद्योगिकी*):** Computer fundamentals, hardware and software, network & internet concepts, e-learning tools, ICT in educational management.
