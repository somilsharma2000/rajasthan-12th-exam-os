## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Matrix

The Rajasthan Lab Assistant (Prayogshala Sahayak) Examination is one of the most sought-after **12th-pass technical recruitment examinations** in Rajasthan. Administered by the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB), Jaipur, it offers direct entry into state government departments including Secondary Education, Agriculture, College Education, and State Forensic Science Laboratories (SFSL). 

Unlike most administrative 12th-pass state posts, **Lab Assistant is an Independent Direct Recruitment Exam and is strictly EXEMPTED from the CET 12th Level (Common Eligibility Test)**. Candidates with Senior Secondary (12th) certificates in Science, Geography, or Home Science streams can apply directly without a CET scorecard or any prior lab diploma.

### Field 1: Recruiting Body
* **Official Designation:** Rajasthan Staff Selection Board, Jaipur (राजस्थान कर्मचारी चयन बोर्ड, जयपुर).
* **Former/Alternate Names:** RSMSSB (Rajasthan Subordinate and Ministerial Services Selection Board) / RSSB.
* **Constitutional & Legal Framework:** Constituted under Rule 3 of the Rajasthan Public Service Commission (Limitation of Functions) Regulations and state executive notifications to handle Group-B (non-gazetted) and Group-C direct recruitment for state subordinate services.
* **Headquarters:** State Agriculture Management Institute Campus, Durgapura, Jaipur - 302018, Rajasthan.
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

### Field 2: Pay Level & Scale
* **Pay Matrix Structure (7th Pay Commission / Rajasthan Civil Services Rules 2017):**
  * **Lab Assistant (Grade-III / Senior Secondary Depts / College Education / SFSL):** Pay Matrix Level **L-8** (Grade Pay ₹2,800, Pay Band PB-1 ₹5,200 – ₹20,200).
  * **Junior Lab Assistant (State Forensic Science Laboratory - SFSL):** Pay Matrix Level **L-5** (Grade Pay ₹2,400, Pay Band PB-1 ₹5,200 – ₹20,200).
* **Probation Period & Fixed Remuneration:**
  * **Probation Duration:** Mandatory 2 Years (24 months) probation.
  * **Probation Monthly Stipend:**
    * Level **L-8** Posts: Fixed stipend of **₹18,500/- per month** during 2-year probation.
    * Level **L-5** Posts (Junior Lab Assistant): Fixed stipend of **₹14,600/- per month** during 2-year probation.
  * **Post-Probation Salary (Regular Matrix Entry):**
    * Level **L-8**: Initial Basic Pay starts at **₹26,300/-** + Dearness Allowance (DA @ 50%+) + House Rent Allowance (HRA @ 9%/18%) + Special Allowances. Gross starting monthly salary post-probation is approx. **₹41,000 - ₹45,500/-**.
    * Level **L-5**: Initial Basic Pay starts at **₹20,800/-** + DA + HRA + Allowances. Gross starting monthly salary post-probation is approx. **₹32,000 - ₹35,000/-**.
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

### Field 3: Qualifications & Eligibility
* **CET Screening Exemption:**
  * **Is CET Required?** **NO**. Lab Assistant (Prayogshala Sahayak) is an **Independent Non-CET Direct Recruitment Examination**. CET 12th Level scorecard is **NOT** required.
* **Lab-Oriented Diploma Prerequisite:**
  * **Is a Diploma (DMLT / Lab Tech Diploma) Required?** **NO**. DMLT or any technical laboratory diploma is **NOT required** for Lab Assistant. Senior Secondary (12th Pass) certificate with prescribed academic subjects is the sole educational requirement.
* **Department-Wise Educational Eligibility (Per 04/2022 Notification Standard):**

  1. **Post Code 1 - Lab Assistant (Science) [Secondary Education Department / माध्यमिक शिक्षा विभाग]:**
     * **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass) from RBSE/CBSE or equivalent recognized board with **at least 3 subjects** out of the following 7 subjects:
       1. Micro-biology (सूक्ष्म जीव विज्ञान)
       2. Bio-technology (जैव प्रौद्योगिकी)
       3. Bio-chemistry (जैव रसायन विज्ञान)
       4. Physics (भौतिक विज्ञान)
       5. Chemistry (रसायन विज्ञान)
       6. Biology (जीव विज्ञान)
       7. Mathematics (गणित)
  
  2. **Post Code 2, 3, 4, 5 - Lab Assistant (Science) & Junior Lab Assistant [Agriculture Dept / College Education / SFSL]:**
     * **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass) in Science stream (with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Maths/Agriculture).

  3. **Post Code 6 - Lab Assistant (Geography) [College Education Commissionerate / कॉलेज शिक्षा आयुक्तालय]:**
     * **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass) with **Geography** as one of the optional subjects.

  4. **Post Code 7 - Lab Assistant (Home Science) [College Education Commissionerate / कॉलेज शिक्षा आयुक्तालय]:**
     * **Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass) with **Home Science** as one of the optional subjects.

* **Mandatory General Criteria (All Cadres):**
  * Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script (देवनागरी लिपि में लिखी हिंदी में कार्य करने का ज्ञान).
  * Knowledge of Rajasthan's Culture & Heritage (राजस्थान की संस्कृति का ज्ञान).
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
* **Verification Sources:**
  1. *Primary Official Source:* RSSB Official Notification No. 04/2022 (Section 5: "पात्रता एवं शैक्षणिक योग्यता" / Educational Qualifications).

### Field 4: Age Limits & Relaxations
* **Age Calculation Anchor Date:** As of **January 1** of the year following the notification release (e.g., 1st January 2023 for 2022 recruitment cycle).
* **Age Bracket:**
  * **Minimum Age:** 18 Years.
  * **Maximum Age:** 40 Years.
* **Category-Wise Upper Age Relaxations:**
  * **Male candidates belonging to SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS of Rajasthan state:** 5 Years Relaxation (up to 45 years).
  * **General Category Female candidates:** 5 Years Relaxation (up to 45 years).
  * **Female candidates belonging to SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS of Rajasthan state:** 10 Years Relaxation (up to 50 years).
  * **Ex-Servicemen:** Maximum age limit relaxed up to 50 years.
  * **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD / PH):** 10 to 15 years relaxation depending on disability sub-category.
  * **Special Non-Conduction Relaxation Rule:** Under Rajasthan Service Rules, if the recruitment exam was not conducted in preceding calendar years, a general relaxation of up to **3 years** is extended to candidates exceeding the age limit.

### Field 5: Selection Stages & Pipeline
* **Selection Process Workflow:**
  1. **Stage 1: Single-Phase Competitive Written Examination**
     * Objective Multiple-Choice Question (MCQ) Examination conducted in OMR offline mode (or CBT mode).
  2. **Stage 2: Shortlisting & Document Verification (DV)**
     * Candidates scoring above the minimum qualifying mark (40%) are shortlisted in a ratio of **1.5 times (150%)** to **2 times** total advertised vacancies per category for Document Verification.
  3. **Stage 3: Final Merit List & Medical Examination**
     * Final merit list published based strictly on aggregate marks obtained in the written exam.
     * Medical examination conducted by designated District Medical Boards prior to departmental posting orders.
* **CET Screening Requirement:** **Exempted** (Direct written entry).
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

### Field 6: Written Exam Pattern & Scheme
* **Format:** Single-day offline OMR-based competitive examination.
* **Stream-Wise Structure:**

  #### Stream 1: Science Stream (Posts 1 to 5 - School Education, Agriculture, College Ed, SFSL)
  * Exam divided into **Two Papers** conducted in two separate sessions on the same day.

  | Paper | Subject / Domain | No. of Questions | Maximum Marks | Duration |
  | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
  | **Paper-I** | General Knowledge of Rajasthan (History, Art, Culture, Geography), World & India GK, Educational Psychology | 100 Qs | 200 Marks | 2 Hours (120 Mins) |
  | **Paper-II** | General Science (Senior Secondary Standard: Physics, Chemistry, Biology - Botany & Zoology) | 100 Qs | 200 Marks | 2 Hours (120 Mins) |
  | **Total** | **Combined Science Stream Pattern** | **200 Qs** | **400 Marks** | **4 Hours Total** |

  #### Stream 2: Geography Stream (Post Code 6 - College Education)
  * Exam conducted as a single combined paper split into two parts.

  | Part | Subject / Domain | No. of Questions | Maximum Marks | Duration |
  | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
  | **Part A** | General Knowledge of Rajasthan (History, Art, Culture, Geography) | ~50 Qs | 100 Marks | Combined |
  | **Part B** | Geography Subject (Senior Secondary Standard) | ~100 Qs | 200 Marks | Combined |
  | **Total** | **Combined Geography Stream Pattern** | **150 Qs** | **300 Marks** | **3 Hours (180 Mins)** |

  #### Stream 3: Home Science Stream (Post Code 7 - College Education)
  * Exam conducted as a single combined paper split into two parts.

  | Part | Subject / Domain | No. of Questions | Maximum Marks | Duration |
  | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
  | **Part A** | General Knowledge of Rajasthan (History, Art, Culture, Geography) | ~50 Qs | 100 Marks | Combined |
  | **Part B** | Home Science Subject (Senior Secondary Standard) | ~100 Qs | 200 Marks | Combined |
  | **Total** | **Combined Home Science Stream Pattern** | **150 Qs** | **300 Marks** | **3 Hours (180 Mins)** |

* **Negative Marking & Penalty Rules:**
  * **Negative Marking Rate:** **1/3rd mark (33.33% deduction)** of the marks assigned to a question for every wrong answer.
  * **Minimum Qualifying Marks:** Mandatory **40% minimum aggregate marks** required to be eligible for merit consideration. Candidates scoring below 40% are disqualified regardless of vacancy seats.
  * **5th Option OMR Rule (Recent RSSB Standard):** Candidates must darken Option 'E' (Option 5) if they choose not to attempt a question. If an unattempted question is left blank without darkening Option 'E', **1/3rd negative deduction** is applied. Leaving more than **10% of total questions blank** leads to immediate disqualification.

#### Detailed Topic Distribution:

##### 1. Paper-I: General Knowledge & Educational Psychology
* **Part A: Rajasthan History, Art, Culture & Heritage:**
  * Ancient Civilizations: Kalibangan, Ahar, Ganeshwar, Bairath, Bagor.
  * Major Dynasties of Rajasthan: Guhilas, Rathores, Kachwahas, Chauhans, Parmars - Political achievements and cultural contributions.
  * Forts, Palaces, Monuments, Architecture, Temples, Stepwells (Baoris).
  * Painting Styles (Marwar, Mewar, Jaipur, Kishangarh, Dhundhar) & Handicrafts.
  * Fairs, Festivals, Folk Deities (Lok Devta & Lok Deviya), Saints & Sects (Sants).
  * Folk Music, Musical Instruments, Folk Dances, Folk Dramas.
  * Rajasthani Literature, Dialects, Manuscripts, Famous Authors.
  * Freedom Movement in Rajasthan, 1857 Revolution, Peasant & Tribal Movements (Prajamandal Movements).
  * Integration of Rajasthan (7 Stages).
* **Part B: Rajasthan Geography & Economy:**
  * Physical Features, Physiographic Divisions (Western Desert, Aravalli Range, Eastern Plains, Hadoti Plateau).
  * Climate, Rainfall Patterns, Seasons.
  * Drainage System: Rivers, Lakes, Dams, Water Conservation Techniques.
  * Soil Types & Distribution, Soil Erosion.
  * Natural Vegetation, Forest Types, Wildlife Conservation, National Parks & Sanctuaries.
  * Agriculture & Principal Crops, Irrigation Projects.
  * Mineral Resources (Metallic & Non-Metallic), Energy Resources (Thermal, Solar, Wind, Hydro).
  * Population Geography: Density, Literacy, Sex Ratio, Scheduled Castes & Tribes.
  * Tourist Circuits & Economic Schemes of Rajasthan Government.
* **Part C: World & India General Knowledge:**
  * Continents, Oceans, Major Mountain Ranges, Global Environmental Issues.
  * Indian Geography: Physical divisions, Rivers, Climate, Forests, Agriculture.
  * Indian Constitution & Administrative System.
  * Major Current Affairs (National & State level).
* **Part D: Educational Psychology (शैक्षणिक मनोविज्ञान):**
  * Concept, scope, and significance of Educational Psychology in science education.
  * Child Development: Physical, cognitive, emotional, and social development.
  * Learning Theories: Behavioral, cognitive, and constructivist learning concepts.
  * Individual Differences, Personality, Intelligence, Motivation, and Mental Health.

##### 2. Paper-II: General Science (Senior Secondary Standard - Physics, Chemistry, Biology)

* **Physics Topics (Sr. Sec. Standard):**
  1. *Electric Charges and Fields:* Coulomb's law, electric field, electric dipole, Gauss's law and its applications.
  2. *Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance:* Potential due to point charge, capacitors, combination of capacitors, energy stored in capacitors.
  3. *Current Electricity:* Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, Wheatstone bridge, Potentiometer, Meter bridge.
  4. *Optics:* Ray Optics (Reflection, Refraction, Lenses, Total Internal Reflection, Optical instruments) & Wave Optics (Huygens principle, Interference, Young's double slit experiment, Diffraction, Polarization).
  5. *Thermodynamics & Heat:* First & Second laws of thermodynamics, Heat engines, Refrigerators, Heat transfer (Conduction, Convection, Radiation).
  6. *Magnetic Effects of Current & Magnetism:* Biot-Savart law, Ampere's circuital law, Cyclotron, Moving coil galvanometer, Earth's magnetism, Magnetic materials.
  7. *Electromagnetic Induction & Alternating Current:* Faraday's laws, Lenz's law, Self & Mutual induction, AC circuits, Transformers.
  8. *Atoms & Nuclei:* Alpha-particle scattering experiment, Rutherford & Bohr models, Nuclear binding energy, Radioactivity, Fission & Fusion.
  9. *Semiconductor Electronics:* Energy bands, Intrinsic & Extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction diode, Rectifiers, Transistors, Logic gates.

* **Chemistry Topics (Sr. Sec. Standard):**
  1. *Periodic Table & Periodicity:* Periodic trends in properties (Atomic radii, Ionization enthalpy, Electron gain enthalpy, Electronegativity).
  2. *Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure:* Ionic bond, Covalent bond, VSEPR theory, Valence Bond Theory, Hybridization, Molecular Orbital Theory, Hydrogen bonding.
  3. *States of Matter:* Gas laws, Ideal gas equation, Dalton's law, Kinetic theory of gases, Liquefaction of gases, Surface tension & Viscosity.
  4. *Solutions:* Types of solutions, Raoult's law, Colligative properties (Relative lowering of vapor pressure, Elevation of boiling point, Depression of freezing point, Osmotic pressure), Van't Hoff factor.
  5. *Chemical Equilibrium & Ionic Equilibrium:* Law of mass action, Le Chatelier's principle, pH scale, Buffer solutions, Solubility product, Common ion effect.
  6. *Thermodynamics:* Enthalpy, Entropy, Gibbs free energy, Spontaneity of reactions, Hess's law.
  7. *Electrochemistry:* Nernst equation, Conductance in electrolytic solutions, Kohlrausch's law, Fuel cells, Corrosion.
  8. *Organic Chemistry Basics & Hydrocarbons:* IUPAC Nomenclature, Isomerism, Inductive effect, Resonance, Hyperconjugation, Preparation & properties of Alkanes, Alkenes, Alkynes, Arenes.
  9. *Organic Functional Groups:* Haloalkanes, Haloarenes, Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers, Aldehydes, Ketones, Carboxylic acids, Amines.
  10. *Polymers & Biomolecules:* Classification of polymers, Addition & Condensation polymerization; Carbohydrates, Proteins, Nucleic acids (DNA/RNA), Vitamins.

* **Biology - Part I: Botany Topics (Sr. Sec. Standard):**
  1. *Plant Cell & Tissue:* Cell structure, Organelles, Cell wall, Cell membrane, Cell cycle & Mitosis/Meiosis, Plant tissues (Meristematic & Permanent).
  2. *Plant Anatomy:* Anatomy of Dicot & Monocot Roots, Stems, and Leaves; Secondary growth in stems and roots.
  3. *Plant Physiology:* Water relations, Transpiration, Mineral nutrition, Photosynthesis (Light & Dark reactions, C3 & C4 pathways), Plant Respiration (Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, ETS), Plant growth regulators (Auxins, Gibberellins, Cytokinins, Abscisic acid, Ethylene).
  4. *Plant Reproduction:* Sexual reproduction in flowering plants, Microsporogenesis, Megasporogenesis, Pollination, Double fertilization, Seed & Fruit development.
  5. *Genetics & Biotechnology:* Mendel's laws of inheritance, Chromosomal basis of inheritance, DNA structure & Replication, Recombinant DNA technology, Transgenic plants, Applications in agriculture.
  6. *Ecology & Environment:* Ecosystem structure & function, Energy flow, Ecological pyramids, Nutrient cycles, Biodiversity conservation, Environmental pollution & issues.

* **Biology - Part II: Zoology Topics (Sr. Sec. Standard):**
  1. *Animal Kingdom Classification:* Non-Chordates (Porifera to Echinodermata) and Chordates up to Class level with general characteristics and examples.
  2. *Human Physiology:*
     * Digestion & Absorption (Digestive organs, Enzymes, Process).
     * Breathing & Respiration (Respiratory organs, Gas exchange, Regulation).
     * Body Fluids & Circulation (Blood components, Heart structure, Cardiac cycle, ECG, Double circulation).
     * Excretory Products & Elimination (Nephron structure, Urine formation, Regulation).
     * Locomotion & Movement (Muscle contraction, Skeleton system, Joints).
     * Neural Control & Coordination (Neuron, Brain, Spinal cord, Reflex action, Eye & Ear).
     * Chemical Coordination & Integration (Endocrine glands, Hormones & Mechanisms).
  3. *Human Reproduction & Development:* Male & Female reproductive systems, Gametogenesis, Menstrual cycle, Fertilization, Embryonic development, Parturition & Lactation.
  4. *Human Health & Diseases:* Pathogens causing human diseases (Malaria, Typhoid, Pneumonia, Amoebiasis, AIDS), Immunity (Innate & Acquired), Vaccination, Cancer.
  5. *Economic Zoology:* Apiculture, Sericulture, Aquaculture, Animal Husbandry.
