## Field 2: Pay Level & Remuneration Structure

* **Pay Matrix Level:** **Pay Matrix Level L-5** (under 7th Pay Commission / Rajasthan Civil Services Revised Pay Rules 2017).
* **Grade Pay Reference:** Equivalent to Grade Pay ₹2,400 under earlier 6th CPC structure.
* **Probationary Period Remuneration:**
  * **Duration:** 2 Years compulsory probation as "Probationer Trainee".
  * **Fixed Remuneration:** **₹14,600 per month** (fixed allowance during probation; no DA, HRA, or other allowances during probation).
* **Post-Probation Pay Scale:**
  * **Starting Basic Pay:** **₹20,800** at Pay Matrix Level L-5 (Cell 1).
  * **Allowances Included Post-Probation:** Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Washing Allowance, Conveyance Allowance, Special Police Risk/Duty Allowance, and Ration Money.
  * **Gross Monthly Pay Estimate:** ~₹32,000 to ₹36,000 per month (depending on posting location/city HRA tier and prevalent DA rate).
* **Pension Scheme:** Governed by Rajasthan Civil Services Pension Rules (Old Pension Scheme - OPS restored for Rajasthan State Government Employees).
* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`

## Field 3: Qualifications & Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Educational Qualifications (Post-Wise)
1. **Constable (General Duty / GD / Band / RAC / MBC / Police Intelligence):**
   * Passed **10+2 / Senior Secondary / 12th Standard** or equivalent from a recognized Board / University.
2. **Constable (Telecommunication):**
   * Passed **10+2 / Senior Secondary / 12th Standard** with **Physics and Mathematics / Science** subjects from a recognized Board / University.
3. **Constable (Driver):**
   * Passed **10+2 / Senior Secondary / 12th Standard** from a recognized Board.
   * **Mandatory Driving License:** Must possess a valid Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) or Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving license issued **at least 1 year prior** to the date of publication of the recruitment notification.

### 3.2 CET Requirement (Rajasthan Common Eligibility Test 12th Level)
* **CET Mandate:** Appearing and qualifying in the **Rajasthan CET (Senior Secondary Level / 12th Level)** conducted by RSMSSB/RSSB is **mandatory** for applying to Rajasthan Police Constable.
* **Minimum Application CET Threshold (Marks out of 300):**
  * **General / OBC / EWS / MBC:** **40% marks** (120 / 300 marks in CET).
  * **SC / ST:** **36% marks** (108 / 300 marks in CET).
  * **TSP Area SC/ST & Sahariya candidates:** **30% marks** (90 / 300 marks in CET).
* **15-Times (15x) Shortlisting Rule:**
  * Candidates who apply online are ranked district-wise/unit-wise and category-wise according to their CET scores.
  * Candidates equal to **15 times (15x) the total vacancies** in each district/unit category are called for Stage 1: PET/PST.

### 3.3 Language & Cultural Requirement
* Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script.
* Knowledge of Rajasthan culture, customs, and regional dialects.

* **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
  * *Source 1 (Official):* Notification Advt No. 04/2023, Section 5 ("पात्रता / शैक्षणिक योग्यता").
  * *Source 2 (Secondary):* Adda247 & Prepp Rajasthan Police Constable Eligibility Criteria articles.

### 5.1 Stages of Selection (Updated Order from 2023 Onwards)
Unlike earlier recruitment cycles where the written test was held first, the selection sequence was updated in 2023 to conduct Physical Tests prior to the written exam:

```
[ CET 12th Level Shortlisting (15x Rule) ]
                   │
                   ▼
[ STAGE 1: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST) ] ➔ Qualifying Nature
                   │
                   ▼
[ STAGE 2: Computer Based Test (CBT) / Written Exam ] ➔ 150 Marks
                   │
```

## Field 6: Written Examination Pattern

* **Mode of Examination:** **Computer Based Test (CBT)** or OMR-based offline test (2023/2024 exam conducted as CBT).
* **Question Type:** Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
* **Total Number of Questions:** **150 Questions**.
* **Total Marks:** **150 Marks** (1 Mark per question).
* **Duration:** **2 Hours (120 Minutes)**.
* **Negative Marking:** Deduction of **1/4th mark (0.25 marks)** for each wrong answer.
* **5th Option Rule (Rajasthan Exam Standard):** Unattempted questions must have the 5th option bubbled/selected to avoid penalty per RSMSSB/Rajasthan Police rules.

## Field 7: Detailed Syllabus Breakdown

### Part A: Reasoning, Logic & Computer Literacy (60 Questions | 60 Marks)

#### 1. Reasoning & Logical Ability
* **Verbal Reasoning:** Analogies, Classification, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense Test, Alphabet Test, Word Formation, Venn Diagrams, Syllogism, Statement and Assumptions, Statement and Arguments, Statement and Conclusions, Cause and Effect, Ranking and Time Sequence Test.
* **Non-Verbal & Analytical Reasoning:** Series Completion, Figure Matrix, Embedded Figures, Paper Cutting and Folding, Mirror and Water Images, Completion of Incomplete Patterns, Cube and Dice, Clock and Calendar, Data Sufficiency.

#### 2. Computer Literacy
* **Computer Fundamentals:** Basic Architecture of Computers, CPU, RAM, ROM, Input & Output Devices, Memory Storage Units (KB, MB, GB, TB), Computer Generations and Types.
* **Software & Operating Systems:** System Software vs Application Software, MS Windows Basics, File & Directory Management, Shortcuts and Command Utilities.
* **MS Office Suite:** MS Word (formatting, tables, shortcuts), MS Excel (spreadsheets, basic formulas, charts), MS PowerPoint (slides, animations).
* **Internet & Networking:** Internet Basics, World Wide Web (WWW), Web Browsers, Search Engines, E-mail protocol, IP Address, LAN/WAN/MAN, Wi-Fi, Cyber Security basics, Malware, Viruses, Trojan, Antivirus, Firewall, Cyber Crime & IT Act basics.

---

### Part B: General Knowledge, General Science, Current Affairs & Crime Against Women/Children Laws (45 Questions | 45 Marks)

#### 1. General Science
* **Physics:** Units & Measurement, Motion, Work, Energy, Power, Gravitation, Heat & Thermodynamics, Light & Optics, Sound, Electricity & Magnetism.
* **Chemistry:** States of Matter, Atomic Structure, Acids, Bases & Salts, Metals & Non-Metals, Carbon & its Compounds, Chemical Reactions, Chemistry in Daily Life.
* **Biology & Human Body:** Cell Structure & Functions, Human Physiology & Systems (Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Nervous, Excretory), Health, Nutrition & Vitamins, Diseases (Bacterial, Viral, Fungal, Deficiency) & Immunity, Genetics, Biodiversity & Environment.

#### 2. General Knowledge & Indian History/Polity/Geography/Economy
* **Indian History:** Ancient India (Indus Valley, Vedic Age, Buddhism/Jainism, Maurya & Gupta Empires), Medieval India (Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire, Bhakti & Sufi Movements), Modern India (1857 Revolt, Social Reform Movements, Indian National Movement, Freedom Struggle & Independence).
* **Indian Polity:** Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Fundamental Duties, Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP), President, Prime Minister, Parliament, Supreme Court, High Courts, Panchayati Raj, Constitutional Bodies.
* **Indian Geography:** Physical Features of India, Rivers & Lakes, Climate & Monsoons, Forests & Wildlife, Soil & Agriculture, Mineral & Energy Resources, Transport & Industry.
* **Indian Economy & Current Affairs:** Basic Economic Concepts, Union Budget, Inflation, National Income, Major Government Welfare Schemes, National & International Current Events, Sports, Awards & Honors, Important Books & Authors, Defense & Technology updates.

#### 3. Rights of Women and Children & Legal Provisions (Crime Against Women/Children)
* **POCSO Act 2012:** Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, key sections, reporting mechanisms, child welfare committees.
* **Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005:** Definition of domestic violence, protection orders, rights of victims.
* **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act 2006:** Legal age of marriage, penalties, prevention measures.
* **Dowry Prohibition Act 1961:** Definition of dowry, legal penalties, amendments.
* **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act 2013 (POSH Act):** Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), procedures.
* **IPC & Legal Provisions:** IPC Sections related to rape, molestation, kidnapping, acid attacks, indecent representation of women (Indecent Representation of Women Prohibition Act 1986).
* **Helplines & Emergency Services:** Women Helpline 1090/181, Childline 1098, Rajasthan Police initiatives (Chirali Scheme, Aawaz Campaign, Sakhi Centers).

### Part C: History, Geography, Art, Culture, Polity & Economy of Rajasthan (45 Questions | 45 Marks)

#### 1. Geography of Rajasthan
* **Physiographic Divisions:** Arid Western Desert (Thar), Aravalli Range Region, Eastern Plains, South-Eastern Hadoti Plateau.
* **Drainage System:** Inland Drainage, Bay of Bengal Rivers (Chambal, Banas, Kalisindh), Arabian Sea Rivers (Luni, Mahi, Sabarmati), Major Lakes and Dams.
* **Climate & Agriculture:** Agro-climatic zones, Rainfall distribution, Drought & Famine, Major Crops (Kharif, Rabi), Irrigation Projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project - IGNP, Bisalpur, Mahi Bajaj Sagar).
* **Natural Resources & Demographics:** Soils, Vegetation & Forests, Wildlife Sanctuaries & National Parks (Ranthambore, Keoladeo, Sariska, Mukundra), Minerals (Metallic & Non-Metallic), Livestock, Census 2011 Demographics of Rajasthan (Literacy, Sex Ratio, Density).

#### 2. History of Rajasthan
* **Major Dynasties:** Guhilas/Sisodias of Mewar, Rathores of Marwar/Bikaner, Kachhwahas of Amber/Jaipur, Chauhans of Shakambhari/Ajmer/Ranthambore.
* **Historical Milestones:** Resistance against foreign invasions (Maharana Kumbha, Maharana Sanga, Maharana Pratap, Rao Chandrasen), Mughal-Rajput relations.
* **Freedom Movement:** 1857 Revolution in Rajasthan, Peasant Movements (Bijolia, Begun), Tribal Movements (Bhagat Movement - Govind Guru, Eki Movement - Motilal Tejawat), Prajamandal Movements, Integration of Rajasthan (7 Stages of Unification from 18 March 1948 to 1 November 1956).

#### 3. Art, Culture, Fairs & Festivals of Rajasthan
* **Architecture:** Famous Forts (Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Mehrangarh, Amber, Junagarh, Jaisalmer), Palaces, Haveli Architecture, Stepwells (Bawris), Temples and Cenotaphs (Chhatris).
* **Folk Arts & Performing Arts:** Painting Styles (Mewar, Marwar, Kishangarh - Bani Thani, Bundi, Kota, Dhundhar), Folk Dances (Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Chari, Agni, Kachhi Ghodi, Gair), Folk Dramas (Khyal, Rammat, Swang, Tamasha), Musical Instruments.
* **Folk Deities & Saints:** Panch Pirs (Ramdevji, Pabuji, Gogaji, Tejaji, Harbhuji), Lok Devis (Karni Mata, Jeen Mata, Sheetla Mata), Saint Sects (Jambhoji - Bishnoi, Jasnathji, Dadu Dayal, Meera Bai).
* **Fairs, Festivals, Attire & Literature:** Major Fairs (Pushkar, Teja Dashmi, Desert Festival), Rajasthani Dialects (Marwari, Mewati, Dhundhari, Malvi, Wagdi), Rajasthani Literature, Traditional Ornaments and Handicrafts (Blue Pottery, Bandhani, Block Printing, Thewa Art).

#### 4. Polity & Economy of Rajasthan
* **State Administrative Framework:** Governor, Chief Minister, State Cabinet, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly, High Court of Rajasthan.
* **State Commissions:** RPSC, RSSB, State Human Rights Commission, State Election Commission, State Information Commission, Lokayukta.
* **Local Self-Government:** Panchayati Raj (Nagaur 1959 significance), Municipalities, District Administration.
* **Economy & Schemes:** Chief Minister's Flagship Schemes, Industrial Parks (RIICO), Renewable Energy (Solar/Wind Leadership), Tourism Policy and Circuits.
