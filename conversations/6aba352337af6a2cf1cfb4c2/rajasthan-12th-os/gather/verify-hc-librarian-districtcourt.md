## Detailed Fact Verification Breakdown

### FACT 1: Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant / LDC
- **Disputed Question:** Is the educational qualification for Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant / LDC 12th pass or graduate?
- **Detailed Findings:**
  - Under the *Rajasthan High Court Staff Service Rules* and the official joint recruitment notifications issued by the Registrar Examination, Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur (e.g., Advt. No. RHC/Exam Cell/JJA & Clerk Grade-II/2020 dated 19.03.2020 and Advt. No. RHC/Exam Cell/JJA & Clerk Grade-II/2022/1647 dated 05.08.2022), Section 5 ("Minimum Academic Qualification") explicitly mandates:
    > *"A candidate for direct recruitment to the service must be a Graduate of any University established by Law in India or its equivalent examination from any University recognized by the Government for the purpose and must have basic knowledge of Computer."*
  - Candidates often confuse Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant / JJA with RSMSSB LDC (State Departments), which requires Senior Secondary (12th Pass). For High Court Junior Assistant / JJA, **Graduation is mandatory**.
- **Exact Source URLs:**
  - Primary Official PDF: `https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/ph167542421967.pdf`
  - Official Recruitment Portal Notice: `https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/Notice17.pdf`
  - Official Portal: `https://hcraj.nic.in`
- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
- **One-Line Verdict:** Educational qualification for Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant / LDC is **Graduate (Bachelor's Degree) plus basic computer knowledge**, NOT 12th pass.

---

### FACT 2: RSMSSB / RSSB Librarian Grade-3
- **Disputed Question:** What is the exact educational qualification (12th + library science certificate? diploma? degree?) from the official RSSB notification?
- **Detailed Findings:**
  - As per official RSSB (Rajasthan Staff Selection Board) recruitment advertisements (e.g., Advt. No. 06/2022 and Advt. No. 18/2024 / 2025 recruitment for 548 posts), the eligibility clause specifies:
    1. **Senior Secondary (10+2 / 12th Pass)** from a recognized Board, **AND**
    2. Any one of the following qualifications in Library Science:
       - **Certificate in Library Science** (C.Lib / C.Lib.Sc) recognized by the Government, **OR**
       - **Diploma in Library & Information Science** (D.Lib / D.L.I.Sc), **OR**
       - **Bachelor's Degree in Library & Information Science** (B.Lib / B.L.I.Sc).
    3. Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan's culture.
- **Exact Source URLs:**
  - Official Portal: `https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in`
  - Official PDF Copy: `https://www.adda247.com/jobs/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2024/12/13150354/Rajasthan-Librarian-Notification-2024-1.pdf`
  - Official Notice Archive: `https://img.freejobalert.com/uploads/2024/12/Short-Notification-RSMSSB-Librarian-Grade-III-Posts.pdf`
- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
- **One-Line Verdict:** Educational qualification for RSMSSB Librarian Grade-3 is **12th Pass (Senior Secondary) + Certificate / Diploma / Bachelor's Degree in Library Science** + Hindi Devanagari & Rajasthan culture knowledge.

### FACT 3: Rajasthan District Court Junior Clerk / LDC (Lower Division Clerk)
- **Disputed Question:** Is Rajasthan District Court junior clerk / LDC (lower division clerk in district courts) a 12th-level or graduate qualification?
- **Detailed Findings:**
  - Recruitment for Clerk Grade-II (LDC) in District Courts across Rajasthan (both Non-TSP and TSP areas) is conducted centrally by the High Court of Rajasthan, Jodhpur (`hcraj.nic.in`) under the *Rajasthan District Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986*.
  - In the official combined recruitment advertisement (Advt. No. RHC/Exam Cell/2020 and 2022 for 2,756 posts), the posts of **Junior Judicial Assistant (Rajasthan High Court)**, **Junior Assistant (RSLSA & DLSAs)**, and **Clerk Grade-II (District Courts & RSJA)** are advertised together with a single, uniform qualification requirement:
    > *"Must be a Graduate of any University established by Law in India or equivalent examination from any University recognized by the Government for the purpose and must have basic knowledge of Computer."*
  - Therefore, District Court Clerk Grade-II / LDC in Rajasthan requires **Graduation**, unlike RSMSSB State Department LDCs which require 12th pass.
- **Exact Source URLs:**
  - Official Portal: `https://hcraj.nic.in`
  - Direct PDF Notice: `https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/notice167456225459.pdf`
  - Direct PDF Notification: `https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/ph167542421967.pdf`
- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
- **One-Line Verdict:** Educational qualification for Rajasthan District Court Junior Clerk / LDC (Clerk Grade-II) is **Graduate (Bachelor's Degree) + basic computer knowledge**, NOT 12th pass.

### BONUS 1: REET Level 1 Eligibility Basis
- **Disputed Question:** What is the eligibility basis for REET Level 1 (Primary Teacher, Classes 1 to 5)? Is 12th + D.El.Ed / JBT required?
- **Detailed Findings:**
  - Official Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (BSER / RBSE) guidelines mandate for REET Level 1:
    1. **Senior Secondary (12th Pass or equivalent) with at least 50% marks** (or 45% as per NCTE regulations for specific legacy categories), **AND**
    2. **Passed or appearing in the final year of 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / BSTC / D.Ed / JBT)**, **OR** 4-year B.El.Ed, **OR** 2-year Diploma in Education (Special Education).
  - Note: Following the Hon'ble Supreme Court judgment (2023), B.Ed candidates are strictly excluded from REET Level 1; D.El.Ed / BSTC alongside 12th is the sole valid path.
- **Exact Source URLs:**
  - Official Portal: `https://rajeduboard.rajasthan.gov.in`
  - Secondary Corroborated Source: `https://result91.com/article/reet-mains-notification-2025`
- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
- **One-Line Verdict:** REET Level 1 eligibility requires **12th Pass (minimum 50% marks) + 2-Year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / BSTC / JBT)**.

### BONUS 2: RSSB Stenographer / Personal Assistant Grade-II Qualification & Speed
- **Disputed Question:** What is the educational qualification and shorthand speed requirement for RSSB Stenographer / PA Grade-II?
- **Detailed Findings:**
  - As per RSSB Direct Recruitment Advertisement (Advt. No. 07/2024 for Stenographer & PA Grade-II):
    1. **Academic Qualification:** Senior Secondary (12th Pass / 10+2) from a recognized Board or equivalent.
    2. **Computer Qualification:** RS-CIT Certificate / O Level / COPA / Diploma in Computer Science or equivalent.
    3. **Phase-II Shorthand Skill Test Requirement:**
       - **Shorthand Dictation Speed:** **100 words per minute (wpm)** for **10 minutes** dictation (total 1,000 words).
       - **Transcription Time on Computer:** **50 minutes** for English Shorthand OR **70 minutes** for Hindi Shorthand.
- **Exact Source URLs:**
  - Official Portal: `https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in`
  - Secondary Corroborated Result Notice: `https://resultdekho.net/result/rsmssb-stenographer-typing-test-result-2026`
- **Evidence Level:** `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMED`
- **One-Line Verdict:** RSSB Stenographer / PA Grade-II requires **12th Pass + RS-CIT (or computer cert)** and a shorthand speed of **100 wpm dictation for 10 minutes** (50 min transcription for English / 70 min for Hindi).

## Conclusion & Strategic Recommendations for Exam OS Classification

1. **Category Inclusion for "12th-Level OS":**
   - **INCLUDE:** RSMSSB Librarian Grade-3, REET Level 1, and RSSB Stenographer / PA Grade-II. All three accept 12th pass as the base academic qualification (with relevant diplomas/certificates).
   - **EXCLUDE / CLASSIFY AS GRADUATE-LEVEL:** Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant / JJA and Rajasthan District Court Clerk Grade-II / LDC. Both require a **Graduate degree** and must NOT be listed under 12th-level exam prep modules.
