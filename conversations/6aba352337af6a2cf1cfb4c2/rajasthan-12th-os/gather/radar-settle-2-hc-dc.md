# Rajasthan High Court & District Court Recruitment Qualification Findings

- FACT: Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant (JA) recruitment minimum educational qualification
- VERDICT: SETTLED
- OFFICIAL VALUE: A candidate for direct recruitment: 1. must be a graduate of any University established by law in India or equivalent examination from any University recognized by the Government for the purpose and ; 2. must have basic knowledge of Computer.
- SOURCE: Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur Notification No. RHCJ/Exam Cell/Junior Assistant/2022/2748, Dated 05.08.2022 ("Recruitment to the posts of Junior Judicial Assistant for Rajasthan High Court, Junior Assistant for RSLSA & DLSAs and Clerk Grade-II for RSJA and District Courts, 2022"), Clause 7 "Minimum Academic Qualification" (Page 5). URL: https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/jja37.pdf
- CROSS-CHECK: https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/LDC71.pdf (Official Instructions for Online Application Form - Recruitment to the post of Junior Judicial Assistant / Junior Assistant / Clerk Grade-II 2020, Section 6.3 requiring Graduation), confirming complete agreement across multiple official High Court recruitment notifications that Graduation + basic knowledge of computer is required for Junior Assistant.
- CONFLICT HISTORY: Various unofficial coaching websites, job aggregators, and outdated study guides claimed the minimum qualification was 12th-pass (Senior Secondary) with an RSCIT diploma/typing certificate. This confusion stemmed from conflating Rajasthan High Court Junior Assistant posts with State Subordinate LDC posts (conducted by RSSB/RSMSSB, which permit 12th + RSCIT) and pre-amendment recruitment rules. Official Rajasthan High Court notifications explicitly confirm that Graduation is required.

- FACT: District Court junior clerk (Clerk Grade-II) minimum educational qualification
- VERDICT: SETTLED
- OFFICIAL VALUE: A candidate for direct recruitment: 1. must be a graduate of any University established by law in India or equivalent examination from any University recognized by the Government for the purpose and ; 2. must have basic knowledge of Computer.
- SOURCE: Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur Notification No. RHCJ/Exam Cell/Junior Assistant/2022/2748, Dated 05.08.2022 ("Recruitment to the posts of Junior Judicial Assistant for Rajasthan High Court, Junior Assistant for RSLSA & DLSAs and Clerk Grade-II for RSJA and District Courts, 2022" under Rajasthan District Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986 as amended), Clause 7 "Minimum Academic Qualification" (Page 5). URL: https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/hcraj_admin/uploadfile/recruitment/jja37.pdf
- CROSS-CHECK: https://hcraj.nic.in/hcraj/recruitment/advert_ldc18022017.pdf (Rajasthan High Court Notification No. RHC/Exam Cell/Sub.Court LDC/2017/177 Dated 18.02.2017 for Subordinate/District Court LDC recruitment), Clause 3 "Minimum Academic Qualification" (Page 5), which demonstrates that in 2017 the requirement was "Senior Secondary Examination... and RSCIT/Computer qualification". Subsequent amendments to the Rajasthan District Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986 upgraded the minimum educational qualification to Graduation prior to the 2020 and 2022 combined recruitment notifications.
- CONFLICT HISTORY: Under the original/earlier provisions of the Rajasthan District Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986, direct recruitment for District Court LDC / Junior Clerk required 12th Pass (Senior Secondary) + computer qualification (e.g. RSCIT), as published in the official 2017 District Court LDC advertisement. However, the rules were subsequently amended, elevating the minimum educational qualification to Graduation for all subsequent recruitments (2020, 2022). Coaching platforms and third-party portals that recycled outdated 2017 eligibility details erroneously continued to list 12th-pass as the qualification, creating the dispute.
