### C. Strategic Copywriting & Price Anchoring Formulas

1. **The "Offline vs. Online" Extreme Anchor (Utkarsh / Quality Education):**
   * *Formula:* Compare the ₹15,000 classroom coaching + ₹40,000 PG stay cost in Jaipur/Jodhpur against the ₹1,800 online batch fee.
   * *Copy Example:* *"जयपुर आने-रहने का 50,000 रुपये का खर्चा बचाएं! वही जोधपुर के टॉप टीचर्स अब आपके मोबाइल पर मात्र ₹1,800 में।"*
   * *Effectiveness:* Highly effective for rural parents who make the financial decision.

2. **The "Daily Pocket Money" Micro-Anchor (Physics Wallah / Testbook):**
   * *Formula:* Divide the yearly subscription cost by 365 days to show per-day cost.
   * *Copy Example:* *"₹349 per year = Less than 1 Rupee per day! Tea costs more than your entire year of test preparation."*
   * *Effectiveness:* Eliminates price resistance among 18-year-old students using pocket money.

3. **Faculty Promo Code Attribution (Adda247):**
   * *Formula:* Assign a unique discount code (e.g., `Y777`, `RAJ77`) to each YouTube teacher. Teacher screams the code every 10 minutes during live streams for a extra 77–78% discount.
   * *Effectiveness:* Creates competition among faculty to drive app conversions while tracking multi-channel attribution.

### B. Analysis of Promotional Mechanics

1. **"Double Validity" Strategy (Adda247 / Testbook):**
   * *How it works:* Pay for 12 months, get 12 months free (24 months total).
   * *Psychological Effect:* Aspirants preparing for Rajasthan exams know state exam recruitments often take 1.5 to 2 years due to legal delays or paper leaks. Double validity eliminates the fear of subscription expiry before exam day.

2. **Faculty Discount Codes (Adda247):**
   * *How it works:* Faculty members give 77-78% discount codes on YouTube.
