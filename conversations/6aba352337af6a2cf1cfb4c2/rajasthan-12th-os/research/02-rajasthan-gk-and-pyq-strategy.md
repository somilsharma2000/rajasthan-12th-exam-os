## 1. RAJASTHAN GK SYLLABUS MAP

Rajasthan-specific General Knowledge (GK) forms the heaviest common core (40%–60% of total exam weightage) across all 12th-level and senior-secondary Rajasthan state government examinations conducted by the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) and the Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC). Below is the comprehensive subject-by-subject syllabus map.

### 1.1 Rajasthan History (इतिहास)

* **Rajput Period & Major Dynasties (राजपूत काल एवं प्रमुख राजवंश):**
  * **Guhils & Sisodias of Mewar (मेवाड़ का गुहिल व सिसोदिया वंश):** Bappa Rawal, Rana Sanga (Battle of Khanwa), Maharana Pratap (Battle of Haldighati 1576, Chawand capital), Rana Kumbha (architectural patron, Vijay Stambha).
  * **Rathores of Marwar & Bikaner (मारवाड़ व बीकानेर का राठौड़ वंश):** Rao Jodha (founding of Jodhpur, Mehrangarh), Maldeo Rathore (Battle of Giri Sumel 1544), Jaswant Singh I, Durga Das Rathore (Rathore-Mughal 30-year war), Bika (Bikaner).
  * **Kachwahas of Dhundhar/Jaipur (ढूंढाड़/जयपुर का कछवाहा वंश):** Raja Man Singh I (Mughal alliance), Sawai Jai Singh II (founding of Jaipur 1727, Jantar Mantar observatories, Ashwamedha Yajna).
  * **Chauhans (चौहान वंश):** Prithviraj Chauhan III (Battles of Tarain 1191, 1192), Hammir Dev Chauhan of Ranthambore (1301 Saka), Kanhad Dev Chauhan of Jalore (1311 Saka).
* **Maratha Incursions & British Treaty Era (मराठा प्रभाव एवं ब्रिटिश संधियां):**
  * First Maratha entry in Bundi (1734), Hurda Conference (1734).
  * Subordinate Alliance treaties signed with the British East India Company (1817–1818) led by Charles Metcalfe (first: Karauli; last: Sirohi 1823).
* **1857 Revolution in Rajasthan (1857 की क्रांति):**
  * **6 Military Cantons (6 सैनिक छावनियां):** Nasirabad (first outbreak, May 28, 1857), Neemuch, Erinpura, Kota, Beawar (inactive), Kherwara (inactive).
  * **Major Centers & Leaders:** Erinpura rebellion ("Chalo Delhi, Maro Firangi"), Auwa siege (Thakur Kushal Singh Champawat, Battle of Bithoda & Chetawat/Chello), Kota rebellion (Jaidayal & Hardayal). Political Agents (P.A.s: Mason at Jodhpur, Eden at Jaipur, Burton at Kota, Morrison at Bharatpur).
* **Freedom Movement & Social Awakenings (स्वतंत्रता आंदोलन एवं प्रजामंडल):**
  * **Prajamandal Movement (प्रजामंडल आंदोलन):** Jaipur Prajamandal (1931 - Jamnalal Bajaj, Karpuri Chand Patni; reorganized 1936 by Hiralal Shastri), Mewar Prajamandal (1938 - Manikya Lal Verma), Marwar Prajamandal (1934 - Jainarayan Vyas), Bikaner Prajamandal (1936 - Magaram Vaidya), Kota Prajamandal (Nayanuram Sharma).
  * **Peasant Movements (किसान आंदोलन):** Bijolia Peasant Movement (1896–1941, longest non-violent movement led by Sadhu Sitaram Das, Vijay Singh Pathik, Manikya Lal Verma), Begun Peasant Movement (Rampura/Chetram), Alwar & Nimuchana Massacre (1925), Bird Peasant Movement.
  * **Tribal Movements (जनजातीय आंदोलन):** Bhil Movement / Bhagat Movement led by Govind Giri (Mangarh Dham Massacre Nov 17, 1913), Eki Movement led by Motilal Tejawat (Matrikundiya, Chittorgarh).
* **Integration of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का एकीकरण):**
  * Completed in 7 distinct stages from **March 17, 1948** to **November 1, 1956** under the leadership of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and VP Menon.
  * *Stage 1:* Matsya Union (March 17, 1948) – Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli.
  * *Stage 2:* Rajasthan Union / पूर्व राजस्थान (March 25, 1948) – 9 princely states + Chiefship of Kushalgarh.
  * *Stage 3:* United State of Rajasthan (April 18, 1948) – Rajasthan Union + Mewar (Udaipur).
  * *Stage 4:* Greater Rajasthan / बृहत् राजस्थान (March 30, 1948/1949) – Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer added (Observed as Rajasthan Diwas on March 30).
  * *Stage 5:* United State of Greater Rajasthan (May 15, 1949) – Matsya Union merged into Greater Rajasthan (Shankar Rao Deo Committee recommendation).
  * *Stage 6:* Rajasthan Union (January 26, 1950) – Sirohi (excluding Abu-Delwara) merged.
  * *Stage 7:* Reorganized Rajasthan (November 1, 1956) – Ajmer-Merwara, Abu-Delwara, and Sunel Tappa merged; Sironj sub-division given to MP.

### 1.2 Rajasthan Geography & Environment (भूगोल एवं पर्यावरण)

* **Physical Divisions (भौतिक प्रदेश):**
  1. **Western Sandy Plain / Thar Desert (थार मरुस्थल):** ~61.11% area, ~40% population. Sub-divided into Sandy Arid Plain (Marusthali) and Semi-Arid Transit Plain (Luni Basin, Shekhawati, Nagauri Upland, Ghaggar Plain). Sand dunes types: Barchan, Seif, Transverse, Longitudinal.
  2. **Aravalli Range (अरावली पर्वतमाला):** ~9% area, ~10% population. Oldest fold mountain system. Total length 692 km (550 km in Rajasthan from Khedbrahma to Khetri). Highest Peak: Guru Shikhar (1,722 m, Mount Abu), Ser (1,597 m), Dilwara (1,442 m), Jarga (1,431 m).
  3. **Eastern Plains (पूर्वी मैदान):** ~23% area, ~39% population. Highly fertile alluvial region formed by Banas, Chambal, and Mahi rivers. Sub-divided into Chambal Basin, Banas-Banganga Plain, Mahi Plain (Fifty-Six Villages / Chhappan Ka Maidan).
  4. **South-Eastern Hadoti Plateau (दक्षिण-पूर्वी हाड़ौती पठार):** ~6.89% area, ~11% population. Volcanic basaltic lava plateau. Vindhyan Scarpland & Deccan Lava Plateau.
* **Drainage & River Systems (अपवाह तंत्र एवं नदियां):**
  * **Arabian Sea Drainage (अराकान/अरब सागर अपवाह):** Luni (Jad/Nag Pahar, Ajmer; saline at Balotra), Mahi (cuts Tropic of Cancer twice; Suphala Kranti/Triveni Sangam at Beneshwar with Som & Jakham), Sabarmati, West Banas.
  * **Bay of Bengal Drainage (बंगाल की खाड़ी अपवाह):** Chambal (only perennial river, Kamdhenu, originates at Janapav Hills MP), Banas (Hope of the Forest / Varna-Asha, originates at Khamnor Hills), Banganga, Gambhiri.
  * **Inland Drainage (आंतरिक अपवाह):** Ghaggar (Dead River / Nali), Kantli, Sabi, Kakni/Masurdi, Rupangarh.
* **Lakes, Dams & Irrigation Projects (झीलें, बांध एवं सिंचाई परियोजनाएं):**
  * **Saltwater Lakes:** Sambhar (largest inland salt lake in India, source URL: [https://rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajasthan.gov.in)), Pachpadra, Didwana, Lunkaransar.
  * **Freshwater Lakes:** Pichola, Fateh Sagar, Jaisamand (Dhebar Lake - largest artificial freshwater lake in Raj), Ana Sagar, Pushkar, Nakki Lake (highest altitude lake).
  * **Major Dams & Canals:** Indira Gandhi Canal Project (IGNP - length, main canal, lift canals), Bisalpur Dam (Banas - drinking water for Jaipur/Ajmer), Rana Pratap Sagar Dam (Chambal - Rawatbhata), Mahi Bajaj Sagar Dam (Banswara), Meja Dam (Kothari river, Bhilwara).
* **Climate & Soil Types (जलवायु एवं मृदा):**
  * **Köppen's Climate Classification:** BWhw (Arid West), BShw (Semi-arid Steppe), Cwg (Sub-humid Eastern Plain), Aw (Humid South/Hadoti).
  * **Soils (USDA Classification):** Aridisols, Entisols, Inceptisols, Vertisols (Black cotton soil of Hadoti), Alfisols.
* **Wildlife, Protected Areas & Minerals (वन्यजीव एवं खनिज):**
  * **National Parks & Tiger Reserves:** Ranthambore NP (Sawai Madhopur), Keoladeo Ghana NP (Bharatpur - UNESCO World Heritage wetland), Sariska Tiger Reserve (Alwar), Mukundra Hills NP (Kota/Chittorgarh), Ramgarh Vishdhari Tiger Reserve (Bundi - 4th Tiger Reserve of Raj).
  * **Minerals:** Rajasthan holds virtual monopoly in Lead & Zinc (Zawar mines, Rampura Agucha), Wollastonite, Jasper, Fluorite, Gypsum (Jamsar), Rock Phosphate (Jhamarkotra), Copper (Khetri Singhana), Petroleum & Natural Gas (Barmer-Sanchore Basin - Mangala, Bhagyam, Aishwarya).

### 1.3 Polity & Administration of Rajasthan (राजव्यवस्था एवं प्रशासन)

> **TIME-SENSITIVE NOTICE (September 2026 Verification):** Executive officeholders listed below reflect current appointments as of late 2024–2026. Always verify latest official state gazette notifications before publishing dynamic mock questions.

* **State Governance Structure (राज्य शासन संरचना):**
  * **Governor (राज्यपाल):** Haribhau Kisanrao Bagade (*Sworn in July 31, 2024*; Source: [https://lokbhavan.rajasthan.gov.in](https://lokbhavan.rajasthan.gov.in)). Constitutional duties, Ordinance making power (Art 213), Raj Bhavan administration.
  * **Chief Minister (मुख्यमंत्री):** Bhajan Lal Sharma (*14th Chief Minister, Assumed office Dec 15, 2023*; Source: [https://rajasthan.gov.in](https://rajasthan.gov.in)). Cabinet Secretariat, Chief Secretary role.
  * **Legislature (विधानमंडल):** Unicameral State Legislative Assembly (विधानसभा) with 200 Seats (SC: 34, ST: 25 reserved).
  * **Judiciary (न्यायपालिका):** Rajasthan High Court established Aug 29, 1949. Principal Seat: Jodhpur; Permanent Bench: Jaipur (established 1977).
* **District Administration & Panchayati Raj (जिला प्रशासन एवं पंचायती राज):**
  * **Administrative Divisions:** 10 Administrative Divisions (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Ajmer, Bikaner, Kota, Udaipur, Bharatpur, Sikar, Pali, Banswara).
  * **Panchayati Raj System:** Historic launch at Nagaur on October 2, 1959 by PM Jawaharlal Nehru. Implementation of 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act via Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994. Three-tier structure: Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad.
* **Constitutional & Statutory Bodies (संवैधानिक एवं वैधानिक निकाय):**
  * **RPSC (राजस्थान लोक सेवा आयोग):** Headquartered in Ajmer (Est. 1949 under Art 315).
  * **RSSB / RSMSSB (राजस्थान कर्मचारी चयन बोर्ड):** Est. 2014, Durgapura, Jaipur. Conducts 12th level recruitment exams.
  * **State Election Commission (राज्य निर्वाचन आयोग):** Est. 1994 (Art 243K).
  * **State Human Rights Commission (SHRC):** Est. 1999/2000 (Protection of Human Rights Act 1993).
  * **State Information Commission (SIC):** Est. 2006 under RTI Act 2005.

### 1.4 Art, Culture, Traditions & Heritage (कला, संस्कृति एवं साहित्य)

* **Folk Deities (लोक देवता एवं लोक देवियां):**
  * **Panch Pirs (पांच पीर):** Pabuji (Deity of camels, Rathore lineage, Kolu), Ramdevji (Runicha/Ramdevra, Kamadiya sect, Babari Dooj), Gogaji (Dadrewa/Gogamedi, Snake deity), Harbhuji (Bungti), Mehaji Mangaliya (Bapini).
  * **Other Major Folk Deities:** Tejaji (Khadnal/Sursura, Snake deity, Tejaji Mela at Parbatsar), Devnarayanji (Asind, Gujjar deity, Neem leaf leaves worship), Mallinathji (Tilwara cattle fair).
  * **Folk Goddesses (लोक देवियां):** Karni Mata (Deshnoke Bikaner - Temple of Rats), Shila Devi (Amber Jaipur), Tanot Mata (Jaisalmer - Goddess of Soldiers), Jeen Mata (Rewasa Sikar), Banana Mata / Sakambhari Mata.
* **Folk Dances & Drama (लोक नृत्य एवं लोक नाट्य):**
  * **Folk Dances:** Ghoomar (State Dance of Raj), Kalbelia (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage List 2010), Agni Dance (Jasnathi Sampraday, Katariyasar), Kacchi Ghodi (Shekhawati), Terah Tali (Kamad tribe, Ramdevra fair), Chari Dance (Kishan Singh / Gujjar community), Gair Dance (Mewar/Barmer).
  * **Folk Drama (लोक नाट्य):** Khyal (Turra-Kalangi, Kuchamani, Jaipuri, Shekhawati), Rammat (Bikaner/Jaisalmer), Tamasha (Jaipur), Phad Painting/Recitation (Devnarayanji & Pabuji Phad by Bhopa-Bhopi).
* **Folk Fairs & Festivals (मेले एवं त्यौहार):**
  * **Major Fairs:** Pushkar Fair (Kartik Purnima, Ajmer - largest camel fair), Desert Festival (Jaisalmer), Beneshwar Fair (Magh Purnima, Banswara - Kumbh of Tribals), Karni Mata Fair (Deshnoke), Kapil Muni Fair (Kolayat Bikaner).
  * **Festivals:** Gangaur (Chaitra Shukla Tritiya), Teej (Hariyali Teej - Shravan Shukla Tritiya; Badi Teej / Kajali Teej - Bhadrapada Krishna Tritiya at Bundi).
* **Dialects, Literature & Painting Schools (बोली, साहित्य एवं चित्रकला):**
  * **Dialects (बोलियां):** Marwari (Western Raj), Mewati (Alwar/Bharatpur), Dhundhari (Jaipur/Dausa), Hadoti (Kota/Bundi/Jhalawar), Wagdi (Dungarpur/Banswara), Malvi.
  * **Painting Schools (चित्रकला शैली):** Mewar School (Chawand/Ragamala), Marwar School (Jodhpur/Bikaner - yellow color dominance, hunting scenes), Kishangarh Style (*Bani Thani* by Nihal Chand - "Indian Mona Lisa"), Dhundhar School (Jaipur/Alwar), Hadoti School (Bundi - natural scenes/paintings of birds/animals; Kota - hunting scenes by women).
* **Costumes, Turbans, Jewelry & Tribes (वस्त्र, पगड़ियां, आभूषण एवं जनजातियां):**
  * **Turbans (पगड़ियां/साफे):** Pachrang Safa (Jaipur), Motra Safa, Lahriya (Shravan season), Amar Shahi, Arsi Shahi.
  * **Ornaments (आभूषण):** Head/Forehead (Borla, Rakhdi, Shishphool, Tikda), Ear (Toti, Oganya, Jhumka, Surliya), Neck (Hansli, Kanthi, Timaniya, Tulsi, Madaliya), Arm/Hand (Gokhru, Hathphool, Chuda), Waist (Tagdi/Kandhora), Foot (Pajeb, Anwal, Bichhiya).
  * **Major Tribes (प्रमुख जनजातियां):** Mina (largest tribe, Jaipur/Dausa), Bhil (2nd largest, Mewar/Banswara, Gavari dance), Garasia (3rd largest, Abu/Sirohi, Walar dance - performed without musical instruments), Sahariya (only PVTG in Raj, Shahbad/Kishan Ganj in Baran), Damor (Dungarpur), Kathodi.

### 1.5 Economy of Rajasthan (राजस्थान की अर्थव्यवस्था)

* **Agriculture & Livestock (कृषि एवं पशुपालन):**
  * Major Producer in India for Bajra (Pearl Millet), Mustard (Raye/Sarson), Guar, Isabgol, Coriander, Cumin.
  * Livestock census & breeds: Gir & Tharparkar cattle, Marwari horses, Chokla sheep ("Indian Merino"), Jamnapari goats.
* **Industries & Infrastructure (उद्योग एवं बुनियादी ढांचा):**
  * RIICO (Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation - Est. 1980).
  * Key Industrial Hubs: Bhiwadi (Alwar - Hub for Japanese Zone), Bhilwara ("Textile City" / "Manchester of Rajasthan"), Chittorgarh/Lakheri (Cement industries).
* **Key Government Welfare Schemes (प्रमुख राज्य प्रायोजित योजनाएं):**
  * **Shri Annapurna Rasoi Yojana** (formerly Indira Rasoi - subsidised meals at ₹8).
  * **Ayushman Bharat Chief Minister Mukhya Mantri Ayushman Swasthya Bima Yojana** (Universal health insurance coverage).
  * **Chief Minister Free Medicines Scheme (मुख्यमंत्री नि:शुल्क दवा योजना)** (Est. Oct 2, 2011).
  * **Mukhya Mantri Anuprati Coaching Scheme** (Free coaching for competitive exams for underprivileged students).
  * **Kalibai Bhil & Devnarayan Scooty Schemes** for meritorious girl students.

### 2.2 Shared Section Syllabus Details

1. **General Intelligence & Reasoning (मानसिक योग्यता एवं तर्कशक्ति):**
   * Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense Test, Syllogism (न्याय निगमन), Venn Diagrams, Series Completion (Number & Alphabet), Non-Verbal Reasoning (Cube, Dice, Mirror/Water Images, Counting Figures).
2. **10th-Level Mathematics (गणित):**
   * Arithmetic: Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion, Time & Work, Speed, Time & Distance, Average.
   * Mensuration & Geometry: Area and Perimeter of 2D figures (Circle, Triangle, Quadrilateral), Surface Area & Volume of 3D solids (Sphere, Cone, Cylinder, Cube, Cuboid).
   * Algebra & Statistics: Linear Equations, Polynomials, Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation.
3. **General Science (सामान्य विज्ञान - 10th Class Standard):**
   * Physical Science: Reflection & Refraction of Light, Electricity & Ohm's Law, Work, Energy & Power, Metals & Non-metals, Chemical Reactions, Carbon and its Compounds.
   * Biological Science: Human Body Systems (Digestive, Circulatory, Excretory, Nervous), Nutrition & Vitamins, Diseases & Human Health, Plant Genetics (Mendel's laws), Ecosystem & Environment.
4. **Computer Basics (कंप्यूटर ज्ञान):**
   * Fundamentals: Architecture of Computer, CPU, Input/Output Devices, Memory (RAM, ROM, Cache, Secondary Storage).
   * Software & OS: Operating System concepts (Windows, Linux basics).
   * Microsoft Office: MS Word (formatting, shortcuts), MS Excel (formulas, cell referencing, charts), MS PowerPoint (slide transition, master view).
   * Networking & Internet: IP Address, WWW, Browsers, Email Protocols (SMTP, POP3, IMAP), Cybersecurity & Malware (Viruses, Trojan, Phishing).
5. **General Hindi Language & Grammar (सामान्य हिंदी):**
   * संधि एवं संधि विच्छेद, समास (सामासिक पदों की रचना एवं विग्रह), उपसर्ग एवं प्रत्यय.
   * पर्यायवाची शब्द, विलोम शब्द, अनेकार्थक शब्द, शब्द-युग्म.
   * वाक्य शुद्धि, शब्द शुद्धि, मुहावरे एवं लोकोक्तियां, पारिभाषिक शब्दावली (Administrative terms).
6. **General English Language (सामान्य अंग्रेजी):**
   * Tenses / Sequence of Tenses, Voice (Active and Passive), Narration (Direct and Indirect), Articles & Prepositions.
   * Correction of Sentences, Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Substitution, Comprehension Passage, Glossary of Official Terms.

## 3. PYQ SOURCING & PROVENANCE STRATEGY

### 3.1 Official Government Sourcing Portals

To maintain 100% academic integrity and exam fidelity, all Previous Year Questions (PYQs) must be harvested directly from primary state recruitment portals:

1. **RSSB / RSMSSB Official Portal:**
   * **Domain URLs:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in) (Primary) and [https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in) (Legacy / Redirect).
   * **Target Section:** Candidate Corner -> Question Papers & Answer Keys.
   * **Data Artifacts Harvested:**
     * **Master Question Booklet (MQB):** Unaltered exam paper PDF as administered in the examination center.
     * **Provisional Answer Key:** Released immediately post-exam for candidate objections.
     * **Final Revised Answer Key:** Published post-objection review. *Crucial:* Identifies deleted/cancelled questions (Marked as `'DELETE'` or `'*'` by RSSB).
2. **RPSC Official Portal:**
   * **Domain URL:** [https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in)
   * **Target Section:** Candidate Information -> Question Papers / Answer Keys.

## 5. TOP 15 HIGHEST-YIELD RAJASTHAN GK SUBTOPICS

Based on historical PYQ frequency analysis across RSSB 12th-level papers (CET 12th, LDC, Patwari, Police Constable, VDO), these 15 subtopics consistently yield the highest number of direct questions:

1. **Integration of Rajasthan (राजस्थान का एकीकरण - 7 Stages):**
   * *Justification:* Yields 1–3 direct questions per paper on stage dates, prime ministers, inaugurators, and princely state combinations.
2. **1857 Revolution in Rajasthan (1857 की क्रांति):**
   * *Justification:* High-yield focus on the 6 military cantons, British Political Agents (Burton, Mason, Eden), and Auwa/Thakur Kushal Singh.
3. **Prajamandal Movements & Key Leaders (प्रजामंडल आंदोलन):**
   * *Justification:* Tested in almost every exam on founding years, founders (Jamnalal Bajaj, Manikya Lal Verma, Jainarayan Vyas), and regional freedom struggle events.
4. **Folk Deities - Panch Pirs & Lok Deviya (लोक देवता एवं लोक देवियां):**
   * *Justification:* Guaranteed 2–3 questions on Pabuji, Ramdevji, Tejaji, Gogaji, Karni Mata, main temples, and fair dates.
5. **Physical Divisions - Aravalli Range & Thar Topography (भौतिक प्रदेश):**
   * *Justification:* Focus on peak heights (Guru Shikhar, Ser, Dilwara), pass names (Nal/Ghat), and desert dune classification (Barchan, Seif).
6. **River Systems & Major Dams (अपवाह तंत्र एवं प्रमुख बांध):**
   * *Justification:* Heavy PYQ concentration on Luni, Chambal, Banas, Mahi Triveni Sangams, and Bisalpur / Rana Pratap Sagar dams.
7. **Major Rajput Dynasties & Key Battles (प्रमुख राजवंश एवं युद्ध):**
   * *Justification:* Questions centered on Haldighati (1576), Giri Sumel (1544), Tarain (1191/1192), Rana Sanga, Maharana Pratap, Sawai Jai Singh.
8. **UNESCO World Heritage & Hill Forts of Rajasthan (राजस्थान के दुर्ग एवं किले):**
   * *Justification:* Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore, Amber, Gagron, and Jaisalmer (The 6 Hill Forts of Rajasthan) and their architectural features.
9. **Folk Fairs & Calendar Dates (प्रमुख मेले एवं त्यौहार):**
   * *Justification:* Frequent matching questions between fairs (Pushkar, Beneshwar, Kapil Muni) and Hindu lunar calendar months (Kartik Purnima, Magh Purnima).
10. **Rajasthani Folk Dances & UNESCO Recognition (लोक नृत्य):**
    * *Justification:* Kalbelia (UNESCO), Ghoomar, Agni, Kacchi Ghodi, Terah Tali, and associated tribal groups.
11. **Rajasthani Painting Schools & Bani Thani (चित्रकला शैली):**
    * *Justification:* High-frequency questions on Kishangarh style (*Bani Thani*), Mewar, Bundi (nature/birds), and Kota (hunting) styles.
12. **Tribes of Rajasthan & Cultural Traditions (प्रमुख जनजातियां):**
    * *Justification:* Demographics, geographical distribution, and customs of Mina, Bhil, Garasia, Sahariya, and Kathodi tribes.
13. **Panchayati Raj & Nagaur Historic Origin (पंचायती राज प्रणाली):**
    * *Justification:* Articles 243-243O, Nagaur Oct 2 1959 launch, Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act 1994 provisions.
14. **Wildlife Sanctuaries & Tiger Reserves (अभयारण्य एवं राष्ट्रीय उद्यान):**
    * *Justification:* Ranthambore, Keoladeo Wetland, Sariska, Mukundra, Ramgarh Vishdhari, and regional sanctuaries.
15. **Flagship State Schemes & Budget Highlights (प्रमुख राज्य योजनाएं):**
    * *Justification:* Direct questions on Shri Annapurna Rasoi, Chiranjeevi/Ayushman Swasthya Bima, Anuprati Coaching, and recent budget figures.
