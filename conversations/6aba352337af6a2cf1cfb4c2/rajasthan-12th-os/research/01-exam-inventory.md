## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture of 12th-Pass Exams in Rajasthan

In Rajasthan state government recruitments, 12th-pass recruitment is split into two primary framework categories:
1. **CET 12th Level (Senior Secondary CET) Governed Posts:** A common gateway screening test conducted by the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB). Passing CET 12th Level is a mandatory prerequisite to appear for mains/departmental recruitment for 7 major cadre posts (LDC, Police Constable, Forester, Hostel Superintendent, Jamadar Grade-II, Jail Prahari, Assistant Jailor).
2. **Non-CET Independent Direct Recruitment Exams:** Specialized technical or qualifying examinations conducted independently by RSMSSB or RBSE without requiring CET screening (e.g., Lab Assistant, Agriculture Supervisor, REET Level 1, Stenographer / PA).

### Profile 01: RSMSSB Common Eligibility Test (CET) 12th Level (Senior Secondary)
* **Recruiting Body:** Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB)
* **Official Website:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Source URL:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/examscheme](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/examscheme)
* **Eligibility:** 
  * Educational: 12th Pass (10+2 Senior Secondary) from Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (RBSE), CBSE, or any recognized board.
  * Age Limit: 18 to 40 years (as of January 1 of the recruitment year; relaxations apply for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Female candidates).
* **Role & Cadre Scope:** Mandatory screening gateway for 7 cadres:
  1. Clerk Grade-II / Junior Assistant (LDC)
  2. Rajasthan Police Constable
  3. Forester (Vanpal)
  4. Hostel Superintendent (Chhatravas Adhyaksh - Social Justice & Minority Affairs)
  5. Jamadar Grade-II (Excise / Abkari Dept)
  6. Jail Prahari (Warder) / Assistant Jailor
* **Stages:**
  1. Stage 1: Written Examination (Objective MCQ Screening)
  2. Stage 2: Shortlisting (15 times total advertised vacancies per post cadre) for Tier-2 Mains / Skill / Physical tests. Score validity: 3 years (revised per Rajasthan Govt 2024 guidelines).
* **Written Paper Pattern:**
  * **Format:** Single Paper (Objective Type)
  * **Number of Questions:** 150 MCQs
  * **Total Marks:** 300 Marks (2 marks per question)
  * **Duration:** 3 Hours (180 minutes)
  * **Negative Marking:** **NO negative marking** in CET 2022 and 2024 screening notifications. *(Note: While RSSB uses 1/3 negative marking in Mains exams, the CET screening paper has no negative marking).*
* **Core Syllabus Outline & Weightage:**
  * *Rajasthan History, Art, Culture, Heritage, Literature, Tradition:* ~30-35 Qs (~60-70 Marks)
  * *Rajasthan Geography, Economy & Administrative System:* ~30 Qs (~60 Marks)
  * *Everyday Science, Technology & Environment:* ~20-25 Qs (~40-50 Marks)
  * *General Mental Ability, Reasoning & Basic Mathematics (up to 10th level):* ~20-25 Qs (~40-50 Marks)
  * *General Hindi:* ~15 Qs (~30 Marks)
  * *General English:* ~15 Qs (~30 Marks)
  * *Basic Computer Knowledge:* ~10 Qs (~20 Marks)
  * **Weightage Insight:** Rajasthan GK accounts for nearly **45-50%** of the entire paper, while Hindi + English language sections account for **20%**.
* **Typical Posts & Pay Level:** Gateway to Level L-5 to L-8 posts (Pay Matrix GP 2400 to GP 2800).
* **Notification Frequency & Last Recruitment:** Annual / Biennial. Last conducted: October 2024.
* **PYQ Availability:** Official Master Question Papers and Answer Keys available on [rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in) under "Candidate Corner -> Question Papers / Answer Keys".

### Profile 02: RSMSSB LDC / Junior Assistant (Clerk Grade-II & Kanishth Sahayak)
* **Recruiting Body:** Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB / RSSB)
* **Official Website:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Source URL:** [https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/recruitmentadvertisement](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in/recruitmentadvertisement)
* **Eligibility:**
  * Educational: 12th Pass (10+2 / Senior Secondary) + RSCIT Diploma / O-Level / COPA / Diploma or Degree in CS/IT.
  * Prerequisite: Must have qualified **CET 12th Level**.
  * Age Limit: 18 to 40 years.
  * *Verification Note on 2024 LDC Standard:* The 2024 recruitment (4,197 vacancies) requires 12th pass (Senior Secondary). The question paper difficulty for Paper 1 (Science/Maths/GK) is 10th (Secondary) level, whereas Paper 2 (Hindi/English) is 12th (Senior Secondary) standard.
* **Stages:**
  1. Stage 1: Shortlisting via CET 12th Level Score (15x candidates)
  2. Stage 2: Phase-I Written Examination (2 Papers on the same day)
  3. Stage 3: Phase-II Computer Typing Speed & Efficiency Test (Hindi & English) for 1:3 shortlisted candidates
  4. Stage 4: Document Verification (DV) & Medical
* **Written Paper Pattern (Phase-I):**
  * **Paper 1 (General Knowledge, Everyday Science, Mathematics):**
    * 150 Questions | 100 Marks | Duration: 3 Hours
    * Breakdown: Rajasthan GK (50 Qs), Everyday Science (50 Qs), Mathematics (50 Qs)
  * **Paper 2 (General Hindi & General English):**
    * 150 Questions | 100 Marks | Duration: 3 Hours
    * Breakdown: General Hindi (75 Qs / 50 Marks), General English (75 Qs / 50 Marks)
  * **Total Phase-I:** 300 Questions | 200 Marks | 6 Hours total duration.
  * **Negative Marking:** **1/3rd mark (0.33)** deducted for each wrong answer. Minimum 40% qualifying marks required in each individual paper.
  * **Phase-II Typing Test (100 Marks Total):**
    * Hindi Typing Speed Test (10 mins, 25 marks) + Efficiency Test (10 mins, 25 marks) = 50 Marks
    * English Typing Speed Test (10 mins, 25 marks) + Efficiency Test (10 mins, 25 marks) = 50 Marks
* **Core Syllabus Outline:**
  * *Paper 1:* Rajasthan History, Geography, Culture, Economy (33%); Everyday Science - Physics, Chem, Biology, Environmental Studies (33%); Mathematics - Vedic Maths, Factorization, Equations, Ratio, Profit/Loss, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics (33%).
  * *Paper 2:* General Hindi (Sandhi, Samas, Upsarg, Pratyay, Synonyms, Antonyms, Official Terminology - 50%) & General English (Tenses, Passive/Active Voice, Direct/Indirect, Articles, Prepositions, Translation, Vocabulary - 50%).
* **Typical Posts & Pay Level:** Clerk Grade-II (Secretariat, RPSC) and Junior Assistant (State Subordinate Depts). Pay Matrix Level **L-5** (Grade Pay 2400 / Basic initial probation pay ₹14,600, post-probation ₹20,800 + allowances).
* **Notification Frequency & Last Recruitment:** Periodical (every 3-5 years). Last recruitment: **2024 (4,197 posts)**, exam held August 2024. Previous major batch: 2018 (11,000+ posts).
* **PYQ Availability:** Official Master Question Papers and Answer Keys for 2018 and August 2024 exams on [rssb.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in).

### Profile 03: Rajasthan Police Constable
* **Recruiting Body:** Rajasthan Police Department (PHQ Jaipur) / RSMSSB
* **Official Website:** [https://police.rajasthan.gov.in](https://police.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Source URL:** [https://recruitment2.rajasthan.gov.in](https://recruitment2.rajasthan.gov.in)
* **Eligibility:**
  * Educational: 12th Pass (Senior Secondary) for District Police Constable. (Physics and Mathematics compulsory in 12th for Police Telecommunication branch).
  * Prerequisite: CET 12th Level score card.
  * Age Limit: 18 to 23 years for General Constable (up to 26 years for Drivers), with 5 years relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Women.
* **Stages:**
  1. Stage 1: Shortlisting via CET 12th Level Score (15 times total vacancies)
  2. Stage 2: Physical Efficiency Test (PET - 5km run) & Physical Measurement Test (PMT) - Qualifying
  3. Stage 3: Computer-Based Test (CBT) Written Exam
  4. Stage 4: Proficiency Test (30 Marks for Drivers, Band, Mounted)
  5. Stage 5: Document Verification & Medical Examination
* **Written Paper Pattern (CBT):**
  * **Total Questions:** 150 MCQs
  * **Total Marks:** 150 Marks (1 mark per question)
  * **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 minutes)
  * **Sectional Breakdown:**
    * **Part A:** Reasoning Ability, Decision Making & Computer Fundamentals -> 60 Qs / 60 Marks (40% weightage)
    * **Part B:** General Knowledge, General Science, Social Science, Current Affairs, Crime against Women & Children (Laws, POCSO, Safety Rights) -> 45 Qs / 45 Marks (30% weightage)
    * **Part C:** Rajasthan History, Art, Culture, Geography, Economy, Polity -> 45 Qs / 45 Marks (30% weightage)
  * **Negative Marking:** **1/4th mark (0.25 = 25%)** deducted for each wrong response. Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for Gen/OBC/EWS, 36% for SC/ST.
* **Core Syllabus Outline:**
  * *Part A:* Reasoning logic, coding-decoding, Venn diagrams, MS Office, Hardware/Software, OS, Internet basics.
  * *Part B:* Indian History, Geography, Indian Constitution, General Science basics, National/State Current Affairs, Laws & acts protecting women and children.
  * *Part C:* Rajasthan Geography, History, Culture, Forts, Fairs, Festivals, State Government Schemes.
* **Typical Posts & Pay Level:** Constable (General), Constable (Driver), Constable (Telecommunication), Constable (Band). Pay Matrix Level **L-5** (Grade Pay 2400 / Fixed stipend ₹14,600 during 2-year probation).
* **Notification Frequency & Last Recruitment:** Conducted almost every 1-2 years. Last recruitment: **2023-2024 (3,578 posts)**, CBT held June 2024.
* **PYQ Availability:** Official Answer Keys and Question Papers available on [police.rajasthan.gov.in](https://police.rajasthan.gov.in).

* **Written Paper Pattern:**
  * **Science Stream (School Education, College Education, Forensic Science Lab):**
    * **Paper 1:** Rajasthan GK (History, Culture, Geography, Economy), World & India GK, Educational Psychology -> 100 Qs | 200 Marks | 2 Hours
    * **Paper 2:** General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology - Senior Secondary Standard) -> 100 Qs | 200 Marks | 2 Hours

    * Part A: Rajasthan GK -> 100 Marks (approx 50 Qs)
    * Part B: Geography Subject -> 200 Marks (approx 100 Qs)
    * Total: 300 Marks | 3 Hours.
  * **Home Science Stream (College Education):**
    * Part A: Rajasthan GK -> 100 Marks
    * Part B: Home Science Subject -> 200 Marks
    * Total: 300 Marks | 3 Hours.

  * *Geography/Home Science Stream:* Standard 11th & 12th curriculum of RBSE / NCERT.
* **Typical Posts & Pay Level:** Lab Assistant in Secondary Education, College Education, Agriculture Department, and Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL). Pay Matrix Level **L-8** (Grade Pay 2800) / Level L-5.
* **Notification Frequency & Last Recruitment:** Held every 3-4 years. Last recruitment: **2022 (1,019 posts)**.

## 3. Explicitly Excluded Exams (Graduate-Only & Non-12th Direct Entry)

For product clarity and to avoid misleading app users, the following popular Rajasthan examinations are **EXCLUDED** from the direct 12th-pass inventory:

1. **RSMSSB Patwari:** Requires Graduation + Computer Certificate (RSCIT / O-Level) + CET Graduate Level. *(Formerly 12th-pass prior to 2019 rules revision; now strictly Graduate-only).*
