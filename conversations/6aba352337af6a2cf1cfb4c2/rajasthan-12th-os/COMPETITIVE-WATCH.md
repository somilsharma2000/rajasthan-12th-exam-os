## 2. Top 5 Exploitable Gaps & Opportunities

### 1. The "Fake PYQ & Bad Translation" Epidemic
* **Competitor Vulnerability:** Testbook and Adda247 frequently re-label SSC/Railway questions as "Rajasthan CET PYQs" or use machine-translated Hindi that distorts local Rajasthan terms (e.g., misinterpreting historic places, dynasties, or district names). Utkarsh has volume but fails to cite exact RSMSSB shift/paper numbers and updated answer keys.
* **Our Exploitation Strategy:** Market our **4-Pass Provenance Audit** heavily. Show exact provenance metadata on every question: *Exam Name, Board (RSMSSB/RPSC), Date, Shift, Question #, Official Master Answer Key ID, and Revision History*. This creates instant trust among serious aspirants burnt by wrong answer keys.

### 2. Video Fatigue vs. High-Velocity Practice
* **Competitor Vulnerability:** Utkarsh, PW, and Dhurina push 300–500 hours of video per course. Students spend 6 hours daily watching videos and 0 hours practicing actual questions under exam timing.
* **Our Exploitation Strategy:** Position our OS as the **"Zero-BS Practice & Revision Engine"**. Emphasize: *"Done with classes? Now build your exam speed."* A ₹99 subscription gives unlimited, distraction-free testing, micro-concept summaries, and active recall without wasting mobile data on video buffering.

### 3. Rural Rajasthan Network & Device Constraints
* **Competitor Vulnerability:** Coaching apps (Utkarsh, PW, Adda) range from 80MB to 150MB, load slowly, consume heavy RAM, and require strong 4G/5G connections. Rural students in districts like Barmer, Jaisalmer, or Banswara suffer frequent app crashes and buffering.
* **Our Exploitation Strategy:** Our **Offline PWA** loads instantly (<2MB initial bundle), functions 100% offline once synced, and consumes near-zero data. Position as: *"Study anywhere in Rajasthan—even without internet in your village or hostel."*

### 4. Unmonitored Negative Marking & Blind Guesswork
* **Competitor Vulnerability:** Competitor test series give a basic total score and accuracy percentage. They do not analyze *why* a student lost marks in RSMSSB negative marking (1/3rd penalty), nor do they track recurring mistake patterns across sub-topics.
* **Our Exploitation Strategy:** Leverage **Exam DNA & Automated Error Book**. Show students exactly which sub-topics cause 80% of their negative marks and automatically log every wrong attempt into a personalized revision queue.

### 5. High Batch Prices & Aggressive Sales Spam
* **Competitor Vulnerability:** Competitors charge ₹1,000–₹3,500 per batch with 1-year expiration, followed by constant upsell calls, pop-up ads, and push notification spam.
* **Our Exploitation Strategy:** Disrupt with **₹99 Transparent Subscription**. Zero ads, zero spam calls, honest UI, and full access to all 12th-level exams under one roof.
